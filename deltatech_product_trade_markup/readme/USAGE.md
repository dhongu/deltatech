1. Navigate to **Sales > Products > Products**.
2. Open a product and go to the **Sales** or **Inventory** tab.
3. Set the **Trade Markup Percent** (e.g., 20%).
4. The system will automatically calculate the **List Price** based on
   the product's **Cost**.
5. If the cost is updated, the selling price will be re-evaluated to
   maintain the 20% markup.
