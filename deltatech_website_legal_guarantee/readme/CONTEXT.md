The harmonised notice is an obligation of the seller at store level, not per product. The
Commission's guidelines recommend, for online shops, showing it as a general reminder on the
website, with the full and legible notice, plus a link to the "Your Europe" portal. The GARAN label
(the commercial guarantee of durability offered by the producer) is a separate, per-product
obligation and is not covered by this module.

The guidelines also recommend including the notice in the order confirmation e-mail; the e-mail
templates are `noupdate` and are adapted per customer.

The official files (PDF, PNG, JPG, SVG, in all EU languages):
https://commission.europa.eu/publications/high-resolution-vector-files-eu-notice-and-label-product-guarantees_en
