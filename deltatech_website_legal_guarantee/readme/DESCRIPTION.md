Displays on the website the harmonised notice of the European Union on the legal guarantee of
conformity, mandatory for sellers of goods to consumers from 27 September 2026:

- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960, Annex I (design and content of the notice);
- Directive (EU) 2024/825, which introduces Article 22a in Directive 2011/83/EU;
- Directive (EU) 2019/771 on contracts for the sale of goods, the source of the summary of consumer
  rights shown on the page.

The module adds:

- the public page `/garantie`, with the full official notice, the link to the "Your Europe" portal
  (the same destination as the QR code), the notice as PDF, the consumer rights in short, how to
  report a problem and the seller details;
- the link "Legal guarantee" in the footer, in the copyright row;
- on the product page and at the payment step, the link "Your legal guarantee rights", which opens
  the notice on the first click, as recommended by the Commission's practical guidelines (DG JUST,
  April 2026).

The notice is the official file published by the European Commission, in colour and unmodified, in
the language of the page, for all 24 official EU languages (English for any other language). The
page and the links are translated into the same languages (AI-assisted translation).

The summary of rights follows the Directive; where national law adds rules, a country block is shown
for the country of the website company. The module ships the block for Romania (GEO no. 140/2021,
ANPC); other countries are added with a template `national_<country code>`.

In *Website → Configuration → Settings*, section *EU legal guarantee notice*, you choose, for each
website, where the link to the notice appears: footer, product page, payment step. All are ticked by
default. The page uses the phone, e-mail, tax ID and address of the website company; extra content
(for example your own service terms) is added from the website editor, in the areas above and below
the page content.
