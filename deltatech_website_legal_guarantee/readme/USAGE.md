Once installed, the notice is available on the website without further setup.

1. Open **Website → Configuration → Settings**, section **EU legal guarantee notice**, and tick
   where the link to the notice should appear for each website: **footer**, **product page** and
   **checkout**. All three are ticked by default.

   ![Website settings, EU legal guarantee notice section: three ticked options, link in footer, legal guarantee on product page and legal guarantee at checkout](static/description/en/01_setari_website.png)

2. Check the public page `/garantie`. It shows the full official notice (a click on the image opens
   the PDF), the link to the "Your Europe" portal, a short summary of the consumer rights, how to
   report a problem and the seller details taken from the website company (name, tax ID, address,
   phone, e-mail). The notice is shown in the language of the page for all 24 official EU languages, and in
   English for any other language. The summary of rights follows Directive (EU) 2019/771; for a
   company in Romania a block with the national rules (GEO no. 140/2021, ANPC) is added.

   ![The public legal guarantee page: the official EU notice on the left, consumer rights, national rules, how to report a problem and the seller details on the right](static/description/en/02_pagina_garantie.png)

3. With the footer option ticked, the link **Legal guarantee** appears in the copyright row on
   every page.

   ![Website footer: the Legal guarantee link, highlighted, in the copyright row](static/description/en/03_footer_link.png)

4. With the product page option ticked, the link **Your legal guarantee rights** appears on the
   product page, under the product details. The same link appears at checkout, under the billing
   address, when the checkout option is ticked.

   ![Product page: the Your legal guarantee rights link, highlighted, under the Add to cart button](static/description/en/04_pagina_produs_link.png)

5. A click on the link opens the notice in a window, so the customer keeps the product page or the
   cart.

   ![The window opened by the link, showing the official EU notice in English](static/description/en/05_fereastra_notificare.png)

Extra content, such as your own service terms, can be added from the website editor, in the areas
above and below the page content.
