Free Disassembly - Bill of Materials
====================================

Overview
--------

Bridge between **Free Disassembly** and **Manufacturing**, installed
automatically when both are present. The resulting products of a
disassembly report can be filled in from a bill of materials, instead of
being entered one by one; the report stays free and does not need a
manufacturing order.

Features
--------

- **Bill of Materials** field on the disassembly report, proposed from
  the disassembled product (normal bills of materials, matching the
  product or its template), and changeable.
- **Load from BoM** button (draft only): replaces the product lines by
  the components of the bill of materials, for the disassembled quantity
  converted to the unit of the bill of materials. Kits are exploded down
  to their components, services are skipped and the loss lines are kept.
- The loaded lines can then be changed, removed or completed freely; the
  cost allocation and the accounting are those of the disassembly
  report.

Usage
=====

1. Create a disassembly report and select the disassembled product: its
   bill of materials is proposed in **Bill of Materials**.
2. Set the quantity, then click **Load from BoM**. The product lines are
   replaced by the components, in the proportion of the bill of
   materials.
3. Adjust the lines if needed (quantities, lots, loss lines), choose the
   allocation method and **Validate**.
