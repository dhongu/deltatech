## Prerequisites

The module needs no setting. Install it; it also installs `website_sale` and
`deltatech_data_sheet`.

## Attaching the data sheets to a product

Open the product from **Sales > Products > Products** and go to the **Sales** tab. In the
**Data Sheet** group, at the bottom of the tab, fill in **Data Sheet** and **Safety Data
Sheet** with the PDF of each sheet, then save. The fields accept only public PDF
attachments, so a file uploaded from the field can be downloaded by website visitors.

Publish the product with the **Is Published** toggle of the **eCommerce Shop** group.

![Product form, Sales tab: the Data Sheet group with the highlighted Data Sheet and Safety Data Sheet fields, each filled with a PDF](static/description/en/01_formular_produs_fise.png)

## Viewing the data sheets on the website

Open the product page in the shop. The **Documents** section, below **Add to cart**, lists a
**Show Data Sheet** and a **Show Safety Data Sheet** link, one for each sheet set on the
product. A click downloads the PDF, also for visitors who are not signed in.

The section shows even when the product has no other product document.

![Product page in the shop: the highlighted Documents section with the Show Data Sheet and Show Safety Data Sheet links, each with a download icon](static/description/en/02_pagina_produs_fise.png)

When the product also has standard product documents published on the website (from the
**Documents** smart button of the product form), the sheets are listed after them, in the
same **Documents** list.

![Product page in the shop: the Documents section lists the product's own document, followed by the Show Data Sheet link](static/description/en/03_pagina_produs_documente.png)

Products without a data sheet show no link; without any document, the **Documents** section
is not shown.
