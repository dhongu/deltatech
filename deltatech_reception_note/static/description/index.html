Overview

Receives goods from a vendor on reception notes that draw on the open quantities of several purchase orders at once, instead of validating the receipt of each order separately. Useful when one delivery covers several orders, or one order arrives in several deliveries.

Purchase order types. Every purchase order gets a Type:

  • ✓
    Normal: the standard order;
  • ✓
    RFQ Only: a request that keeps the quantities still expected from the vendor; it cannot be confirmed and has a Set as sent button;
  • ✓
    Reception Note: the delivery actually received, with the vendor's Delivery Note No.

Create reception note. On a Normal purchase order, Actions > Create reception note moves its open quantities to a new RFQ Only order in the RFQ Sent state: same vendor, company, currency, fiscal position, payment terms, prices and taxes, and the quantity not received yet on each line. The receipts of the source order that are Ready are cancelled, so the goods are received only through reception notes.

Reception note. A purchase order of type Reception Note holds the products and quantities delivered. When it is confirmed, its quantities are taken from the sent RFQ Only orders of the same vendor and company, oldest first, in the unit of measure of the product, and the receipt is created by the standard confirmation. An RFQ Only order that is fully consumed is marked as empty.

Ignore quantities. If a product or a quantity is not found on a sent RFQ Only order, the confirmation stops with a message. Tick Ignore quantities on the reception note to confirm it anyway; the forced quantities are listed in the chatter.

Menus and filters. Purchase > Reception notes has three entries:

  • ✓
    Prepare reception note: the reception notes not confirmed or invoiced yet;
  • ✓
    To arrive: incoming transfers in the Ready state;
  • ✓
    To invoice: incoming transfers in the Done state.

The purchase lists show the Type and Date sent as optional columns, and the search has the Not empty and Empty filters for RFQ Only orders.

Limitations

  • The module adds no printed document of its own: a reception note is printed with the standard purchase order reports.
  • To invoice lists every done incoming transfer, whether it was invoiced or not.

Versions

20.0.0.1.7 (2026-11-03)

  • Description rewritten after the code: the three purchase order types, the Create reception note action (open quantities moved to an RFQ Only order), the consumption of those quantities when a reception note is confirmed, Ignore quantities and the Reception notes menu.
  • Removed the claims of grouping transfers into one note and of a dedicated printed report; added a Limitations section.
  • Summary and Apps banner aligned with the description.

20.0.0.1.6 (2026-10-16)

  • Development status raised from Beta to Production/Stable: the module has been in production use at customers for a long time.

20.0.0.1.5 (2026-10-15)

  • Fixed: a reception note consumed the sent RFQs without unit conversion, so a note of 1 dozen took 1 unit from an RFQ of 12 units, and 6 units emptied an RFQ of 2 packs of 12. The quantities are now compared in the product unit, and what is left on the RFQ is written back in the unit of the RFQ line. The missing or forced quantity in the messages is shown in the unit of the reception note line (RECEPTION-001).
  • Fixed: confirming again a reception note that was already confirmed (through RPC, an import or a double click) consumed the sent RFQs a second time. Only the notes that are confirmed now, from Draft or Sent, consume the RFQs (RECEPTION-003).
  • Port of 19.0.0.1.5 and 19.0.0.1.6; on 20.0 the purchase lines use uom_id (19.0: product_uom_id).

20.0.0.1.4 (2026-10-04)

  • Fixed: confirming a reception note consumed sent RFQs of every allowed company, so a receipt in one company could reduce (and mark empty) the RFQ of another company. Only the sent RFQs of the reception note company are consumed now, in that company context (RECEPTION-002).
  • Fixed: the "Create reception note" wizard created the RFQ-only counterpart in the active company and its default currency, while copying the prices and taxes of the source order unchanged. The new RFQ now keeps the company, currency and fiscal position of the source order (RECEPTION-004). The amounts of new RFQs created from foreign-currency orders change accordingly (same figures, now in the right currency).
  • Port of 19.0.0.1.4 (dhongu/deltatech#3118); the code applied unchanged on 20.0 (the purchase lines already use uom_id).

19.0.0.1.3 (2026-09-29)

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.

19.0.0.1.2 (2026-07-29)

  • The two errors raised when confirming a reception note now name the missing coverage as a sent RFQ and tell the user what to do about it: tick "Ignore quantities" to receive the goods anyway. Until now the message only stated that the product or the quantity was not found, leaving no clue that the field exists.
  • Restored the Romanian translation of those two errors and of the chatter summary of forced quantities. All three had gone stale: the messages were reworked from .format({}) to named %-placeholders without regenerating i18n/ro.po, so the msgid no longer matched and the translation was silently dropped — the user saw English text on a Romanian database.
