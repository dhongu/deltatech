Overview

Extends the standard Odoo Contacts module with personal data for individual contacts: the Romanian CNP (Personal Numeric Code) with checksum validation, identity card details, birthdate, gender and means of transport.

Personal Data tab. On a contact of type Person with the address type Contact, the tab Personal Data groups the identity card (Card ID Number: series and number; Card ID Issued: issuing authority and date) and the CNP, Birthdate and Gender. The same fields are on the form of a contact added from a company and on the quick-create form.

Birthdate and gender from the CNP. When a CNP is entered, the birthdate is taken from digits 2 to 7 and the gender from the first digit. Changing the birthdate rewrites the date digits of the CNP and recomputes its check digit.

CNP validation. The CNP must have 13 digits and a valid check digit. Saving an existing contact with an invalid CNP stops with the message CNP invalid. On a new contact, created from the form or by import, an invalid CNP is cleared on save, without a message. An empty CNP is accepted.

Means of transport. The field Mean Transport (for example the plate number of a delegate's car) is shown under Reference in the Sales & Purchase tab of a person.

Search filters. In Contacts > Contacts, the search gets the filters Delivery and Invoice for addresses of these types. The standard filters Persons and Companies show only contacts without a parent company.

Contacts tab. On a contact that belongs to a company, the standard Contacts tab is hidden.

Contact name without the company. When the system parameter contact.get_name_only is set, contacts of type Contact are shown by their own name, without the name of their company.

Display name options for developers. The context keys show_phone, show_category and address_inline add the phone number or the tags to the contact name in relational fields, or put the name and address on one line.

Limitations

  • ✓
    Because the birthdate is filled from the CNP, and the CNP is then rebuilt from the birthdate, a CNP typed in the form with a wrong check digit gets the correct check digit without any message. A CNP of 12 digits is completed with a check digit the same way. Compare the CNP with the identity card.
  • ✓
    First digits 1 and 2 give a birth year in the 1900s, any other digit the 2000s; the gender is male only for the first digits 1 and 5.
  • ✓
    A CNP of 8 to 11 characters, or one whose digits 2 to 7 are not a valid date, makes the form fail with a technical error instead of a validation message.

Usage

The module has no settings: the Personal Data tab appears on every contact of type Person with the address type Contact.

  1. Fill in the personal data — Contacts → Contacts, open a person and go to the Personal Data tab. Under Identity Card, fill in the series and number (Card ID Number) and the issuing authority and date (Card ID Issued); under Other Information, the CNP, the Birthdate and the Gender. On a person linked to a company, the standard Contacts tab is hidden.

  1. Let the CNP fill in the birthdate and gender — on a new contact, choose Person, then type the CNP in the Personal Data tab. The birthdate comes from digits 2–7 and the gender from the first digit (1 or 5 = Male). Changing the birthdate rewrites the CNP with the new date and a recomputed check digit. The check digit is also corrected silently when you type a CNP with a wrong last digit, so compare the CNP with the identity card.

  1. Invalid CNP — saving an existing contact whose CNP does not have 13 digits or a valid check digit stops with Validation Error — CNP invalid. On a new contact, or on import, an invalid CNP is cleared on save without a message.

  1. Means of transport — in the Sales & Purchase tab of a person, group Misc, the Mean Transport field holds, for example, the plate number of the delegate's car.

  1. Delivery and invoice addresses — in the contacts search, the Delivery and Invoice filters list the delivery and invoice addresses. The standard Persons and Companies filters show only contacts without a parent company.
