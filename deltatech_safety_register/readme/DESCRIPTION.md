Register of safety events for companies that run safety or World Class Manufacturing programmes:
near misses, incidents and work accidents, each with its root cause and corrective actions, and
the safety indicators of the year.

**Key features:**

- **Safety events** classified as Near miss, Incident (no injury), Injury (no time lost),
  Lost-time injury or Fatal, numbered `SAF/<year>/NNNN`, with location, description and root cause.
- **Injured person**: an employee, a partner (contractor, visitor) or a free-text name; the
  employee department is filled in automatically.
- **Commuting accidents** (accident de traseu) are flagged, kept in the register and excluded
  from LTIFR.
- **Corrective actions** with responsible and deadline; each action schedules a To-Do activity
  for its responsible user. An event can be closed only after all its actions are done, and a
  closed event can be changed only by a Safety manager.
- **Indicators** per company and year: LTIFR (lost-time injuries x 1,000,000 / hours worked, the
  hours coming from Attendances), number of near misses, lost-time injuries and commuting accidents
  of the chosen year, plus the days without accident counted to today.
- Two access groups, Safety officer and Safety manager, with multi-company isolation.
