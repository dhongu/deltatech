Overview

Register of safety events for companies that run safety or World Class Manufacturing programmes: near misses, incidents and work accidents, each with its root cause and corrective actions, and the safety indicators of the year.

Key features:

  • Safety events
    classified as Near miss, Incident (no injury), Injury (no time lost), Lost-time injury or Fatal, numbered SAF/<year>/NNNN, with location, description and root cause.
  • Injured person
    an employee, a partner (contractor, visitor) or a free-text name; the employee department is filled in automatically.
  • Commuting accidents
    (accident de traseu) are flagged, kept in the register and excluded from LTIFR.
  • Corrective actions
    with responsible and deadline; each action schedules a To-Do activity for its responsible user. An event can be closed only after all its actions are done, and a closed event can be changed only by a Safety manager.
  • Indicators
    per company and year: LTIFR (lost-time injuries x 1,000,000 / hours worked, the hours coming from Attendances), number of near misses, lost-time injuries and commuting accidents of the chosen year, plus the days without accident counted to today.
  • Two access groups, Safety officer and Safety manager, with multi-company isolation.
Usage

Give the users the Safety officer or Safety manager access right (Settings → Users, category Human Resources, field Safety register). Users who export the register also need the Export right. Hours worked for LTIFR come from the Attendances app, so the employees must check in and out there.

  1. Safety → Events lists the register: reference, date, type, location, injured employee, commuting flag, open actions and status. Lost-time injuries and fatalities are shown in red. Use the filters and Export (XLSX) to extract the register.
1 Safety events list with near misses, an incident, injuries and lost-time injuries, their SAF references by event year, injured employee, commuting flag, open actions and status
  1. Safety → Events → New: choose the Type, the Date, the Location and describe the event. On save it gets the reference SAF/<event year>/NNNN and the status Reported.
2 A near miss form: reference, type Near miss, date, location and description, status Reported
  1. For an injury (Injury (no time lost), Lost-time injury, Fatal) set the injured person: Injured employee, Injured partner or Injured person name. The department comes from the employee. Tick Commuting accident when it happened on the way to or from work.
3 A lost-time injury form with the injured employee, the department filled in and the Commuting accident checkbox
  1. Write the Root cause, then add the Corrective actions with Responsible and Deadline. Each responsible receives a To-Do activity on the event. Press Mark done when the action is completed.
4 Corrective actions tab: one action done with its done date, one open action with the Mark done button
5 Event chatter: the To-Do activity of the responsible user with the summary and deadline of the open action
  1. Press Close once every action is done. A closed event can only be changed by a Safety manager and cannot be deleted.
6 A closed event: status Closed, closed date and all corrective actions done
  1. Safety → Corrective actions shows the open actions of all events; use My actions or group by Responsible.
7 Corrective actions list filtered on Open, with event, description, responsible, deadline and Mark done
  1. Safety → Reporting → Indicators, choose the Year: LTIFR (lost-time injuries × 1,000,000 / hours worked), lost-time injuries, hours worked, commuting accidents and near misses of that year. Days without accident and the last injury date are counted to today, whatever the year.
8 Safety indicators for last year: LTIFR, lost-time injuries, hours worked, commuting accidents, near misses, days without accident and last injury date

When a month that has already started has no hours in Attendances, LTIFR is hidden and a warning lists the missing months.

9 Safety indicators for the current year with the warning that hours worked are missing for 03/2026
