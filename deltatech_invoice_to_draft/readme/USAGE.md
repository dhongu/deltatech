1. Go to **Settings > Users & Companies > Groups**, find the group **Can reset account move to draft** and add the users who should be allowed to reset posted invoices/bills back to draft.
2. Users who are not in this group will no longer see the **Reset to Draft** button on a posted invoice/bill.
3. Users in the group additionally get a **Cancel Entry** button (next to Reset to Draft) on posted invoices/bills; clicking it resets the entry to draft and then cancels it in one step.
