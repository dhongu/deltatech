Payment Term Correction and Flexibility
=======================================

This module provides a necessary fix and enhancement for the standard Odoo payment term management. It is designed to address specific edge cases or limitations in how payment terms are calculated and displayed, ensuring more accurate and reliable financial forecasting and reporting.

Key Features
============

1.  **Refined Payment Term Logic**:
    *   Adds a dedicated view for configuring and fine-tuning payment term lines.
    *   Ensures that complex payment scenarios (like split payments or specific due date offsets) are handled correctly.

2.  **Accounting Integration**:
    *   Seamlessly integrates with the Odoo `account` module to ensure that all financial documents (Invoices, Bills, etc.) correctly reflect the adjusted payment terms.
    *   Improves the consistency of due date calculations across the entire system.

3.  **Enhanced Data Management**:
    *   Provides a foundation for more advanced financial and credit control features within the Deltatech suite.

Usage
=====

1.  Navigate to **Accounting > Configuration > Invoicing > Payment Terms**.
2.  Open or create a new **Payment Term**.
3.  Configure the lines as needed, taking advantage of the refined logic provided by the module.
4.  Apply these payment terms to partners or individual sales/purchase documents.
5.  Check the calculated **Due Date** on generated invoices to ensure they match your business requirements.
