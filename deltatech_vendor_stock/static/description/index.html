Overview

When the own stock is not enough, the salesperson wants to know whether the vendor can still supply the product, and whether another warehouse of the company has it. This module records the stock reported by the vendor and shows it, together with the free stock per warehouse, on the sales order.

Vendor stock on the vendor pricelist: adds Quantity Available on the vendor pricelist line (product.supplierinfo), on the form and as an optional column of the list, for example in the Vendors list of the product. The vendor stock of a product is the sum of these quantities over its vendor lines of the current company, of its parent companies and the lines without a company; a line set on a specific variant counts only for that variant.

Vendor stock on the sales order line: the availability popover of the order line (the chart icon next to the quantity) shows a Vendor Stock row when the vendor has stock, converted to the unit of measure of the line. Vendor Quantity Available is also available as an optional column of the order lines.

Coloured availability icon: the chart icon of the order line is coloured by availability: green when the demand will be fulfilled, cyan when it will be fulfilled but there is no free quantity today, red when it will not be fulfilled, yellow when it will not be fulfilled but the vendor has stock.

Free stock per warehouse: when the company has more than one warehouse, the popover lists the free stock in each warehouse with stock, the warehouse of the order first, and opens the forecast report of that warehouse on click. The same information is available in the optional Stock/WH column of the order lines.

Configuration

  • Fill in Quantity Available on the vendor lines of the products, by hand or by import.
  • The colours of the icon are set in Sales > Configuration > Settings, in the Quotations & Orders section.
  • The system parameter deltatech_vendor_stock.use_only_main_location set to 1 limits the free stock per warehouse to the main stock location of each warehouse.

Limitations

  • Other Quantity Available (the Other Stock row of the popover) is not filled in by this module; it stays 0 unless another module computes it.
Versions

19.0.1.2.2 (2026-11-03)

  • Description, summary and banner rewritten after the code: yellow means the vendor has stock (cyan is fulfilled without free quantity today), the colours are configurable, and the free stock per warehouse in the availability popover is documented.

19.0.1.2.1 (2026-10-17)

  • The vendor stock counts only the vendor rows of the current company, of its parent companies and the rows without a company; vendor stock recorded for another company is no longer added.
  • On a sale order line the vendor stock is computed for the company of the order.

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-10-15)

  • The availability popover lists the free stock per warehouse as a table, read when the popover opens instead of for every order line: the warehouse of the order comes first, is marked and is shown even without stock; each warehouse shows its code and name.
  • Quantities that cover the quantity to deliver are green; the order warehouse is red when it does not cover it.
  • A click on a warehouse opens the forecast of the product in that warehouse.
  • The vendor stock is shown under the warehouses, and only when it is not zero.
  • Romanian translations for the popover labels; "Other Stock" is now "Alt stoc".

19.0.1.1.12 (2026-10-15)

  • The availability popover shows the vendor stock and the stock per warehouse again: the widget now loads these fields itself instead of relying on columns declared in the order line view.
  • The unit of measure is shown again next to the quantities in the popover (many2one values are objects in 19.0, so product_uom_id[1] was empty).
  • Each warehouse is listed on its own line in the popover.

19.0.1.1.11 (2026-10-09)

  • Apps Store banner (banner.json).

19.0.1.1.10 (2026-09-29)

  • The stock colors set in Settings are applied again: the colors service imported jsonrpc, which no longer exists, so the widget silently fell back to the default colors.
  • Warehouse stock is read in one batch per warehouse, not once per line and warehouse.
  • Only the warehouses of the order's company are listed, and only for storable lines that show the availability widget.
  • Warehouse and vendor quantities are shown in the unit of measure of the line.

19.0.1.1.9 (2026-09-29)

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
