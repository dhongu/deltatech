Overview

Deliver the goods straight from the customer invoice: when an invoice is posted, the storable products are added to a sale order, the order is confirmed and the delivery is validated, in one step and without a separate quotation.

  • One step
    posting the invoice creates the sale order, confirms it and delivers the goods from stock.
  • Enabled per journal
    only the sales journals with Delivery from invoice checked use the flow, so the invoices issued from sale orders keep the standard behaviour.
  • Only what needs a delivery
    services and lines already linked to a sale order are left as they are.
  • Same values as the invoice
    quantity, unit, price, discount and taxes are copied to the order line, which stays linked to the invoice line, so the order is shown as delivered and invoiced.
  • Stock check before posting
    when the stock is not enough, the invoice is not posted and the message lists each product with the available and the requested quantity.
  • Returns on the same invoice
    a line with a negative quantity brings the goods back to stock.

Configuration

  1. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Journals and open the sales journal used for these invoices.
  2. Check Delivery from invoice. A separate journal, for example Store invoices, keeps the invoices issued from sale orders on the standard flow.
  3. The warehouse of the generated order must deliver in one step (Inventory > Configuration > Warehouses, Outgoing shipments: Deliver goods directly).

Usage

  1. Create a customer invoice in the journal with Delivery from invoice and add the products.
  2. Click Confirm. The sale order is created and confirmed, the delivery is validated and a note on the invoice links to the order.
  3. When a product does not have enough stock, the invoice stays in draft and the message shows the available and the requested quantity.
  4. To take goods back on the same invoice, add the product with a negative quantity: a return is validated together with the delivery.

With l10n_ro_stock_account_date installed, the stock accounting entries of the delivery take the accounting date of the invoice, so the sale (707) and the cost of the goods sold (607) fall in the same period.

Limits:

  • Use this journal only when the goods leave on the invoice date. Goods delivered earlier, especially in a previous month, are recorded at the delivery date (418) and invoiced afterwards, from the delivery.
  • A negative line on the invoice must refer to the original invoice (number and date). The goods returned this way enter the stock at the current cost of the product, not at the cost of the original delivery: for an exact reversal of the cost, return them from the original delivery and issue a credit note.

Versions

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-12)

  • Migrated to Odoo 19.
  • The flow is enabled per sales journal (Delivery from invoice). Up to 18.0 it ran on every customer invoice; when upgrading, the option is checked on all the sales journals, so the behaviour stays the same.
  • The discount of the invoice line is copied to the sale order line, and the order line stays linked to the invoice line.
  • The lines without a sale order always go to a new order. Up to 18.0 they could be added to an existing order of the customer, which was then not delivered.
  • The delivery keeps the reserved quantities: every move with a demand is delivered in full. The 18.0 code deleted the moves that already had a quantity, so those products were not delivered.
  • When the stock is not enough, the message lists each product with the available and the requested quantity.
  • The delivery is refused, with a clear message, for warehouses that deliver in more than one step.
  • A negative quantity is returned by the standard Odoo 19 flow; the custom stock rule override was removed.
  • The delivery takes the accounting date of the invoice, so that the sale and the cost of the goods sold fall in the same period.
  • Own module icon, in the flat style of the other invoice modules, instead of the generic gears.
