1. Go to *Invoicing > Configuration > Journals* and open the sales journal used for these invoices.
2. Check **Delivery from invoice**. A separate journal, for example *Store invoices*, keeps the invoices
   issued from sale orders on the standard flow.
3. The warehouse of the generated order must deliver in one step (*Inventory > Configuration > Warehouses*,
   *Outgoing shipments: Deliver goods directly*).
