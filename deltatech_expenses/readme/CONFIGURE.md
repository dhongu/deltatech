1. Prepare a journal whose default account is **542** (advances to be settled). Choose it as the
   **Expense Journal** of each report.
2. The **Cash Journal** (the first cash journal by default), the **Diem Journal** (a general
   journal) and the **Diem Account** (625 by default) are filled in on the report and can be
   changed there.
3. In *Settings > Users*, give each user a role for expense reports: **Angajat** (employee),
   **Aprobator** (approver) or **Contabil** (accountant).
4. On each employee, the **Work Contact** is used as partner on the journal entries; without it,
   the entries are posted without a partner.
