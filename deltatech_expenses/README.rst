Cash advances to employees, settled with an expense report, the way
Romanian accounting books them: the advance goes to account 542, the
employee brings the receipts, and validating the report books the
purchase receipts, the per diem and the difference to return or to pay,
until 542 is back to zero for that employee.

- **Advance on account 542**: The advance is paid from the cash journal
  and booked on the employee's 542.
- **Receipts as report lines**: Each line is a receipt, entered with VAT
  included; validation creates the purchase receipts and settles them
  from the advance. A line can also be a payment to a supplier made from
  the advance.
- **Per diem**: A daily amount (42.5 by default) times the number of
  days, booked on a travel expense account (625 by default).
- **Difference settled**: What is left of the advance is returned by the
  employee, or what was spent above it is paid to the employee, through
  the cash journal.
- **Approval roles**: Employee, Approver and Accountant: the approver
  validates the advance, only the accountant posts or reverses the
  report.
- **Employee view**: A smart button on the employee shows their expense
  reports; each report can be printed.

The module does not replace the standard Expenses app (``hr_expense``),
which reimburses expenses paid by employees and has no cash advance.
Both can be used together; the optional module
``deltatech_expenses_hr_expense`` brings standard expenses into this
report without booking them twice.

Configuration
=============

1. Prepare a journal whose default account is **542** (advances to be
   settled). Choose it as the **Expense Journal** of each report.
2. The **Cash Journal** (the first cash journal by default), the **Diem
   Journal** (a general journal) and the **Diem Account** (625 by
   default) are filled in on the report and can be changed there.
3. In *Settings > Users*, give each user a role for expense reports:
   **Angajat** (employee), **Aprobator** (approver) or **Contabil**
   (accountant).
4. On each employee, the **Work Contact** is used as partner on the
   journal entries; without it, the entries are posted without a
   partner.
5. For expense reports in a foreign currency, create a cash journal with
   that **Currency** (account 5314) and an expense journal on a 542
   account in foreign currency, keep the exchange rates up to date and
   set the exchange gain/loss accounts (765/665) in *Accounting >
   Settings*.
6. For bank transfers to the personal card of the employee and for
   company cards, set an **Outstanding Payments** and an **Outstanding
   Receipts** account (5125 "Sume în curs de decontare") on the payment
   methods of the bank / card journal. The company card journal can be
   set on the employee (*Settings* tab, **Company Card Journal**).

Usage
=====

1. In *Accounting > Vendors > Expenses Deduction*, create a report for
   the employee, with the advance amount and date, and click
   **Advance**: the advance is booked on 542 against the cash journal.
2. When the employee returns, add the receipts as lines (amount with VAT
   included, supplier, account) and, for a trip, the number of days for
   the per diem.
3. The accountant clicks **Validate**: the purchase receipts, their
   settlement from 542, the per diem and the payment of the difference
   are posted, and the report is **Done**.
4. **Invalidate** reverses a validated report, so it can be corrected.

**Supplier payment.** A line of type *Supplier Payment* is a bill paid
by the employee from the advance (Dr 401 = Cr 542). Its **Bills Paid**
button lists the supplier bills it pays, with the amount allocated to
each (partial payments allowed); without bills, it settles the oldest
open bills first. The unallocated part is a supplier advance,
reclassified Dr 4092 = Cr 401.

**Settlement mode.** *Cash* pays the advance from the cash register.
*Bank transfer (personal card)* pays it from a bank journal: Dr 542 = Cr
5125 (outstanding payments), matched with the bank statement line (Dr
5125 = Cr 5121); a refund is Dr 5125 = Cr 542. *Company card* has no
advance: the receipts were paid by the company, so each one is settled
Dr 401 = Cr 5125 of the card journal and matched with the card
statement; only the per diem is owed to the employee and paid from the
payment journal. A cash withdrawal with the company card is a separate
*Bank transfer* report (Dr 542 = Cr 5121).

**Foreign currency.** A report on a foreign currency cash journal is
kept in that currency: the advance, the receipts, the per diem and the
difference are entered in the currency, and each journal entry carries
the currency amount and its company currency value at the exchange rate
of its date (the advance at the advance date, each receipt at its date,
the per diem and the difference at the expense date). At validation, 542
is closed in the currency and the remaining company currency balance is
posted as exchange difference: Dr 665 = Cr 542 (loss) or Dr 542 = Cr 765
(gain).

Example: an advance of 1,000 RON and receipts of 800 RON leave 200 RON
to be returned by the employee; after validation, the employee's 542
balance is zero.

Changelog

Changelog
=========

19.0.3.11.0 (2026-11-05)
------------------------

- The option **Advance and Settlement via Bank Statement / Cash
  Register** moves from the company to the journal (cash, bank or
  company card journal, Journal Entries tab). An expense report takes it
  from the journal of its settlement mode (the card journal for Company
  card, the payment journal otherwise), so the same company can settle
  cash advances on the expense report and company card advances through
  the bank statement. The variant is stored on the expense report:
  reports in Draft follow a change of the journal option, reports in
  Advance or Done keep their flow, so the option no longer needs to be
  blocked by pending advances.
- Settlement mode and payment / card journal stay visible on the expense
  reports settled through the bank statement or cash register, as they
  choose the variant.
- Smart button **Balance 542** on the expense report: the balance of the
  employee on the 542 account of the report (in the account currency for
  a 542 in a foreign currency); it opens the 542 ledger of the employee,
  posted entries with the cumulated balance. Users without accounting
  read rights see the balance as a field, without the link.
- Migration: on a company that had the option, it is set on its cash,
  bank and card journals, and its expense reports keep the variant they
  were created with.
- Consultant sheet and screenshots updated (journal option, balance
  button, 542 ledger).

19.0.3.10.0 (2026-11-04)
------------------------

- New company option **Advance and Settlement via Bank Statement / Cash
  Register** (Invoicing settings, Bank & Cash): the expense report books
  only the expenses, always on 542 with the employee as partner
  (receipts, supplier payments, per diem), whatever the settlement mode,
  company card included. It posts no advance, no refund and no extra
  payment, and no exchange difference on 542: the money handed to the
  employee and the refunds are recorded from the cash register or the
  bank statement, reconciled on 542. The report has no Advance step: it
  is validated from Draft, gets its number then and ends in Done. The
  form shows the employee balance on 542 instead of the difference.
- The advance date is today by default.
- Search filters **Advance** and **Done** on the expense reports.
- The option cannot be enabled while expense reports of the company are
  in the Advance state: they are finalized first, in the flow with
  payment entries.
- The money handed to the employee and the refunds are reconciled on 542
  with the standard bank statement or cash register reconciliation:
  account 542 must allow reconciliation and the partner is the work
  contact of the employee.
- Consultant sheet: at year end the credit balance of an employee on 542
  is reclassified to 4281 (``Dr 542 = Cr 4281``), as a manual entry.
- Without payment entries, an expense report in a foreign currency is
  validated only on a 542 account in that currency (account currency
  set), so that the monthly revaluation of the localization revalues the
  balance of the employee; a report in the company currency only on a
  542 account without currency. Changing the currency proposes the
  matching expense journal.
- Consultant sheet: checks and error messages of the variant without
  payment entries.
- With the option, the status bar of the expense report shows only Draft
  and Done.
- With the option, the settlement lines on 542 are labelled "Decontare
  cheltuieli" (no advance).
- The title of a draft expense report is translated.
- Screenshots of the variant via bank statement / cash register in the
  consultant sheet.

19.0.3.9.1 (2026-11-04)

- Fix: the **Expenses Deductions** counter on the employee
  (``expenses_deduction_count``) is now restricted to HR users
  (``groups="hr.group_hr_user"``), like the company card journal. It is
  not on the public employee profile, so users without HR rights

19.0.3.9.0 (2026-11-04)
-----------------------

- A **Supplier Payment** line takes the payable account of the supplier
  (401), set from the partner and read-only; an **Expense** line keeps
  the default expense account, chosen by the accountant.
- A **Supplier Payment** line has no VAT: the taxes cannot be filled in
  and are ignored, as the bill paid already has its VAT booked. The
  amount paid is the total of the line.

19.0.3.8.0 (2026-10-29)
-----------------------

- A supplier payment line can name the bills it pays (button on the
  line, **Bills Paid**): for each bill, the allocated amount (by default
  its amount due, at most the unallocated part of the payment). On
  validation the entry Dr 401 = Cr 542 has one 401 line per bill,
  reconciled only with that bill, so the bill becomes paid or partially
  paid and shows the payment with the report number. Bills in the
  currency of the report are settled in that currency; the exchange
  difference goes to 665/765. Invalidating the report undoes the
  reconciliations.
- The unallocated part of a supplier payment is a supplier advance and
  is still reclassified Dr 4092 = Cr 401, as when no bill covers the
  payment.
- Without bills named on the line, the payment still settles the oldest
  open bills first.
- The 401 line of a supplier payment is booked on the supplier company
  (commercial partner): a line entered with a contact person of the
  supplier did not find the company's bills.

19.0.3.7.1 (2026-10-27)
-----------------------

- A direct supplier payment from the advance settles the supplier's open
  bills oldest first (by due date); it used to settle the newest bill
  first.

19.0.3.7.0 (2026-10-27)
-----------------------

- Settlement mode on the expense report: **Cash** (as before), **Bank
  transfer (personal card)** or **Company card**.

  - Bank transfer: the advance and the difference go through the bank
    journal, on the outstanding payments / receipts account of its
    payment method (5125), so they are matched with the bank statement
    lines instead of being posted twice on 5121 (Dr 542 = Cr 5125,
    refund Dr 5125 = Cr 542).
  - Company card: no advance and no 542 for the receipts, which are paid
    by the company: the receipt Dr 6xx/4426 = Cr 401 is settled Dr 401 =
    Cr 5125 of the card journal (supplier partner), matched with the
    card statement. Only the per diem is owed to the employee (Dr 625 =
    Cr 542, paid from the payment journal). The card journal can be set
    on the employee.

- The per diem entry falls back to today when the expense date is empty
  (the entry could not be created without a date).
- The migration (``migrations/19.0.3.7.0``) sets the Bank transfer mode
  on the existing reports whose payment journal is a bank or card
  journal. A report already in the Advance state keeps its posted
  advance (Dr 542 = Cr 5121, posted directly on the bank account as
  before); its difference is posted at validation on the outstanding
  account (5125) of the bank journal, which must then be set on its
  payment methods.

19.0.3.6.0 (2026-10-27)
-----------------------

- Expense reports in a foreign currency. The report currency follows the
  currency of the cash journal (it can be changed in draft) and the
  lines take it. Every journal entry (advance, receipts, settlement from
  542, per diem, difference) carries the currency amount and its company
  currency value at the exchange rate of its own date (OMFP 1802/2014
  pt. 319); the company currency balance left on 542 is closed as
  exchange difference on 665/765. The journal items of the report show
  the currency amount. The migration (``migrations/19.0.3.6.0``) keeps
  existing reports in the company currency.

19.0.3.5.2 (2026-10-09)
-----------------------

- **Fix (EXPENSES-001): the workflow and a done deduction are protected
  on the server.** The status could be changed by a direct write (for
  example over RPC) without the role checked by the Advance / Validate /
  Invalidate buttons, and the amounts and lines of a done deduction
  could still be changed after the receipts and journal entries were
  posted. The status is now changed only by the workflow buttons; on a
  done or cancelled deduction the advance, per diem, days, journals,
  accounts, dates, employee and lines can no longer be changed, and a
  correction is made by invalidating and validating again. A deduction
  is cancelled only by the accountant and only in Draft.
- In the Advance state, once the advance entry is posted, the advance
  amount, the cash and advance journals, the advance date, the employee
  and the company can no longer be changed (the settlement difference
  was computed against a different advance than the posted one: a
  fictitious refund and a credit balance on 542). Lines, days and per
  diem stay editable; to change the advance, invalidate the deduction.

19.0.3.4.1 (2026-10-03)
-----------------------

- **Fix (EXPENSES-002): the expense lines are always in the company
  currency of the deduction.** A line could get another currency (the
  journal currency, the currency of the user's main company, or one sent
  by an integration such as the HR expense import), but its amount was
  still added to the totals and posted as company currency, so the
  currency shown on the line was wrong. The line currency is now derived
  from the deduction company and a currency sent on create/write is
  ignored; amounts are entered in the company currency. The migration
  aligns the currency of existing lines; amounts, totals and posted
  entries do not change.

19.0.3.4.0 (2026-10-01)
-----------------------

- The line amount is now always the receipt total, VAT included,
  whatever the tax configuration. Before, with the standard Romanian
  purchase taxes (VAT on top of the price), VAT was added over the
  amount, so typing the receipt total inflated the expense, the
  deductible VAT and the amount owed to the employee. The migration
  turns the amount of existing lines into their previous gross (base +
  VAT), so their base and VAT do not change.
- The cash entry that settles the advance difference (refund or extra
  payment) is dated on the expense date, not on the advance date.
- A direct supplier payment from the advance that no open bill covers is
  a supplier advance: the remainder is reclassified Dr 4092 = Cr 401 and
  reconciled, so no debit balance is left on 401.
- Tax columns and a "Vouchers" tab on the deduction form; the "Payments"
  tab is shown only when it has payments. Romanian labels fixed ("Alte
  informații", "Total chitanțe", "Diferență avans", "Cont diurnă",
  journals).
- Consultant sheet and screenshots updated; the screenshot test is back
  in this module and runs without ``hr_expense``.

19.0.3.3.1 (2026-09-25)
-----------------------

- Fix: the "Expenses Deductions" smart button on the employee form had
  no group restriction, so any internal user without an expenses group
  got an access error on ``deltatech.expenses.deduction`` when opening
  an employee (e.g. a Time Off officer). The button is now shown only to
  the expenses groups (Employee, Approver, Accounting).
