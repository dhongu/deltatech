Expenses Deduction

Features:

  • Introducerea decontului de cheltuieli intr-un document distict ce genereaza automat chitante de achizitie
  • Validarea documentului duce la generarea notelor contabile de avans si inegistrarea platilor

Configuration

  1. Prepare a journal of type Cash (or Bank) whose default account is 542 (advances to be settled), and choose it as the Expense Journal of each report. It must be a cash or bank journal because the report pays the receipts and the suppliers from it, and a payment needs an outgoing payment method.
  2. The Cash Journal (the first cash journal by default), the Diem Journal (a general journal) and the Diem Account (625 by default) are filled in on the report and can be changed there.
  3. The employee is a contact (partner): create one for each person who receives advances.

Usage

  1. In Accounting > Vendors > Expenses Deduction, create a report for the employee, with the advance amount and date, and click Advance: the advance is booked on 542 against the cash journal.
  2. When the employee returns, add the receipts as lines (amount, tax, supplier, account) and, for a trip, the number of days for the per diem. If the employee paid a supplier bill from the advance (for example an older vendor bill), add a line of type Supplier Payment with that supplier (see below).
  3. Click Validate: the purchase receipts, their payment from 542, the per diem and the difference are posted, and the report is Done.
  4. Invalidate reverses a validated report, so it can be corrected.

Supplier Payment lines. Validation books the payment as Dr 401 / Cr 542 and reconciles it automatically with the supplier’s open bills on the same payable account (oldest first), so the bill is paid or partly paid without any manual matching. If the payment is larger than the open bills, the rest is an advance to the supplier and is moved to 4092 (Dr 4092 / Cr 401); when the bill arrives, the accountant offsets it (Dr 401 / Cr 4092). Without a 4092 account in the chart of accounts, the rest stays as a debit balance on 401. Invalidating the report removes the payment and the reconciliation: the bill is open again.

Example: an advance of 1,000 RON and receipts of 800 RON leave 200 RON to be returned by the employee; after validation, the employee’s 542 balance is zero.
