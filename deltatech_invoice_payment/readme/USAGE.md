1. Navigate to **Accounting > Customers > Invoices** or **Accounting > Vendors > Bills**.
2. Open an invoice or bill that has one or more payments registered/reconciled against it.
3. A **Payments** smart button appears in the button box at the top of the form (with a dollar icon). Click it to open the related payment(s) — directly on the payment form if there is only one, or as a list if there are several — so you can see exactly how much has been paid and its reconciliation status without leaving the invoice.
