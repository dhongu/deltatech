Key Features
============

This module improves financial tracking in Odoo Accounting by providing
clear visibility of payments directly within the invoice view.

Features:
---------

- Adds a dedicated widget at the top of the invoice where all related
  payments are visible.
- Provides a summary of reconciled payments for each customer invoice or
  vendor bill.
- Enhances financial management by making it easy to see exactly how
  much has been paid for a specific document.
- Streamlines the reconciliation process and helps avoid errors in
  payment tracking.

Usage:
------

1. Navigate to Accounting > Invoices or Accounting > Bills.
2. Open an invoice that has one or more payments registered.
3. You will see a new widget at the top of the invoice view showing the
   list of payments and their status.
