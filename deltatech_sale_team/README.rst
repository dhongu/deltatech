Companies organized in shops, branches or channels often want each sales
team to work on its own documents and to ship from its own warehouse.
This module adds a *Team Manager* access level and a default warehouse
for every sales team.

- **Team Manager group**: The new *Team Manager* group includes the
  salesperson group (``sales_team.group_sale_salesman``) and is included
  in ``sales_team.group_sale_salesman_all_leads``. Its members also see
  the quotations, orders, order lines, sales analysis and invoices of
  their own sales team, and those with no team. The rule on invoices is
  read-only.
- **Own team only**: Members of *Team Manager* who do not have
  ``sales_team.group_sale_salesman_all_leads`` see only their own team
  in the list of sales teams.
- **Personal invoices and contacts**: Users of the salesperson group are
  limited to invoices where they are the salesperson or none is set, and
  the users of ``sales_team.group_sale_salesman_all_leads`` see all
  invoices. Salespeople outside ``sales_team.group_sale_manager`` also
  see only contacts assigned to them, unassigned, or their own.
- **Default warehouse**: The sales team form has a *Default Warehouse*
  field. When a team is chosen on a quotation in the form, the order
  takes the team's warehouse if there is one.

The module depends on ``sales_team``, Invoicing, Inventory and
``sale_stock``. The team warehouse is not applied to orders created by
import or API. Group rules combine, so a wider rule of another group of
the same user still applies.

-----------------------

  Access*). The technical name ``deltatech_sale_team`` does not change.

20.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-02)
-----------------------

- Migration to 20.0: the record rules are now ``ir.access`` records in
  ``security/ir.access.csv``. The team, personal and "all" rules on
  invoices, orders, order lines and the sales analysis are permissions
  with the operations previously granted by the access rights. "Own
  Sales Teams" and "Personal Partners" are restrictions that apply only
  to team managers (without "All Documents") and to salesmen (without
  "Administrator"), respectively, as in 19.0.
- New tests for the team-based access.
