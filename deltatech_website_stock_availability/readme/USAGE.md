1. On a product (**Inventory** tab), set the **Customer Safety Lead Time** (in days) — extra buffer time added on top of the product's own delivery lead time.
2. On the vendor line of a product (**Purchase** tab, vendor pricelist), the **Delivery Lead Time** column shows the number of days needed from that supplier.
3. On the website product page, the module then shows visitors the available quantity when it is low, and computes an estimated delivery date that takes into account: the product's own lead time, the customer safety lead time, and — if the product is out of stock — the vendor's delivery lead time and available vendor quantity.
4. Customers can still place an order even when stock is insufficient; the estimated delivery date shown reflects the extra time needed to replenish from the supplier.

No additional configuration is required beyond filling in the lead time fields on the product/vendor records.
