Transfers:

1. Open a receipt, delivery or internal transfer and click **Import
   Lines**.
2. Select the file, map the columns (product, quantity, optionally unit
   of measure, lot/serial number and description), click **Preview**,
   then **Import**.

Inventory count:

1. Go to **Inventory > Operations > Physical Inventory** and click
   **Import Counts**.
2. Choose the **Counted Location**, select the file and map the product,
   quantity and, for tracked products, lot columns.
3. Click **Preview**, then **Import**. The counted quantities appear in
   the list; check them and click **Apply All**.
