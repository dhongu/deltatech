Loads stock moves and inventory counts from a file with the wizard of
``deltatech_import_lines``:

- **Transfers** (receipts, deliveries, internal transfers): an **Import
  Lines** button on transfers that are not done or cancelled. Each row
  becomes a move between the locations of the transfer. Moves added to a
  confirmed transfer are confirmed, and Odoo merges them with the
  existing moves of the same product.
- **Lots and serial numbers**: a **Lot/Serial Number** column sets the
  lot of the row. On receipts whose operation type allows new lots, a
  lot that does not exist is created at validation; on the other
  operations the lot must exist. A serial number row must have the
  quantity 1.
- **Inventory counts**: an **Import Counts** button on **Physical
  Inventory** sets the counted quantity of the products (and lots) in
  the chosen location. Rows of the same product and lot are added up. A
  product tracked by lot or serial number needs an existing lot on its
  row. The count is applied by the user, with **Apply** or **Apply
  All**.

Installed automatically when both Inventory and
``deltatech_import_lines`` are installed.

Usage
=====

Transfers:

1. Open a receipt, delivery or internal transfer and click **Import
   Lines**.
2. Select the file, map the columns (product, quantity, optionally unit
   of measure, lot/serial number and description), click **Preview**,
   then **Import**.

Inventory count:

1. Go to **Inventory > Operations > Physical Inventory** and click
   **Import Counts**.
2. Choose the **Counted Location**, select the file and map the product,
   quantity and, for tracked products, lot columns.
3. Click **Preview**, then **Import**. The counted quantities appear in
   the list; check them and click **Apply All**.
