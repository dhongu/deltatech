Define alternative components on the lines of a bill of materials and use them on
manufacturing orders when the original component is missing, either by hand or
automatically, in the order you choose.

**✨ Key features:**

- **Alternatives per BoM line**, each with its own quantity and unit: 1 kg of resin can be
  replaced by 1,200 g of another resin, 1 unit of a part by 2 units of another one.
- **Priority and validity dates** on every alternative, for temporary replacements or for a
  component phased out at a given date.
- **Automatic substitution**: when a BoM line is set to *Automatic*, the shortage of the
  original component is covered from the alternatives in stock, at confirmation and again
  at every availability check.
- **Partial substitution**: a component can be split between the original product and one
  or more alternatives, so the stock of the original is used first. It can be turned off
  to allow only alternatives that cover the whole shortage.
- **Substitute button** on the components of a manufacturing order, with the free and
  forecasted stock of the original and of every alternative, and the quantity converted
  for each of them.
- **No false consumption warnings**: the expected consumption follows the substitution,
  so *Produce All* does not ask to confirm a consumption that differs from the BoM.
- **Traceability**: every substituted move keeps the component it replaced; a
  *Component Substitutions* report lists what was consumed instead of what.
