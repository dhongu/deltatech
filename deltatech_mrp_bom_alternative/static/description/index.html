Overview

Define alternative components on the lines of a bill of materials and use them on manufacturing orders when the original component is missing, either by hand or automatically, in the order you choose.

✨ Key features:

  • Alternatives per BoM line
    , each with its own quantity and unit: 1 kg of resin can be replaced by 1,200 g of another resin, 1 unit of a part by 2 units of another one.
  • Priority and validity dates
    on every alternative, for temporary replacements or for a component phased out at a given date.
  • Automatic substitution
    when a BoM line is set to Automatic, the shortage of the original component is covered from the alternatives in stock, at confirmation and again at every availability check.
  • Partial substitution
    a component can be split between the original product and one or more alternatives, so the stock of the original is used first. It can be turned off to allow only alternatives that cover the whole shortage.
  • Substitute button
    on the components of a manufacturing order, with the free and forecasted stock of the original and of every alternative, and the quantity converted for each of them.
  • No false consumption warnings
    the expected consumption follows the substitution, so Produce All does not ask to confirm a consumption that differs from the BoM.
  • Traceability
    every substituted move keeps the component it replaced; a Component Substitutions report lists what was consumed instead of what.

Configuration

  1. Go to Manufacturing > Configuration > Settings, section Operations:
    • Partial Substitution (on by default): a component may be split between the original product and its alternatives. When off, an alternative is used only if it covers the whole shortage.
    • Substitute on Availability Check (on by default): the automatic substitution runs again on Check Availability, not only at confirmation.
  2. Open a bill of materials. On each component line, the exchange button opens the alternatives of the line:
    • set Substitution to Manual or Automatic;
    • add the alternative components, in order of preference, with the quantity that replaces the quantity of the original component on the line, and optionally a validity period and a note.
  3. All the alternatives can also be reviewed in Manufacturing > Products > BoM Alternatives.

Usage

Automatic substitution

  1. Create a manufacturing order for a product whose BoM has components set to Automatic.
  2. Click Confirm. For each such component, the stock of the original product in the source location is used first; the missing quantity is taken from the alternatives in stock, in their order, and added as new component lines.
  3. If the stock arrives later, Check Availability repeats the substitution for the quantity still missing.

Manual substitution

  1. On a confirmed manufacturing order, the components with alternatives show an exchange button.
  2. The dialog shows the free and forecasted stock of the original component and of each valid alternative. Choose the alternative and the quantity of the original component to replace; the quantity of the alternative is converted automatically.
  3. Click Substitute. The original line keeps the rest of its quantity, a new line is added for the alternative, and a message is logged on the order.

Report

  • Manufacturing > Reporting > Component Substitutions lists the substituted moves, with the replaced component, the component consumed instead, the quantity and whether the substitution was manual or automatic.
