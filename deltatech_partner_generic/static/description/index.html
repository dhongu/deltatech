Overview

Shops, points of sale and imports often record sales for customers who are not identified, under one generic partner. That partner must not end up on a real invoice, must not be paid through the wrong journals and must not be edited by mistake, since it is reachable from every sales document. This module declares the generic partner of each company and enforces these rules.

Generic partner per company: set in Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, section Customer Invoices, field Generic Partner. The module creates a company contact named Generic that can be chosen there, or any other contact can be used. The setting only declares the partner: it is not filled in automatically on sales orders or invoices.

No validated invoice for the generic partner: a customer invoice or credit note whose customer or delivery address is the generic partner (or one of its contacts) cannot be validated. The error names the invoice and asks for the real customer. Drafts stay allowed, so the flows that go through the generic partner (Point of Sale, eCommerce, imports) keep working. Vendor bills and journal entries are not affected.

Restricted payment journals: bank and cash journals ticked as Generic Restriction (on the journal form, next to the journal type, and as an optional column in the journal list) are not proposed when a payment is made for the generic partner, either in a new payment or in the Register Payment wizard opened from an invoice. Only bank and cash journals are proposed, and the default journal is always an allowed one.

Protect the generic partner: tick Protect Generic Partner in the same setting to prevent accidental changes. The contact form then shows a warning banner, and any change, archiving or deletion is refused when saving. The protection is off by default. Users who have to change the partner are given the Generic Partner: Editor group; Settings administrators already have it. Writes made by Odoo itself (chatter, activities, portal signup tokens, geolocation) stay allowed, as do automated flows running with elevated rights.

These accounting restrictions used to live in the separate module deltatech_generic_partner_restriction, which is now an empty transition module depending on this one.

Usage

  1. Go to Invoicing → Configuration → Settings, section Customer Invoices (available without the Sales app as well): pick the Generic Partner of the company and, to keep it from being changed by mistake, tick Protect Generic Partner. Save. In a multi-company database, repeat this on every company.

  1. Go to Invoicing → Configuration → Journals and tick Generic Restriction on the journals that must not be used for anonymous customers (usually the banks).

  1. A customer invoice can be drafted on the generic partner (by hand, from a sale order, a POS or an import) and saved as usual.

  1. Confirm is refused while the generic partner is the customer or the delivery address of a customer invoice or credit note. The message names the field to change: set the real customer and confirm again. Vendor bills and journal entries are not affected.

  1. On a new customer payment for the generic partner (Invoicing → Customers → Payments), the Journal list leaves out the restricted journals, and the journal proposed by default is an allowed one. The Register Payment wizard opened from a vendor bill on the generic partner applies the same restriction.

  1. When the protection is on, a user without the Generic Partner: Editor group sees a banner on the generic partner,

and saving a change, archiving or deleting it is refused. Settings administrators already have the Generic Partner: Editor group.

Versions

19.0.2.0.5 (2026-11-03)

  • Description rewritten after the code: the generic partner is declared in the Invoicing settings, section Customer Invoices, and is not selected automatically in sales or invoicing as the old text said. The banner no longer promises it.

19.0.2.0.4 (2026-11-03)

  • A new payment for the generic partner now proposes an allowed journal by default, not the first bank journal of the company even when it was restricted.
  • The Register Payment wizard opened from an invoice now leaves out the restricted journals for the generic partner as well, including the journal of the invoice preferred payment method.
  • The generic partner setting moved from the Sales settings to the Invoicing settings, section Customer Invoices, so it is available without the Sales app (sale_management), for example with only eCommerce or Point of Sale. It now has a real description instead of a repeated title.
  • Romanian translation of the protection message reworded to the formal address.
