## 19.0.2.0.4 (2026-11-03)

- A new payment for the generic partner now proposes an allowed journal by default, not the
  first bank journal of the company even when it was restricted.
- The **Register Payment** wizard opened from an invoice now leaves out the restricted journals
  for the generic partner as well, including the journal of the invoice preferred payment method.
- The generic partner setting moved from the Sales settings to the Invoicing settings, section
  Customer Invoices, so it is available without the Sales app (`sale_management`), for example
  with only eCommerce or Point of Sale. It now has a real description instead of a repeated title.
- Romanian translation of the protection message reworded to the formal address.

  `tests/test_screenshots.py`.
