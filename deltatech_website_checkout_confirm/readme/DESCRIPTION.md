eCommerce Checkout Confirmation Enhancement
==========================================

This module adds an extra layer of order confirmation to Odoo's eCommerce checkout process. It's designed for businesses that want to ensure customers have a final chance to review their purchase before the order is finalized and sent for processing.

Key Features
============

1.  **Mandatory Confirmation Step**:
    *   Inserts a dedicated confirmation stage between the payment and the final order success page.
    *   Provides a summary of the order details, allowing the customer to perform one last check.

2.  **Order Flow Refinement**:
    *   Seamlessly integrates with Odoo's standard `website_sale` checkout flow.
    *   Ensures that only orders with a confirmed intent from the customer reach the backend for fulfillment.

3.  **Enhanced User Experience**:
    *   Improves customer trust by providing a clear, final order summary before concluding the transaction.

Usage
=====

1.  Navigate to your **Odoo Website**.
2.  Add items to your cart and proceed to the **Checkout**.
3.  After selecting the **Payment Method**, you will be presented with a final **Order Confirmation** page.
4.  Review your order and click the **Confirm** button to complete the purchase.
5.  You will then be redirected to the standard "Order Success" page.
