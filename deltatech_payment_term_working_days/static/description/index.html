Overview

This module lets a payment term count its days in working days instead of calendar days, and move a due date that falls on a weekend or on a public holiday to the next working day. Standard Odoo counts calendar days only, so a "3 days" term issued on a Friday falls due on Monday, and a 30-day term may fall due on a Saturday or on Christmas.

✨ Key features:

  • Working days per line
    — each line of a payment term counts its days in Calendar Days (the default, unchanged behaviour) or in Working Days. An installment plan can mix both.
  • Move to the next working day
    — an option on the payment term moves every due date that falls on a non-working day to the next working day. It applies to calendar and working-day lines alike.
  • Company calendar
    — the working days are those of the company's working schedule; the public holidays are the time off of the company that is not linked to a resource. Without a working schedule, Monday to Friday are working days.
  • Movable holidays
    — Easter, Pentecost and any other holiday entered as public time off are taken into account, for every year.
  • Everywhere the due date is used
    — the invoice due date, the due date of each installment line, the aged receivable and payable reports and the follow-ups all read the adjusted dates.
  • Legal ceiling warning
    — the payment term form warns when a line counted in working days may exceed 60 calendar days, the usual legal ceiling of the payment terms between professionals (EU Directive 2011/7/EU).

Configuration

Public holidays

The module reads the public holidays of the company; it does not create them.

  1. Go to Settings > Technical > Resource > Working Schedules (debug mode) and check that the company has a working schedule with the right working days.
  2. Open Settings > Technical > Resource > Resource Time Off (or Time Off > Configuration > Public Holidays when the Time Off application is installed) and add the public holidays of the year, with no resource. A holiday linked to another working schedule or to another company is ignored.
  3. Add the holidays of each new year before the invoices of that year fall due. Movable holidays (Easter, Pentecost) change every year.

For Romanian companies, the public holidays of the Labour Code (art. 139), including the Orthodox Easter and Pentecost, can be generated for a whole year with the wizard of the l10n_ro_hr_pontaj module, or entered by hand.

Usage

Counting a payment term in working days

  1. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Payment Terms and open or create a payment term.
  2. On each line under Due Terms, choose Count:
    • Calendar Days — standard Odoo behaviour;
    • Working Days — the days after the invoice date (or after the end of the month) skip the weekends and the public holidays of the company.

The count does not apply to the lines due "on the X of the next month", whose day is fixed. 3. Tick Move to the Next Working Day to move every due date that falls on a weekend or on a public holiday to the next working day. 4. Save. The new due dates apply to the invoices and bills created or reset to draft from now on; posted invoices keep their due dates.

The Preview on the right shows the due dates computed with the company calendar for the example date.

Examples

With a Monday-to-Friday schedule and the 2026 Romanian public holidays:

Invoice date Term Due date
Friday 06.03.2026 3 working days Wednesday 11.03.2026
Thursday 09.04.2026 2 working days Wednesday 15.04.2026 (Good Friday, Easter, Easter Monday skipped)
Thursday 30.04.2026 1 working day Monday 04.05.2026 (1 May skipped)
Friday 06.03.2026 15 calendar days, moved Monday 23.03.2026 instead of Saturday 21.03.2026

An invoice of Thursday 09.04.2026 with the term above: the first installment (3 working days) skips Good Friday, Easter and Easter Monday and falls due on 16.04.2026; the second one (30 calendar days, Saturday 09.05.2026) moves to Monday 11.05.2026. The printed invoice lists both due dates.

Legal ceiling

When a line counted in working days may exceed 60 calendar days after the invoice date, a warning is shown on the payment term. The overdue days, and the late payment interest, are still counted in calendar days from the day after the (moved) due date.
