A register of the promissory notes received from customers and issued to vendors.

For each note you record the series and number, the issuer and the beneficiary with their bank
accounts and banks, the amount and currency, the due date, the agreement and, optionally, the
invoice it belongs to. The type of the note fills in the company on the right side: the company is
the beneficiary of a customer note and the issuer of a vendor note.

- the notes are listed by due date, with totals for the amount and the cashed amount;
- the state changes with a button: *Not Cashed* → *Cashed*, or *Cancelled*; the state history is
  kept in the chatter;
- when the last note of an agreement is cashed, the users of the *Primeste atentionari BO* group
  are notified in the chatter.

The module keeps the register only: it does not create journal entries and does not reconcile the
linked invoice.
