1. On any transfer (**Inventory > Transfers**), the pickings list now shows an **Amount** column with the stock valuation value of that transfer (sum of the valuation layers of its moves), summable across the list.
2. On a product category form (**Inventory > Configuration > Product Categories**), use the **Propagate Accounts** action (top action menu, requires the technical/"no one" access group) to copy that category's accounting settings — valuation accounts, income/expense accounts, cost method, stock journal, etc. — down to all of its child categories at once.
3. Changing the accounting fields directly on a parent category and saving also propagates them automatically to its children.
4. Changing a product's **Cost** (standard price) while it still has stock on hand is blocked unless the user belongs to the new **Change Product Price** group (grant it under **Settings > Users**, in the relevant group/rule), preventing accidental valuation changes on products in stock.
