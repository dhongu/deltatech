Overview

Small additions to stock valuation: the value of each transfer in the transfers list, accounting settings passed from a product category to its subcategories, and control over who may change the cost of a product that is in stock.

Value of transfers. Adds an Amount column to the transfers list (for example Inventory > Operations > Transfers > Receipts), with the total at the bottom. The amount is the sum of the valuation Value of the stock moves of the transfer; moves that are not valued (for example internal transfers) count as zero.

Accounts propagated to subcategories. When the Stock Account of a product category is changed, the category's accounting settings are copied to all its subcategories: Income Account, Expense Account, Stock Account, Price Difference Account, Stock Journal, Costing Method and Inventory Valuation. The same copy can be run at any time with the Propagate Accounts action on the category form (developer mode).

Settings inherited from the parent category. When you choose a Parent Category on a category, the same accounting settings are copied from the parent.

Cost changes restricted. Only users in the Change Product Price group can change the Cost of a product that has quantity on hand. Other users get an access error. The administrator is in the group by default.

Configuration

Add users to the Change Product Price group in *Settings > Users & Companies

Groups* (developer mode).

Limitations

  • The value of a transfer is shown only in the transfers list, not on the transfer form.
  • The Stock Variation Account of the category is not propagated.

Usage

  1. Open Inventory > Operations > Transfers > Receipts. The Amount column shows the valuation value of each receipt and the total at the bottom. A receipt that is not validated yet is worth 0.00.

  1. The same column in Deliveries shows the cost of the goods that left the stock, not the sales price.

  1. Open the top product category (Inventory > Configuration > Products > Product Categories), set Income Account, Expense Account, Costing Method and Inventory Valuation, then set Stock Account and save. Saving a new Stock Account copies all these settings to every subcategory.

  1. Check a subcategory: it now has the same accounts, costing method and inventory valuation.

  1. When you change another setting of the top category, copy the settings again with the gear icon > Propagate Accounts (developer mode).

  1. On a new category, choosing a Parent Category fills in the accounting settings of the parent.

  1. A user who is not in the Change Product Price group cannot change the Cost of a product that has quantity on hand: saving shows an Access Error.

  1. Manage who may change the cost in Settings > Users & Companies > Groups > Change Product Price (developer mode). The administrator is in the group by default.

The copy to subcategories overwrites their settings, empty values included: set exceptions on a subcategory after the propagation. The cost of products without stock and the automatic cost updates (for example the average cost recomputed at a receipt) are not restricted.
