## Generating a Handover Document

1. Open **Business Processes** and navigate to the relevant **Business Project**.
2. On the project form, fill in the **Provider Information** group:
   - **Provider Company** — name of the implementing company.
   - **Provider Representative** — contact person on the provider side.
   - **Provider Testers** — team members who performed testing.
3. Fill in the **Recipient Information** group:
   - **Recipient Company** — name of the client/receiving company.
   - **Recipient Representative** — contact person on the recipient side.
   - **Recipient Testers** — client-side testers.
4. Ensure that all developments are linked to the project (they are collected automatically via the **Developments** computed field).
5. Click **Print ▸ Handover Document** to generate the PDF report.

The generated PDF lists all project developments and includes signature blocks for both parties.

## Marking Business Areas as Checked

On each **Business Area** form, tick the **Handover Checked** checkbox to indicate that the area has been reviewed and accepted during handover.
