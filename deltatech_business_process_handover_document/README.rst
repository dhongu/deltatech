Extends the **deltatech_business_process** module to generate a formal
**Handover Document** (proces-verbal de predare-primire) in PDF format
directly from a Business Project.

**✨ Key features:**

- Adds provider and recipient company details (name, representative,
  testers) to the Business Project form.
- Automatically collects all developments linked to the project.
- Adds a **Handover Checked** checkbox on each Business Area for
  sign-off tracking.
- Generates a QWeb PDF report (A4 format) accessible via the **Print**
  menu on the Business Project.
- Report is available in both English and Romanian.

Usage
=====

Generating a Handover Document
------------------------------

1. Open **Business Processes** and navigate to the relevant **Business
   Project**.
2. On the project form, fill in the **Provider Information** group:

   - **Provider Company** — name of the implementing company.
   - **Provider Representative** — contact person on the provider side.
   - **Provider Testers** — team members who performed testing.

3. Fill in the **Recipient Information** group:

   - **Recipient Company** — name of the client/receiving company.
   - **Recipient Representative** — contact person on the recipient
     side.
   - **Recipient Testers** — client-side testers.

4. Ensure that all developments are linked to the project (they are
   collected automatically via the **Developments** computed field).
5. Click **Print ▸ Handover Document** to generate the PDF report.

The generated PDF lists all project developments and includes signature
blocks for both parties.

Marking Business Areas as Checked
---------------------------------

On each **Business Area** form, tick the **Handover Checked** checkbox
to indicate that the area has been reviewed and accepted during
handover.
