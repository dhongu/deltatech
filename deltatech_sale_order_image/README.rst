Adds the product image to the lines of the quotation and sale order PDF.

The image is the product (or variant) image, printed in its own column
before the description. It can be switched off per company in *Sales >
Configuration > Settings > Quotations & Orders > Include image* (on by
default). Section and combo rows keep the table aligned.

Usage
=====

1. Open the product (**Sales > Products > Products**) and upload its
   image. For variants, the variant image is used.
2. Create a quotation (**Sales > Orders > Quotations**) with product
   lines, sections and notes.
3. Print it with the **Print** button (on a confirmed order, from the ⚙
   menu > **Print**). Each product line shows its image, at most 50 px
   wide, in the first column. The same PDF goes out by email, and the
   pro-forma invoice shows the image too. Lines without an image keep an
   empty cell, and section, note and combo rows span the whole table.
4. To print without images, clear **Include image** in **Sales >
   Configuration > Settings** (Quotations & Orders). The option is on by
   default and is set per company.

The image only appears on the printed document, not on the quotation
form.
