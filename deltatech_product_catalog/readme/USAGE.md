1. Open a product (or a website public category) in the **Sales**, **Inventory** or **Website** app.
2. From the **Print** menu, choose **Product Catalog**.
3. Run it on a single product, a selection of products, or a whole product public category: the report lays out each product as a card (image, name, price) in a grid, giving you a printable/PDF catalog for one or many products at once.
