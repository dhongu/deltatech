Overview

Adds sales-team tracking to analytic accounting and a tool to split analytic amounts across several analytic accounts by percentage.

Analytic distribution by sales team

Sale team on distribution models. The analytic distribution model gets a Sale team field. On customer invoices, credit notes and sales receipts, the product lines take the distribution of the model whose Sale team is the invoice's sales team, when exactly one such model exists. The distribution is recomputed when the sales team of the invoice changes.

Sales team on analytic items. Analytic items keep a sales team and the product category of their product:

  • ✓
    from the sales team of the customer invoice, credit note or sales receipt;
  • ✓
    for journal entries of a stock transfer (when the stock and sales-stock modules are installed), from the sales team of the sale order of the transfer;
  • ✓
    on vendor bills and refunds, from the sales team whose name equals the name of the analytic account.

In Accounting > Accounting > Transactions > Analytic Items and Accounting > Reporting > Analytic Report, the Group By menu offers Sale team and Product category.

Analytic split

Analytic split templates. A template lists analytic accounts with a percentage each; the percentages must total 100.

Analytic splits. A split distributes either a fixed Amount or an existing analytic item (type Line) according to a template:

  • Compute
    fills the split lines from the template;
  • Confirm
    creates one analytic item per split line, on the split date, and checks that the lines add up to the amount to split. For a Line split, the original analytic item is deleted and replaced by the new ones;
  • Reset
    deletes the created analytic items and returns an Amount split to draft. A Line split cannot be reset.

Configuration

  • Accounting > Configuration > Analytic Accounting > Analytic Distribution Models
    set the Sale team on the models used for sales.
  • Accounting > Configuration > Analytic Accounting > Analytic split templates
    and Analytic splits.
  • ✓
    The split menus are visible to accounting administrators when the Analytic Accounting setting is enabled. Only users in the group Analitic split user (Settings > Users & Companies > Groups) may open templates and splits.

In Odoo Community the app is named Invoicing instead of Accounting.

Limitations

  • On vendor bills the sales team is found only by an exact match between the analytic account name and the sales team name.
  • A split without a Name cannot be confirmed: the analytic items need a description.

Versions

19.0.0.0.11 (2026-11-03)

  • Description rewritten from the code: removed the automatic split of sale analytic lines into stock value and margin and the Split Sale Analytic setting, which are disabled in 19.0, and the split fields on the analytic account form, which do not exist. Documented the distribution by sales team, the sales team on analytic items and the manual analytic split.

19.0.0.0.9

  • Fixed: with this module installed, Timesheets (hr_timesheet, and so sale_timesheet or helpdesk_timesheet) could not be installed: the Sale team and Product category groupings were added to the base analytic search view, which the timesheet analysis report inherits without having these fields. They are now added to the accounting search view of analytic items (Analytic Items, Analytic Reporting).

19.0.0.0.8

  • Fixed ANALYTICSPLIT-001: confirming a Line split before Compute, or after removing its lines, deleted the analytic line to split without creating any replacement. Confirmation now requires split lines whose total equals the amount to split (the source line for a Line split), checked before anything is created or deleted.
  • Fixed ANALYTICSPLIT-002: confirming an already confirmed split again (RPC or a retried request) created a second set of analytic entries and lost the link to the first one. A split can now be confirmed only once.
  • Fixed ANALYTICSPLIT-003: posting an invoice with an analytic distribution failed with AttributeError: stock_move_ids on databases without stock_account. The sales team lookup through the stock transfer is now done only when the stock modules are installed.
  • Fixed ANALYTICSPLIT-004: posting a vendor bill whose analytic distribution used only an additional analytic plan failed with KeyError: 'account_id'.

19.0.0.0.7

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.

Changelog

19.0.0.0.10 (2026-10-22)

  • Listed on Odoo Apps as Account Analytic Split (was Account Analytic). The technical name deltatech_account_analytic does not change.

19.0.0.0.6 (2026-07-31)

  • Fix: analytic lines generated from out_receipt (Sales Receipt) invoices kept team_id empty on account.analytic.line, even though analytic_distribution on the source account.move.line was already correct (fixed in 19.0.0.0.5). AccountAnalyticLine.create() copies team_id from move_id.team_id only for out_invoice/out_refund — out_receipt was missing from that list, unlike the equivalent list in account_move.py. Added out_receipt so both stay consistent.

19.0.0.0.5 (2026-07-27)

  • Fix: _compute_analytic_distribution looked up the distribution model by move_id.team_id but never declared it as a dependency, so lines kept their stale (often empty) analytic distribution whenever the sales team changed on an existing invoice, instead of only at creation time.
  • Fix: out_receipt (Sales Receipt) invoices were excluded from the team-based analytic matching, so their revenue lines never got an analytic account, unlike out_invoice/out_refund lines.
