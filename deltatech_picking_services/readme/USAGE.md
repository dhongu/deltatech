1. Open any stock transfer (**Inventory**) and go to the new **Services** tab, next
   to Operations.
2. Add lines picking a service-type product, quantity, unit of measure and unit
   price — the description and UoM are pre-filled from the product but can be
   edited. Odoo computes a subtotal per line and a total for the tab.
3. These service lines are informational/for costing purposes on the transfer; they
   do not create stock moves and don't affect delivery/receipt quantities.
