1. Open a posted customer invoice or vendor bill and click **Pay**.
2. Choose the **Journal** and the **Payment Method**.
3. Check **Set as default** and click **Create Payment**.
4. The next time you register a payment for this partner (or one of its
   contacts), the wizard proposes the same journal and payment method.

The stored value can be checked or changed on the partner form, tab
**Sales & Purchase**, field **Payment Method** (in the Sales section for
receipts, in the Purchase section for payments).

The checkbox is not shown when several partners are paid at once, nor to
users without the **Contact Creation** right.
