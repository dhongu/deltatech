Product Reordering Limit

Custom reordering limits at the product template level.

This module adds custom reordering limits at the product template level.

  • Adds "Total Minimum" and "Total Maximum" fields to the Product Template form.
  • Provides a "Below Minimum" filter in the Product Search view.
  • Calculates if a product is below its minimum reordering limit based on internal stock locations.
  • Works across all product variants for a given template.
