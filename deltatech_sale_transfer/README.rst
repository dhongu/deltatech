An order may need more of a product than its warehouse holds, while
another warehouse of the company has some. On confirmation, this module
prepares a transfer from the other warehouse for the missing quantity.

- **Check on confirmation**: Confirming a sales order checks each
  storable product line against the stock of the order's warehouse.
  Services and other non-storable products are ignored.
- **Transfer for the shortfall**: One transfer per order moves the
  missing quantity from the other warehouse to the order's warehouse,
  for no more than the other warehouse holds, and is reserved at once.
- **Type and grouping**: *Type auto transfer* on the other warehouse
  sets the operation type (its internal type when empty). *Group
  Transfer With Delivery* on the order's warehouse groups the transfer
  with the order's delivery.
- **Traceability**: The transfer gets a note linking to the order, and
  the order gets a message naming the transfer.
- **Automatic validation**: When *Auto Confirm Transfer* is on for the
  other warehouse (the ``confirm_transfer`` context key overrides it),
  the transfer is validated at once. If its products cannot all be
  reserved, an error stops the order confirmation.

It depends on ``sale_stock``. Only the first other warehouse of the
company is used as the source. The warehouse form of this version does
not show *Auto Confirm Transfer*.

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-11-11)
-----------------------

- Apps Store page: a fuller description (what the module does, the
  problem it solves, its main functions, dependencies and limits). No
  change in behavior.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-03)
-----------------------

- Fixed: the transfer request created on sale confirmation mixed the
  sale line unit with the product unit. For 1 Dozen ordered with 6 Units
  in the sale warehouse it computed 1 - 6, found no shortage and created
  no transfer; in other cases it could request more than the source
  warehouse had. Shortage and source availability are now computed in
  the product unit and the transfer move is created in the product unit
  (before: in the sale line unit). Repeated lines of the same product no
  longer count the same destination/source stock twice
  (SALETRANSFER-001). Transfer quantities change whenever the sale unit
  differs from the product unit or a product is repeated.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
