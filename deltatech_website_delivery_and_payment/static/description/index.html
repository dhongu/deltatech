Overview

Limits the delivery methods and the payment providers offered in the eCommerce checkout by the delivery method chosen, the value and weight of the order and the tags of the customer.

Features

  • Payment providers by delivery method
    a delivery method can list the payment providers allowed with it, for example cash on delivery only with the courier. On the payment step the customer sees only those providers. A delivery method without a list allows all of them.
  • Value limit of a payment provider
    above this order total (taxes included) the provider is no longer offered.
  • Weight limits of a delivery method
    the method is offered only when the estimated weight of the cart is between its minimum and maximum. An empty limit is not checked.
  • Restrictions by customer tags
    a delivery method or a payment provider can be hidden from the customers that have one of its tags. For delivery methods the tags of the customer's company are read; for payment providers, the tags of the customer on the order.

Configuration

  • Website > Configuration > eCommerce > Delivery Methods, on a delivery method:
    • Weight limits, next to the price;
    • tab Payment Acquirer: Payments Provider Allowed and Restrict for partners with label.
  • Website > Configuration > eCommerce > Payment Providers, on a provider, group Restrictions: Value Limit and the tags.

The module also adds a Logo to the delivery method form and a Payment Provider field to the Sales & Purchase tab of the contact form. They are stored for other modules and reports; this module does not use them in the checkout.

Usage

Publish the payment providers and the delivery methods used on the shop first (Website → Configuration → eCommerce → Payment Providers and Delivery Methods). The rules below only hide options; they never add a provider or a delivery method that is not enabled and published.

Configure the rules

  1. Payment providers allowed with a delivery method — open the delivery method (e.g. Curier rapid), go to the Payment Acquirer tab (1) and fill in Payments Provider Allowed (2). With this delivery method the customer gets only these providers. An empty list allows all of them.

  1. Weight limits — on the delivery method, next to the price, fill in the minimum and the maximum of Weight limits (highlighted), in the unit shown at the end of the row. The method is offered only when the estimated weight of the cart (product weight × quantity, goods only) is between them. A limit of 0 is not checked: 50 - 0 means "from 50 kg up".

  1. Hide a delivery method for customer tags — on the Payment Acquirer tab (1), fill in Restrict for partners with label (2). Customers whose company has one of these tags do not see the method.

  1. Value limit and tags of a payment provider — open the provider, group Restrictions: Value Limit (1) is the order total, taxes and delivery included, above which the provider is no longer offered (a total equal to the limit is still allowed; the limit is compared with the total in the order currency, without conversion); Restrict Label (2) hides the provider from the customers on the order that have one of the tags.

  1. Tag the customers — on the customer (Website → eCommerce → Customers), fill in Tags (1).

Which tags are read

For delivery methods the module reads the tags of the customer's company (the commercial partner); for payment providers, the tags of the customer on the order. For a contact person of a company, a tag set only on the company hides the delivery method but not the payment provider: set it on both when both must be hidden.

What the customer sees in the checkout

  1. Delivery step — the Delivery method list (1) shows only the methods left after the rules: here the cart weighs 3 kg and the customer has the tag Provincie, so the pallet transport (from 50 kg) and the local courier (hidden for Provincie) are missing.

  1. Payment step with the courier — the Payment method list (1) shows only the providers allowed with the chosen delivery method. The customer sees the payment method name (Card, Cash on Delivery), not the provider name from the backend.

  1. Payment step with pickup — with the pickup method only Wire Transfer is left, the only provider allowed on it.

  1. Above the value limit — with 10 items the order total, taxes included (2,317.15 lei), is above the 1,500 lei limit of Cash on Delivery, so only Card is left.

Standard rules that still apply

  • Cash on Delivery is offered only by the delivery methods with the standard Cash on Delivery option, and, when the list is filled in, only if it is in Payments Provider Allowed.
  • The standard Maximum Amount of a provider and Max Weight of a delivery method are applied too, next to the rules of this module.
  • If the rules leave no delivery method, the shop shows "Sorry, we are unable to ship your order."; if they leave no payment provider, the payment step shows No payment method available. Keep one delivery method and one provider without restrictions as a fallback.

The Logo of the delivery method and the Payment Provider of the contact are not used in the checkout.
