Features:

- restrict the payment providers offered in the eCommerce checkout by the selected
  delivery method (the payment step shows only the providers allowed on the delivery
  method)
- hide a payment provider when the order total exceeds its value limit
- restrict payment providers for partners with certain labels (labels of the
  commercial partner, so the contacts of a labelled company are restricted too)
- check the payment provider again when the transaction is created, including the
  express checkout: a provider that is not allowed for the order is refused
- restrict delivery methods for partners with certain labels
- minimum and maximum order weight on delivery methods
- preselect the partner's preferred payment provider on the payment step
- show the logo of the delivery method in the shop checkout
