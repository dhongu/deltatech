1. In *Inventory > Reporting > Valuation*, select the valuation layers to close and archive them
   (*Actions > Archive*), after checking the effect on the valuation of the products concerned.
2. Open the storage sheet of the Romanian localization (*Inventory > Reporting*) and tick **Only
   active** to leave the archived layers out; leave it unticked for the full report.
3. To bring a layer back, show the archived layers in the valuation list and unarchive it.
