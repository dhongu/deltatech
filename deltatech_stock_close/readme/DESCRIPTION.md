Stock Closing Operations
========================

This module provides tools for managing stock closing operations at a specific date.
It helps maintain inventory accuracy by allowing users to deactivate valuation layers that are no longer relevant after a certain period or when closing the fiscal year.

Key Features
------------

- Ability to close stock operations as of a given date.
- Adds an "Active" field in the stock valuation layer for better visibility.
- Improves reporting performance by filtering out old or closed stock layers.
- Integration with Romanian localization stock reports.

Usage
-----

- Go to Inventory > Reporting > Valuation.
- Select the valuation layers to be closed or deactivated.
- Mark the records as closed according to your business needs.
