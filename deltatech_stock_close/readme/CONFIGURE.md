1. Install the module; it needs the storage sheet of the Romanian localization
   (`l10n_ro_stock_report`).
2. Decide which stock moves count as closed (for instance every move of a closed fiscal year) and
   close them with *Inventory > Operations > Adjustments > Close Stock Moves*: choose the last day of
   the closed period and press **Close**. The wizard is for stock managers.
3. To reopen a move, show the **Valuation Active** column of the stock move list
   (*Inventory > Reporting > Moves Analysis*, **Closed** filter) and tick it again.
