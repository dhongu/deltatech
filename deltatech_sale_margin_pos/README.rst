Adds the Point of Sale orders that were not invoiced to the sale margin
report (*Raport analiză profit*) of ``deltatech_sale_margin``. The
invoiced orders are already in the report through their invoice, so they
are not counted twice.

For each order line the report shows:

- the sale value without VAT, in the company currency; returns are
  negative;
- the cost of the line, as computed by the Point of Sale when the
  session is closed, or the current product cost while the session is
  still open;
- the salesperson of the order and the sales journal of the point of
  sale; with ``deltatech_sale_commission``, the commission rates set on
  that journal give the computed commission;
- the order itself, with a *POS Sales* filter and a grouping by order.

The rows of the orders are read-only: the real commission, *Commission
Paid* and the purchase price are kept on the invoice lines only, and the
commission and cost wizards leave the orders out.

The module is not installed automatically: it changes the figures of the
report.

Usage
=====

Install the module on demand: it is not installed automatically, because
it changes the figures of the profit report.

**Step 1 — Sell at the Point of Sale**

Sell and take the payment as usual. In **Point of Sale → Orders →
Orders**, the order is *Paid* (or *Posted* after the session is closed),
without an invoice. Nothing else is needed: its lines go to the profit
report on their own.

|Paid Point of Sale order without invoice|

**Step 2 — See the POS sales in the profit report**

Go to **Sales → Reporting → Profit Analysis** and group the pivot by
**Journal**, then by **Product**. The invoices are under the sales
journal, the uninvoiced POS orders under the journal of the point of
sale, with sale value (without VAT), cost and profit. An invoiced POS
order appears only once, through its invoice. A return has negative
quantity and values.

While the session is open, the cost is the current product cost; once
the session is closed, it is the cost computed by the Point of Sale from
the delivery.

|Profit report grouped by journal: invoices and POS|

**Step 3 — Only the POS sales**

In the list view, remove the default groupings and the date filter, and
choose the **POS Sales** filter (**Invoices** shows only the invoice
lines). The **POS Order** column shows the order of each row; group by
**POS Order** to sum the lines per order. The POS rows are read-only.

With ``deltatech_sale_commission``, the POS rows also get the computed
commission of the salesperson of the order, from the rates set on the
**invoice journal** of the point of sale.

|POS sales in the profit report|

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-22)
-----------------------

- Point of Sale orders without an invoice in the sale margin report,
  with the cost computed at the closing of the session and, with the
