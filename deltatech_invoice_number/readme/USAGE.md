1. Go to **Accounting > Configuration > Journals**, open a Sales journal, and on the **Restrict invoice date** field (visible for Sales-type journals) tick the box to enforce chronological numbering: once enabled, an invoice cannot be validated with a date earlier than the last posted invoice's date on that journal.
2. Still on the journal form, optionally set a **Journal Sequence** — a dedicated `ir.sequence` used to allocate an invoice number even while the invoice is still in draft state. Once a number has been allocated this way, the invoice date can no longer be changed.
3. To modify the number of an already-posted invoice, open the invoice and use the **Change invoice number** action (available from the invoice form's action/gear menu). This opens a wizard where you enter the new **Invoice Number** and click **Apply**; the invoice number and the corresponding references on its journal items are updated directly.
4. The **Change invoice number** action is only available to users in the **Allow to change invoice number** security group (`deltatech_invoice_number.group_change_invoice_number`) — grant this group to the users who should be allowed to renumber invoices; by default it is meant for users who already belong to the Accounting & Finance / Billing group (`account.group_account_invoice`).
