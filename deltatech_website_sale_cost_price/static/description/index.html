Overview

This module extends the standard eCommerce option Prevent Sale of Zero Priced Product: the shop also stops selling a product when its price is lower than its cost price plus a minimum margin.

For such a product the customer no longer sees the price and the Add to Cart button, but the Contact Us button (the same behaviour as for zero priced products), and the cart button disappears from the product list.

Key features:

  • Cost price protection
    the website price (after the website pricelist) is compared with the product cost (Cost field).
  • Minimum margin
    a margin percentage set on the website (with 10%, the price must be at least 110% of the cost).
  • Tax aware
    the cost (kept without taxes) is increased with the taxes when the website displays prices with taxes.
  • Currency conversion
    the cost is converted to the website currency.

Usage

  1. Go to Website > Configuration > Settings, section eCommerce. Check Prevent Sale of Zero Priced Product: the module fields appear next to it. Leave Cost Price Includes Tax unchecked when the product cost is kept without taxes (on a website showing prices without taxes this option does not remove the tax from the cost: keep the cost without taxes and leave it unchecked), set Cost Price Margin % (e.g. 10) and save.

  1. On the product (Website > eCommerce > Products, tab General Information), the Sales Price is compared with the Cost plus the margin. Here 42.00 is below 40.00 + 10% = 44.00, so the product cannot be bought online. The compared price is the website price, after the website pricelist.

  1. In the shop, the product below the threshold has no cart button in the product list, while the other one is sold as usual.

  1. On its product page, the price and Add to Cart are replaced by the Contact Us button (the Button URL from the settings).

  1. A product above the threshold shows its price and Add to Cart as usual.

Check the shop as a visitor (private window): users of the Settings group can still add a blocked product to the cart.
