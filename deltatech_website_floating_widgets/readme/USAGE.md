1. Go to **Website > Site > Floating Widgets** (menu added under Website configuration).
2. Click **New** to create a widget and fill in:
   - **Name**: internal label for the widget.
   - **Icon**: pick from the predefined list (Phone, Email, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.). A live preview of the icon is shown next to the field.
   - **Type**: `URL`, `Phone` or `Email` — this determines how the **Link** field is turned into a clickable link (`tel:`, `mailto:` or plain URL).
   - **Link**: the destination — a phone number, an email address, or a full URL. For URLs, always include the protocol (e.g. `https://`), otherwise the link is treated as relative to your own website.
   - **Open in new tab**: check to open the link in a new browser tab.
   - **Button Shape**: Circle, Square or Rounded.
   - **Background Color** / **Text Color**: pick the widget's colors.
   - **Display on Mobile** / **Display on Desktop**: control on which device types the widget is visible.
   - **Sequence**: drag rows in the list view to reorder the widgets.
   - **Active**: uncheck to hide a widget without deleting it.
3. Save. Active widgets appear as floating buttons stacked on the right side of every page of the website.
