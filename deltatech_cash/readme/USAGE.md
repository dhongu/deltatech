1.  Navigate to **Accounting > Dashboards**.
2.  Locate a **Cash** journal.
3.  Use the **Cash In** or **Cash Out** actions (if available in the journal's menu or dashboard).
4.  In the wizard that appears, enter the **Amount**, **Description**, and select the appropriate **Counterpart Account**.
5.  Confirm the operation to create the accounting entry and update the cash balance.
