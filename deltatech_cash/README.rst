Cash In / Out Management
========================

This module provides a streamlined way to manage cash entries (In and
Out) directly from Odoo's accounting interface. It's designed to help
businesses record quick cash movements that don't necessarily originate
from a point of sale, ensuring better control over physical cash boxes.

Key Features
============

1. **Direct Cash In/Out Operations**:

   - Adds intuitive wizards for recording cash infusions or withdrawals.
   - Allows users to specify the reason and amount for each cash
     movement.

2. **Accounting Integration**:

   - Automatically generates the corresponding journal entries in the
     designated cash journal.
   - Ensures that the cash balance is updated in real-time, reflecting
     the physical state of the cash box.

3. **Flexible Counterpart Accounts**:

   - Supports selecting specific counterpart accounts for different
     types of cash operations, providing better financial
     categorization.

Usage
=====

1. Navigate to **Accounting > Dashboards**.
2. Locate a **Cash** journal.
3. Use the **Cash In** or **Cash Out** actions (if available in the
   journal's menu or dashboard).
4. In the wizard that appears, enter the **Amount**, **Description**,
   and select the appropriate **Counterpart Account**.
5. Confirm the operation to create the accounting entry and update the
   cash balance.
