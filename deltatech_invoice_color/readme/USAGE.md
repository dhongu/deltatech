1. Open any customer invoice or vendor bill (**Accounting > Customers > Invoices** or **Accounting > Vendors > Bills**).
2. In the invoice lines list, lines are automatically color-coded:
   - **Red (danger)** — the line has a storable product but is not linked to any Sales Order line (for customer invoices) or Purchase Order line (for vendor bills).
   - **Yellow (warning)** — the line has no product or no unit of measure selected.
3. When at least one line is flagged, a red banner "Careful! Invoice integration requires user intervention!" appears at the top of the invoice/bill form to draw attention before validating it.
