## 19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-17)

- "Restrict done quantities to reserved" now compares the done quantity of each move
  with its demand, in the same unit of measure: a line in dozens no longer blocks a
  correct validation, and a changed quantity is now refused.
- "Restrict new products" now refuses any product added after the transfer was
  confirmed or without demand, whatever its quantity.
