Products sold in bulk, such as bricks or tiles, are delivered on pallets
that the customer pays for. The ``deltatech_sale_pallet`` module adds
the pallet lines on backend sales orders; this module brings the same
logic to the eCommerce shop, so the pallets are shown on the product
page and added to the cart automatically.

**Pallet price on the product page.** For products with a minimum pallet
quantity, the product page shows a line such as *When buying 10 units,
the price is ...*, where the price is the minimum pallet quantity at the
sales price plus the price of the pallet product.

**Pallet lines in the cart.** Each time a product is added to the cart
or its quantity is changed, the pallet lines are recalculated: one line
per pallet product, with the number of full pallets needed by all the
cart lines that use that pallet. A quantity below one full pallet adds
no pallet. When the last product that needs a pallet is removed from the
cart, its pallet line is removed too.

**Delivery price includes the pallets.** The pallet lines are updated
before the delivery price and the cart quantity are recalculated, so
both take the pallets into account.

Configuration
-------------

On the product form, in the *Inventory* tab, the **Palletizing** section
(from ``deltatech_sale_pallet``) holds the settings used by the shop:

- **Pallet Product**: the product that represents the pallet, sold at
  its own sales price;
- **Pallet Qty Min**: the quantity that fills one pallet.

Products without a pallet product and a minimum quantity are sold in the
shop as usual. The **Pallet** flag on product categories is not used by
the shop.

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.4 (2026-11-03)
-----------------------

- Description and banner rewritten from the code: the shop follows the
  *Pallet Product* and *Pallet Qty Min* fields of the product, not the
  *Pallet* flag of the product category; removed the checkout rules that
  the module does not have; documented how the cart counts full pallets.

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-22)
-----------------------

- Listed on Odoo Apps as *Pallet Sales on Website* (was *Sale Pallet
  Website*). The technical name ``deltatech_sale_pallet_website`` does
  not change.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-16)
-----------------------

- The pallet lines of the shop cart are now updated before the delivery
  price and the cart quantity are recalculated, so both include them.
- Removing the last product that needs a pallet from the cart also
  removes its pallet line.
- Pallet lines added to the cart are marked as generated pallet lines
  (``is_pallet_line``), like the ones on the sales order.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
