Overview

Receipts against a purchase order and deliveries by piece or by pack, from the Point of Sale session.

Key features:

  • Burger-menu entries Receipt and Delivery, enabled per point of sale.
  • Receipts only against a purchase order:
    pick a confirmed order of the register's warehouse, adjust the quantity received and validate; what did not arrive stays as a backorder. There is no free receipt.
  • Deliveries:
    scan a product barcode, a pack barcode (a unit of measure linked to the product) or search by name; optional partner from the POS partner list.
  • Creates and validates a standard transfer; if Odoo asks for a manual step, the transfer is left in Inventory and the cashier is told.
  • Journal of POS stock operations under Point of Sale, with who did what, in which session and for which purchase order.
  • Only drives the standard stock flow: accounting entries come from the installed localization.
Usage

Set up

  1. Go to Point of Sale ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings, choose the register and, in the Stock Operations block, tick Receipts from the POS and/or Deliveries from the POS.
  2. Check Receipt Operation Type and Delivery Operation Type; they are proposed from the register's warehouse.
  3. Give each cashier who may use it the POS stock operations right (Settings ‣ Users).
1 Stock Operations settings block with the two options and the operation types

Deliver from the register

  1. Open the register menu: Receipt and Delivery appear after Reload Data.
2 Register menu with the Receipt and Delivery entries
  1. Choose Delivery, scan a product (or a pack barcode) or type part of its name and pick it from the list.
  2. Adjust the quantity, optionally choose a Partner, and press Validate.
3 Delivery dialog with one product line

The delivery transfer is created and validated, and the stock drops.

4 Notification confirming the delivery transfer was validated

Receive against a purchase order

There is no free receipt: goods are received only against a confirmed purchase order of the register's warehouse.

  1. Choose Receipt in the register menu. The dialog lists the purchase orders still to receive; search by order number, supplier or transfer.
5 Receipt dialog listing the purchase orders to receive
  1. Pick the order, then type the quantity that actually arrived on each line (0 leaves a line out). Scanning a product barcode sets its quantity to 1 and then counts up.
  2. Press Validate. What did not arrive stays on the same purchase order as a backorder.
6 Receipt dialog with the received quantity typed on each line

Review the operations

Point of Sale ‣ Orders ‣ Stock Operations lists every operation with its transfer, partner, purchase order (receipts), register, user and state. Needs confirmation means Odoo asked for a manual step: finish that transfer in Inventory.

7 List of POS stock operations with their transfers and states
