Receipts and deliveries made from the Point of Sale session, by piece or by pack.

Key features:

- Burger-menu entries *Receipt* and *Delivery*, enabled per point of sale.
- Scan a product barcode, or a pack barcode (a unit of measure linked to the product), or search by name.
- Optional partner, taken from the POS partner list.
- Creates and validates a standard transfer; if Odoo asks for a manual step, the transfer is left in Inventory and the cashier is told.
- Journal of POS stock operations under Point of Sale, with who did what and in which session.
- Only drives the standard stock flow: accounting entries come from the installed localization.
