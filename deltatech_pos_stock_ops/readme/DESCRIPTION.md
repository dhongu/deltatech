Receipts and deliveries made from the Point of Sale session, by piece or by pack.

Key features:

- Burger-menu entries *Receipt* and *Delivery*, enabled per point of sale.
- **Receipts only against a purchase order:** pick a confirmed order of the register's warehouse, adjust the quantity received and validate; what did not arrive stays as a backorder. There is no free receipt.
- **Deliveries:** scan a product barcode, a pack barcode (a unit of measure linked to the product) or search by name; optional partner from the POS partner list.
- Creates and validates a standard transfer; if Odoo asks for a manual step, the transfer is left in Inventory and the cashier is told.
- Journal of POS stock operations under Point of Sale, with who did what, in which session and for which purchase order.
- Only drives the standard stock flow: accounting entries come from the installed localization.
