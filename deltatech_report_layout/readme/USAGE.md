1. Go to **Settings > General Settings** and, in the **Companies** section, click
   **Configure Document Layout**.
2. Under **Layout**, the eight new layouts (**Grid**, **Inline**, **Band**, **Modern**, **Title Box**, **Dense**, **Minimal** and **Accent**) are listed after the standard
   ones. Select **Grid**: the preview shows a sample invoice with the logo on the left and the
   company address aligned to the right.
   The **Tables** row (Light, Boxed, Bold...) does not change Grid and Inline: each of them has its
   own table style, so that choice only applies to the standard layouts.

   ![Configure your document layout dialog with Grid selected under Layout, next to the standard layouts and the other new layouts, and the invoice preview on the right](static/description/en/01_macheta_style1.png)

3. Select **Inline**: the company address sits next to the logo, and the table header and the total
   use the primary color of the company. Change the colors under **Colors** if needed, then click
   **Continue** to save.

   ![Configure your document layout dialog with Inline selected; the table header and the Total row use the primary color](static/description/en/02_macheta_style2.png)

4. Select **Band**: the document title and the company name sit on a full-width band in the
   primary color, the table header has a colored line and the footer is a colored band too.

   ![Configure your document layout dialog with Band selected; the invoice preview has the title on a purple band and a purple footer band](static/description/en/05_macheta_style3.png)

5. The other layouts differ in header, footer and table style:

   | Layout | What it looks like |
   |---|---|
   | **Modern** | Logo on the left, colored title and company details on the right, colored table header |
   | **Title Box** | Title in a box in the middle, logo on the left, company details on the right |
   | **Dense** | Small font, tight margins and a one-line header, for documents with many lines |
   | **Minimal** | Black and white, no colored backgrounds, cheap to print (ignores the company colors) |
   | **Accent** | Colored vertical bar along the left edge of the content, colored title |

   ![Invoice preview with Modern: logo on the left, title and company address on the right, teal table header](static/description/en/07_document_style4.png)

   ![Invoice preview with Title Box: title in a box between the logo and the company address](static/description/en/08_document_style5.png)

   ![Invoice preview with Dense: one-line header, small font and tight margins](static/description/en/09_document_style6.png)

   ![Invoice preview with Minimal: black and white, company address on the left, logo and title on the right](static/description/en/10_document_style7.png)

   ![Invoice preview with Accent: teal vertical bar along the left edge of the content](static/description/en/11_document_style8.png)

6. Print any document of the company (quotation, invoice, delivery slip). It uses the selected
   layout:

   ![Header and footer of a document printed with Grid: logo on the left, company address on the right](static/description/en/03_document_style1.png)

   ![Header and footer of a document printed with Inline: company address next to the logo, centered footer](static/description/en/04_document_style2.png)
