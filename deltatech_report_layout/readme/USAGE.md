1. Go to **Settings > General Settings** and, in the **Companies** section, click
   **Configure Document Layout**.
2. Under **Layout**, the two new layouts, **Style1** and **Style2**, are listed below the standard
   ones. Select **Style1**: the preview shows a sample invoice with the logo on the left and the
   company address aligned to the right.

   ![Configure your document layout dialog with Style1 selected under Layout and the invoice preview on the right](static/description/en/01_macheta_style1.png)

3. Select **Style2**: the company address sits next to the logo, and the table header and the total
   use the primary color of the company. Change the colors under **Colors** if needed, then click
   **Continue** to save.

   ![Configure your document layout dialog with Style2 selected; the table header and the Total row use the primary color](static/description/en/02_macheta_style2.png)

4. Print any document of the company (quotation, invoice, delivery slip). It uses the selected
   layout:

   ![Header and footer of a document printed with Style1: logo on the left, company address on the right](static/description/en/03_document_style1.png)

   ![Header and footer of a document printed with Style2: company address next to the logo, centered footer](static/description/en/04_document_style2.png)
