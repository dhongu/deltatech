Overview

This module sends your customers automatic e-mail reminders for their invoices: a few days before the due date, on the due date, or some days after it. For each reminder step (a followup) you choose the reference date, the offset in days and the mail template, and the e-mail can list the invoices concerned together with the customer's totals.

✨ Key features:

  • Reminder steps you configure yourself
    the date used (invoice date or due date), the offset in days (-2 means two days before, 3 three days after), and whether only that exact day or every day from then on is processed.
  • Open invoices only
    credit notes are optional, and totals are shown in the company currency or in the customer currency.
  • Your own mail template
    set on the Contact, with the invoices inserted where you place [invoices], one row per invoice (number, dates, payment term, amounts), plus the total to pay, the customer's total debit and the amount already overdue.
  • Only the customers you flag
    those with Send followup e-mails ticked receive reminders.
  • Amount margin
    nothing is sent when the invoices in the e-mail add up to less than the margin (or to a negative amount, when credit notes exceed the invoices), and a note on the customer says why.
  • Amounts and dates in the customer's language
    credit notes are shown negative and subtracted from the total.
  • Errors stay local
    if one customer cannot be processed (for example an unknown placeholder), the error is logged and noted on that customer, and all the other customers are still processed.
  • Run from a scheduled action
    for all the followups or only for those with given codes, so different steps can run at different times.

Configuration

Scheduled action

  1. Activate the developer mode and go to Settings > Technical > Automation > Scheduled Actions.
  2. Create an action on the model Followup Send Wizard (followup.send.wizard), with the frequency you want (usually daily), and this Python code:
    • for all the followups: model.run_followup()
    • for selected followups only: model.run_followup(["f-1", "AZI"]), where the values are the Code of the followups to run.
  3. The user of the scheduled action needs the Followup manager group.

Mail template

Create a mail template on the model Contact. In its body you can use ${object.name}, $total_debit, $total_all_debit, $total_due_debit, $currency and [invoices]. By default the template deletes the mail after it is sent (Auto Delete); untick it if you want to keep a trace in Settings > Technical > Email > Emails.

Testing

To test without writing to customers, create the system parameter followup.override_partner_id (Settings > Technical > Parameters > System Parameters) with the id of a contact: the e-mails go to that contact instead of the customers. Remove the parameter before going live.

Usage

Choosing the customers to remind

  1. Open the customer (Invoicing > Customers > Customers).
  2. Tick Send followup e-mails, under the Tags field. The box must be on the same contact that appears on the invoice, not on its parent company.

Defining a followup

  1. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Followups and click New. You need accounting manager rights and the Followup manager group.
  2. Fill in the Name and the Code, then choose the Date field to use (invoice date or due date), the Comparator and the Days from:
    • Equal processes only the invoices whose date plus the offset is today; a day on which the scheduled action does not run is lost.
    • Greater or equal processes every invoice from that day on, so the reminder is repeated daily.
  3. Choose the Mail template (required) and, under Invoices placeholder, the HTML of one invoice row, with $number, $payment_term_id, $date_invoice, $date_due, $amount_untaxed, $amount_tax, $amount_total, $amount_due and $currency.
  4. Set the options: Only open invoices, Parse refund invoices, Use customer currency and the Amount margin.

What happens when it runs

For each active followup and each flagged customer, the module takes the posted invoices that match the followup, adds up the totals and sends the e-mail built from the template. Amounts and dates follow the language of the customer.

  • If the invoices in the e-mail add up to the margin or less, or to a negative amount (credit notes over invoices), no e-mail is sent and a note is posted on the customer.
  • If a customer cannot be processed, a note says that the followup failed and why; the other customers are processed normally.
  • The e-mails appear in Settings > Technical > Email > Emails until they are sent; with Auto Delete on the template they are removed afterwards.

A reminder does not replace a formal notice of non-payment.

Versions

19.0.0.1.4 (2026-10-29)

  • Fix: the amount margin is now compared with the total of the invoices included in the followup, not with the partner's overdue debit. Followups sent before or on the due date were skipped for partners without other overdue invoices.
  • Fix: credit notes included in a followup are now subtracted from the total to pay (they were added) and shown with a negative sign; the total uses the same currency as the $currency placeholder.
  • Amounts in the e-mail are formatted in the language of the partner.
  • The "Followup not sent" note is translatable and uses the currency format of the followup totals.
  • Fix: $payment_term_id shows the name of the payment term instead of the technical recordset text.
  • Fix: a followup whose invoices total a negative amount (credit notes over invoices) is no longer sent.
  • Fix: a partner without language or an error on one partner (invoice without due date, unknown placeholder) no longer stops the whole run; the error is logged and noted on the partner.
  • The mail template is required on the followup form (a followup without template failed for every partner).
  • Followup form: options moved to the right column; list shows the comparator and the mail template; help texts and Romanian translation updated.

19.0.0.1.3 (2026-10-04)

  • Add unit tests covering followup date matching, residual amount computation and the followup mail sending wizard (codes filter, margin skip, refunds, customer currency, override partner).

  • New module icon in the flat style of the other modules; it replaces the old one.
