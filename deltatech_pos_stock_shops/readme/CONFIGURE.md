1. Go to *Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings* and pick the point of sale.
2. In the *Product Stock* block, tick *Show stock*, then *Show the stock of the other shops*.
3. Optionally choose the *Shops*. Left empty, the warehouses of the other points of sale of the
   company are used. The warehouse of the register itself is never listed as another shop.
4. Reopen the POS session so the stock of the other shops is loaded.
