1. Open the POS. Products that the other shops have show a second, light blue figure on the badge:
   the total of the other shops (`+12`).
2. Use the buttons above the product list:
   - *In stock here* keeps the products this shop can sell right away;
   - *In other shops* keeps the products at least one other shop has, including those that are
     out of stock here;
   - tap the active button again, or *All products*, to take the filter off.
3. Tap the info button of a product to see the quantity per shop. Without network, a smaller
   popup opens with the figures loaded with the session.
