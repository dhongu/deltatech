"Do you have it in the other shop?" — answered at the till, without
calling the other shop.

This module extends ``deltatech_pos_stock`` with the stock of the
**other shops** of the company, loaded with the POS session.

- **On the product card**: under the quantity of this shop, the badge
  shows how much the other shops have together (``+12``). Negative stock
  in one shop does not reduce what another shop can hand over.
- **Filter in the product list**: *All products*, *In stock here*, *In
  other shops*. A second tap on the active filter takes it off. The
  filter stays reachable when the list is empty.
- **Product info popup**: the standard popup lists every warehouse of
  the company. It is limited to this shop and the chosen shops, so the
  central warehouse or a transit location does not show up as something
  the cashier can hand over.
- **Works offline**: the figures travel with the session. When the
  server cannot be reached, the product info button opens a popup with
  the stock per shop, built from the session data.
- **Kept up to date**: a sale, a receipt or a transfer in another shop
  is pushed to the open registers on the channel already used by
  ``deltatech_pos_stock``.
- **One query**: the stock of all the shops is read with a single
  grouped query on the quants, not one query per shop.
- **Same figure as the badge**: on hand or available (on hand minus
  reservations), following the *Quantity shown* setting of
  ``deltatech_pos_stock``.

Use Cases / Context
===================

Part of the Terrabit POS Ecosystem
----------------------------------

- **deltatech_pos_stock** — the stock and price badge on the product
  card; this module adds the other shops to it and reuses its live-bus
  channel
- **deltatech_pos_price_sync** — pushes live price changes to open POS
  sessions
- **deltatech_pos** / **deltatech_pos_base** — fiscal receipts on
  Romanian cash registers

Standard Odoo 19 already lists the stock per warehouse in the product
info popup, but only online, one product at a time and with every
warehouse of the company. This module puts the figure on the card and in
a filter, limits it to the shops, and keeps it available offline.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings* and pick the point
   of sale.
2. In the *Product Stock* block, tick *Show stock*, then *Show the stock
   of the other shops*.
3. Optionally choose the *Shops*. Left empty, the warehouses of the
   other points of sale of the company are used. The warehouse of the
   register itself is never listed as another shop.
4. Reopen the POS session so the stock of the other shops is loaded.

Usage
=====

1. Open the POS. Products that the other shops have show a second, light
   blue figure on the badge: the total of the other shops (``+12``).
2. Use the buttons above the product list:

   - *In stock here* keeps the products this shop can sell right away;
   - *In other shops* keeps the products at least one other shop has,
     including those that are out of stock here;
   - tap the active button again, or *All products*, to take the filter
     off.

3. Tap the info button of a product to see the quantity per shop.
   Without network, a smaller popup opens with the figures loaded with
   the session.
