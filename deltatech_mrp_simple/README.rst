Records a simple production without a bill of materials: you list the
components consumed and the products obtained, and the module moves the
stock and values the products obtained at the cost of the components.

**Simple production document.** In **Inventory > Operations > Simple
Production**, a document lists the products to consume (*Consume*) and
the products obtained (*Receipt*), with their quantities and units.
**Add lines** adds several components at once, with the same quantity.

**Two transfers.** **Confirm** creates a consumption transfer and a
receipt transfer, with the operation types chosen on the document
(*Picking type consume* and *Picking type receipt*). Each is validated
automatically when its option (*Validation consume*, *Validation
receipt*) is ticked, otherwise it stays to be validated by hand. Both
transfers open from the **Consume** and **Receipt** smart buttons. A
document can be confirmed only once.

**Cost of the products obtained.** The cost of the consumed components
(their cost price times quantity) is split over the products obtained,
in proportion to their standard value (cost price times quantity), or to
their quantity when none has a cost price. **Recompute prices** redoes
the split; the *Unit Price* of a product obtained can also be changed by
hand. This price is set on the receipt moves.

**Zero cost check.** A product obtained with a unit price of 0 blocks
the confirmation, unless the system parameter
``deltatech_mrp_simple.allow_zero_cost`` is set to ``1`` (**Settings >
Technical > System Parameters**, developer mode).

**Product and sale order for the result.** Users in the group *Sale
simple production* see the option *Create product & sale*. When it is
ticked, **Confirm** also:

- creates the final product (storable, unpublished) from *Final product
  name*, *Category for final product* and *Unit of Measure*, or updates
  the selected *Final Product*. Its cost is the cost of the components
  and its sales price the sum of their sales prices, both per unit of
  the final quantity;
- adds it as a product obtained;
- creates a quotation for the *Client*, with the client's payment terms
  and pricelist.

**Link with the sale order.** When a *Sale Order* is set on the
document, or created by it, the order shows the **MRP Simple** smart
button, which opens the simple production.

On installation, the administrator is the only member of the group *Sale
simple production*; add other users in **Settings > Users & Companies >
Groups**.

Limitations
-----------

- **Add lines** stays available after confirmation, although the lines
  of a confirmed document no longer change the transfers.
- In Odoo 20 the receipt is valued by the standard stock valuation (at
  the current cost of the product), not at the *Unit Price* computed by
  the document.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.1.4 (2026-11-03)
-----------------------

- Description rewritten from the code: the zero-cost system parameter is
  ``deltatech_mrp_simple.allow_zero_cost`` (it was given as
  ``simple_production_allow_zero_cost``); added the menu, the buttons,
  the validation options and the link with the sale order.
- Limitation noted: on 20.0 the receipt is valued by the standard stock
  valuation, not at the computed *Unit Price*.

20.0.1.1.3 (2026-10-08)
-----------------------

- Fixed SIMPLE-001: with several received products, each one was valued
  at the whole consumed cost, so the production was valued at a multiple
  of what was consumed. The consumed cost is now split once over the
  received products, in proportion to their standard value (standard
  price x quantity), or to their quantity when none has a standard
  price; the last line takes the rounding difference. A received line
  without quantity no longer causes a division by zero.
- Fixed SIMPLE-002: *Confirm* could be run again on a done simple
  production through RPC or a retried request, creating new consumption
  and receipt transfers and moving the stock a second time. A simple
  production can now be confirmed only once.

20.0.1.1.2 (2026-10-01)
-----------------------

- Migration to Odoo 20.0.

19.0.1.1.2 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
