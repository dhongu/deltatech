Keeps stock and invoicing in step: storable products are invoiced only
from a sale order and billed only from a purchase order, so every
invoiced storable line has an order behind it, with its delivery or
receipt. Set the invoicing policy to *Delivered quantities* to invoice
only what was delivered.

- **Customer invoices**: a customer invoice, credit note or sales
  receipt with a storable product that does not come from a sale order
  cannot be posted.
- **Vendor bills**: a vendor bill, refund or purchase receipt with a
  storable product that does not come from a purchase order cannot be
  posted.
- **Every way of posting**: the *Confirm* button, the mass confirmation
  from the list and the scheduled auto-posting are all checked. Point of
  Sale invoices are not checked.
- **Services pass**: services (transport, labour etc.) and goods without
  *Track Inventory* are not checked.
- **Credit notes from an order**: a credit note or refund reversing an
  invoice issued from an order keeps the link to the order and is posted
  normally.
- **Per company**: the two checks are enabled separately, in *Invoicing
  / Accounting → Configuration → Settings*, in the *Customer Invoices*
  and *Vendor Bills* sections.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Invoicing → Configuration → Settings**. In the **Customer
   Invoices** section, check **Customer invoices for storable products
   only from sale orders** and click **Save**. The option is saved on
   the current company.

   |Invoicing settings, Customer Invoices section: the option Customer
   invoices for storable products only from sale orders is checked and
   highlighted|

2. If all purchases of goods go through purchase orders, check also
   **Vendor bills for storable products only from purchase orders**, in
   the **Vendor Bills** section.

   |Invoicing settings, Vendor Bills section: the option Vendor bills
   for storable products only from purchase orders is checked and
   highlighted|

3. A customer invoice or credit note with a storable product that does
   not come from a sale order can no longer be confirmed: **Confirm**
   shows the products without a sale order. Create the sale order,
   deliver the goods and invoice from the order. Sales receipts and the
   confirmation from the list (**Actions → Confirm Entries**) are
   checked too. A credit note created from an invoice issued from an
   order keeps the link and is confirmed normally. Lines with services
   are not checked. To invoice only what was delivered, set the product
   **Invoicing Policy** to **Delivered quantities**.

   |Draft customer invoice for Client Demo SRL with the storable product
   Marfă depozit, and the Invalid Operation dialog: storable products
   can be invoiced only from a sale order|

4. In the same way, a vendor bill or refund with a storable product that
   does not come from a purchase order cannot be confirmed. Fill the
   bill from the purchase order (**Auto-Complete** field) or create the
   purchase order and the receipt first.

   |Draft vendor bill from Furnizor Demo SRL with the storable product
   Marfă depozit, and the Invalid Operation dialog: storable products
   can be billed only from a purchase order|
