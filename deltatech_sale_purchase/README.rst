When a sales order with make-to-order products generates a request for
quotation through the procurement rules, cancelling the sales order in
standard Odoo leaves the purchase lines in place. The buyer then has to
find and delete them by hand, or the products are ordered anyway.

**Purchase lines removed when the sales order is cancelled**: on
**Cancel** of a sales order, the purchase lines created for its stock
moves are deleted, as long as their purchase order is still an **RFQ**
(draft). A purchase order that was already sent or confirmed is left to
the buyer.

**Shared purchase lines reduced, not deleted**: when the vendor groups
RFQs (**Group RFQ** set to *Daily*, *Weekly* or *Always* on the vendor's
**Sales & Purchase** tab), one purchase line can supply several sales
orders. In that case the line is kept, the cancelled moves are detached
from it and their quantity, converted to the purchase unit of measure,
is subtracted from the line.

There is nothing to configure: the behaviour is active once the module
is installed.

Limitations
-----------

- Only the cancellation of the whole sales order is handled. Lowering
  the quantity on a confirmed sales order line does not change the
  purchase line.
- Purchase orders in **RFQ Sent** or **Purchase Order** state are not
  changed.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.0.4 (2026-11-03)
-----------------------

- Description and banner rewritten after the code: removed the claim
  that decreases of the ordered quantity update the purchase lines (only
  the cancellation of the whole sales order is handled); documented the
  reduction of purchase lines shared by several sales orders.

20.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-22)
-----------------------

- Listed on Odoo Apps as *Sale-Purchase Sync* (was *Sale Purchase*). The
  technical name ``deltatech_sale_purchase`` does not change.

20.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-02)
-----------------------

- Migration to 20.0: the quantity of the cancelled moves is converted
  with the new ``uom_id`` fields of ``stock.move`` and
  ``purchase.order.line``.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-01)
-----------------------

- Cancelling a sale order no longer deletes a draft purchase line that
  also supplies another sale order. With a vendor grouping RFQs Daily,
  Weekly or Always, the needs of several sale orders are merged on one
  line; now only the cancelled moves are detached from it and their
  quantity (converted to the purchase unit of measure) is subtracted. A
  line left without any active sale order is removed as before.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-07-29)
-----------------------

- Port of the module from 18.0. Cancelling a sale order still removes
  the purchase lines it generated, as long as their purchase order is
  still a draft.
- ``sale.order._action_cancel`` now works on the whole recordset of
  generated purchase lines instead of assuming a single one, so an order
  whose moves feed several purchase lines no longer raises a singleton
  error.
- Dropped the ``stock.rule._make_po_get_domain`` override that
  neutralized the procurement group: ``group_propagation_option`` no
  longer exists on 19.0, where the grouping of replenishment needs is
  driven by ``res.partner.group_rfq`` instead. Set that field to
  ``Always`` to collect the needs of several sale orders into a single
  draft purchase order.
- Dropped the ``_log_decrease_ordered_quantity`` override as well. On
  19.0 core propagates a decrease of the ordered quantity to the draft
  purchase line by itself and logs no exception activity, so the
  override had no effect left. A test guards that core behaviour, so the
  override can be restored if it ever changes.
- Replaced the tests: the previous ones asserted nothing and exercised
  the removed override.
