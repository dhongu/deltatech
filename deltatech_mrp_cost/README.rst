Labor, utilities and overhead are production costs that components do
not show. This module records them on the bill of materials and the
manufacturing order and adds them to the order cost.

- **Costs tab**: A *Costs* tab on the bill of materials and the
  manufacturing order holds *Overhead*, *Utility consumption*, *Net
  Salary Rate*, *Salary Contributions* and a *Duration*. A new order
  copies *Overhead*, *Utility consumption*, *Net Salary Rate* and
  *Salary Contributions* from its bill of materials.
- **Order cost**: *Overhead* is a fixed amount; utilities, net salary
  and contributions are rates multiplied by the *Duration*. *Production
  Amount* and *Calculate Price* show the total and unit cost: storable
  components at standard price plus these costs, or, once produced, the
  stock valuation of the finished moves.
- **Finished product cost**: Before Odoo prices the finished product,
  the module sets the order's ``extra_cost`` to the costs divided by the
  order quantity, when they are not zero, then runs the standard
  calculation.
- **Printed order**: The production order report adds the start date,
  the non-zero cost values, a *Finished Products* table, and *Effective
  Quantity*, *Price* and *Amount* columns on the components.

It depends on ``mrp_account``. Later changes to a bill of materials do
not reach existing orders, and costs typed on a new order are replaced
by those of its bill of materials at the first save.
