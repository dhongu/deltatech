Labor, utilities and overhead are costs that components do not show.
This module records them on the bill of materials and the manufacturing
order and adds them to the order cost.

- **Costs tab**: A *Costs* tab on the bill of materials and the
  manufacturing order holds *Overhead*, *Utility consumption*, *Net
  Salary Rate*, *Salary Contributions* and a duration. On the order, the
  tab shows the standard ``duration`` field, not the ``duration_cost``
  field that the cost formulas read.
- **New orders**: A new order copies the four cost values from its bill
  of materials and sets ``duration_cost`` to the *Duration* of the bill,
  scaled to the order quantity.
- **Order cost**: *Overhead* is a fixed amount; utilities, net salary
  and contributions are rates multiplied by ``duration_cost``.
  *Production Amount* and *Calculate Price* show the total and unit
  cost: storable components at standard price plus these costs, or, once
  produced, the value of the finished moves.
- **Finished product cost**: Before Odoo prices the finished product,
  the module sets ``extra_cost`` to the costs divided by the order
  quantity, when they are not zero.
- **Printed order**: The production order report adds the start date,
  the non-zero cost values, a *Finished Products* table, and cost
  columns on the components.

It depends on ``mrp_account``. Later bill changes do not reach existing
orders.
