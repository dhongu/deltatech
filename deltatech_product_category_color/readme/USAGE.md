1. Go to **Inventory > Configuration > Product Categories** and open a
   category. A new **Color** field (color picker) is available on the
   form — a color is assigned automatically when the category is
   created, but you can change it.
2. On any **Delivery/Receipt/Transfer** (stock.picking) kanban view, the
   product categories involved in that transfer now show up as colored
   tags next to the status, using the category's color, so you can spot
   at a glance what kind of products a transfer contains.
