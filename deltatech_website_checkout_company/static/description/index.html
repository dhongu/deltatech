Overview

Checkout address form for Romanian shops, with optional company billing.

  • The address is asked in the Romanian order: last name, first name, country, county, street, city, zip code, then email and phone. The last name and the first name are kept together in the name of the contact.
  • Below the address, an optional Billing details section: a customer who buys as a company ticks I am buying as a legal entity (company) and types the company name, its VAT number (CUI) and its registered office (county, city, street, zip code). The CUI is checked (checksum) before the order goes further.
  • When no company is recorded with this CUI, the company is created as a company partner, with its registered office, and the customer becomes one of its delivery addresses, keeping its own address. The order is invoiced to the company and delivered to the customer.
  • When a company is already recorded with this CUI (with or without the RO prefix), a new invoice address is created under it with the name and the registered office typed by the customer, and the order is invoiced to it; the checkout shows it as the selected billing address. Of the recorded company only its name shows (as the parent of the address, a public data of the CUI); its recorded address and contacts do not, and the customer is not attached to it: typing a CUI must not give a visitor the documents of another company. The form asks the same fields whether or not the CUI is known. An invoice address with the same name and registered office under that company is reused, so the same data typed again do not add duplicates.
  • The company billing is available only for deliveries in Romania: the fiscal position of the order follows the customer and the delivery address, not the invoice address, and the domestic VAT of a Romanian company applies to goods delivered in Romania.
  • The CUI is saved as typed, without spaces, dots and hyphens (with or without the RO prefix); whether the company is registered for VAT is not checked with ANAF, so the accountant reviews the new companies before invoicing.
  • Customers who already belong to a company, or are one, keep the standard company name and VAT fields.
Usage
  1. In the shop, add a product to the cart and go to checkout. On the Address step, fill in Last name and First name (two fields, saved together as "Last name First name"), then Country, State / Province, Street and Number, City, Zip Code, Email and Phone. A private person leaves Billing details (optional) unticked.
1 Checkout address form: Last name and First name in separate fields, then the address, email and phone; below, the optional Billing details section with its checkbox unticked
  1. To be invoiced as a company, tick I am buying as a legal entity (company) and fill in Company name, VAT number (CUI) (with or without the RO prefix) and the registered office: Registered office county, Registered office city, Registered office address and Zip Code. The registered office is in Romania.
2 Billing details section ticked, with the company name, its VAT number and the registered office filled in
  1. Click Confirm. A VAT number with a wrong check digit stops the form with The company VAT number (CUI) is not valid.; missing company data (name, VAT number, registered office city, address or zip code) shows Fill in the company details: name, VAT number (CUI) and registered office. Company billing is available only for deliveries in Romania; otherwise the form shows Billing to a company is available only for deliveries in Romania. No partner is created until the data is corrected.
3 Address form after Confirm with the VAT number 12345678: the field is red and the message The company VAT number (CUI) is not valid shows above the form
  1. With a VAT number that is not recorded yet, the company is created with its registered office and becomes the selected Billing address; the customer becomes one of its delivery addresses and keeps its own address as Delivery address.
4 Delivery step of the checkout: the new company is the selected billing address, with its registered office; the customer is the delivery address
  1. In the backend (Sales > Orders > Quotations, or Orders once confirmed), the order has the customer as Customer and Delivery Address, and the company as Invoice Address. The fiscal position and the taxes follow the customer and the delivery address, not the invoice address.
5 Sales order header: Customer and Delivery Address are the customer, Invoice Address is the company
  1. In Contacts, the new company has the typed Tax ID and registered office, and the customer is listed in its Contacts tab as a delivery address.
6 The new company in Contacts: Tax ID and registered office, with the customer as a delivery address in the Contacts tab
  1. With the VAT number of a company already recorded (with or without RO), the form asks the same fields and does not show the recorded data. A new invoice address is created under that company, with the name and the registered office typed by the customer, and is selected as Billing address. The customer is not attached to the company.
7 Delivery step for a recorded company: the selected billing address is Ana Agro SRL, Ana Agro SRL, with the typed registered office; the delivery address is the customer alone
  1. On that order, Customer and Delivery Address are the customer, and Invoice Address is the new address under the recorded company. Each such order adds an invoice address to the company; backend users can merge the duplicates from Contacts.
8 Sales order header for a recorded company: Customer and Delivery Address are the customer, Invoice Address is Ana Agro SRL, Ana Agro SRL

The Billing details section shows only on the main billing address of a customer who does not belong to a company and is not a company itself; the others keep the standard company name and VAT fields. The company is created only from the shop checkout: the address form of the customer portal shows the section too, but saving it there does not create a company.

Versions

20.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

  • Port to 20.0: no base_vat (merged into base), the company is recognised by its VAT number (is_company is computed), the mandatory registered office fields come from res.partner, the city is typed as text also when the country enforces a choice from the city list.
  • Company billing only for deliveries in Romania; consultant sheet (from 19.0).
  • For a CUI already recorded, the company itself or an invoice address under it with the same name and registered office is reused instead of adding a duplicate (from 19.0).

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

  • First version: checkout address in the Romanian order (last name and first name, country, county, city, street, email, phone) and an optional Billing details section that invoices the order to a company: created from the name, the CUI and the registered office typed, or, for a CUI already recorded, a new invoice address under that company with the typed data, without attaching the customer to it and without showing the recorded company data.
