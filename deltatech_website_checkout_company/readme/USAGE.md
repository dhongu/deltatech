1. In the shop, add a product to the cart and go to checkout.
2. Fill in the address: last name, first name, country, county, city, street, email, phone.
3. To be invoiced as a company, tick *I am buying as a legal entity (company)* under
   **Billing details** and fill in the company name, its VAT number (CUI), the county and the
   city of its registered office.
4. Continue the checkout. In the backend, the sales order has the customer as *Customer* and
   *Delivery Address*, and the company as *Invoice Address*.
