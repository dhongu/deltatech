Invoice Delivery / Reception

Adding button in invoice for display reception or delivery

Odoo 19.0 Generic Modules/Other OPL-1 Online • Odoo.sh • On-premise

Overview

Key Features

This module enhances the integration between Odoo Accounting and Inventory by providing quick access to related warehouse operations directly from an invoice.

Features:

  • ✓
    Adds a "Delivery" or "Reception" button to the invoice view, depending on whether it's a customer invoice or a vendor bill.
  • ✓
    Allows users to quickly view all related stock pickings (deliveries or receipts) associated with the current invoice.
  • ✓
    Streamlines the workflow for accountants and warehouse managers who need to verify physical shipments against financial documents.
  • ✓
    Improves traceability and reduces the time spent searching for linked documents.

Usage:

  1. Open a Customer Invoice or a Vendor Bill.
  2. In the top action bar, you will see a new button labeled "Delivery" (for invoices) or "Reception" (for bills).
  3. Clicking this button will open a list of all stock pickings related to the sale/purchase orders linked to this invoice.
