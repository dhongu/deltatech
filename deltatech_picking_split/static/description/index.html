Overview

In standard Odoo a backorder is created when a transfer is validated with less than the full quantity. This module lets the warehouse operator split a transfer by hand before that, choosing line by line what stays in the transfer and what moves to a new one, for example when only part of an order can ship today.

  • Split from the transfer
    An action in the Action menu of a transfer opens a window with one row per line, showing the demand and an editable Kept quantity.
  • Sensible default
    The proposed kept quantity is the available or the reserved quantity, whichever is higher, converted to the unit of measure of the line and never below zero or above the demand.
  • New backorder transfer
    Whatever is not kept goes to a new transfer in Draft, linked to the original one as its backorder. A line with nothing kept moves whole; a line kept in part is split.
  • Trace on the original
    A message with a link to the new transfer is posted on the original one.
  • Safeguards
    The window refuses a kept quantity below zero or above the demand, a line that no longer belongs to the transfer, and a transfer that is already done or cancelled.

The module depends only on Inventory and is available to the Inventory users. The new transfer is left in Draft and is not reserved automatically. At least one line must keep some quantity: if every kept quantity is zero, nothing is changed.

Versions

19.0.1.0.5 (2026-11-11)

  • Apps Store page: a fuller description (what the module does, the problem it solves, its main functions, dependencies and limits). No change in behavior.

19.0.1.0.4 (2026-10-17)

  • Fixed PICKSPLIT-001: the manual backorder wizard proposed as kept quantity the forecast of the product in its stock unit, without converting it to the unit of the move. A move in dozens with 6 units available proposed 6 dozen kept, and a move with stock missing proposed a negative quantity. The proposed kept quantity is now converted to the move unit, at least the reserved quantity, and limited between 0 and the demand.

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-09)

  • Apps Store banner (banner.json).

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-08)

  • Fixed PICKSPLIT-002: the manual backorder wizard checked the kept quantity only in the form. Through RPC or a stale wizard, a kept quantity above the demand raised the demand of the original move and created a backorder with a negative quantity. The wizard now refuses, before changing anything, a kept quantity below zero or above the current demand of the move, a move that no longer belongs to the transfer, and a transfer already done or cancelled.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-09-29)

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
