Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.4 (2026-10-17)
-----------------------

- Fixed PICKSPLIT-001: the manual backorder wizard proposed as kept
  quantity the forecast of the product in its stock unit, without
  converting it to the unit of the move. A move in dozens with 6 units
  available proposed 6 dozen kept, and a move with stock missing
  proposed a negative quantity. The proposed kept quantity is now
  converted to the move unit, at least the reserved quantity, and
  limited between 0 and the demand.

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-09)
-----------------------

- Apps Store banner (banner.json).

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-08)
-----------------------

- Fixed PICKSPLIT-002: the manual backorder wizard checked the kept
  quantity only in the form. Through RPC or a stale wizard, a kept
  quantity above the demand raised the demand of the original move and
  created a backorder with a negative quantity. The wizard now refuses,
  before changing anything, a kept quantity below zero or above the
  current demand of the move, a move that no longer belongs to the
  transfer, and a transfer already done or cancelled.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
