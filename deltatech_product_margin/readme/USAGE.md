1. Open a product (**Inventory/Sales > Products > Products**) and find
   the **Margin** field next to **Taxes** on the product form. It shows
   the profit margin, computed automatically as
   `(Sales Price - Cost) / Sales Price * 100`, and is editable.
2. To set the sales price starting from a target margin instead: type
   the desired **Margin** percentage and click the **Update sale
   price** link next to it — the **Sales Price** is recalculated from
   the **Cost** so that it matches the entered margin (taxes included
   in price are taken into account).
