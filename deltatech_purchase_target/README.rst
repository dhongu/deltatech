This module adds **purchase targets per manufacturer** and shows, live
from the posted vendor bills, how much of the target was achieved —
overall and per eCommerce category, per quarter and cumulated over the
year.

From a business standpoint, it answers the question "where do we stand
with the commercial agreements of our manufacturers?": which
manufacturer or category needs more purchases to reach the volume bonus,
and whether the sales on the same products keep up with the purchases.

Key features:

- **Manufacturer targets** (Purchase › Products): one target per
  manufacturer and year, as an amount (without VAT, company currency) or
  a quantity, split per quarter and per eCommerce category.
- **Only the manufacturer's bills count**: by default the vendor is the
  manufacturer itself; the bills of the resellers are left out. Other
  companies of the manufacturer can be added.
- **Achieved vs Target** (Purchase › Reporting): achieved in the quarter
  and cumulated, achieved %, remaining to target, and the sales of the
  same products, with drill-down to the bill lines and to the sale
  margin report.
- **Vendor Cost Comparison** (Purchase › Reporting): average purchase
  price of each product per vendor, from the vendor bills.
- **Manufacturer in the Purchase Analysis**: search and group the
  purchase orders by manufacturer.

Configuration
=============

- Tick **Is manufacturer** on the manufacturer partners and fill in
  **Manufacturer** on the products (module
  ``deltatech_product_extension``).
- Put the products in the **eCommerce categories** used by the targets.
- Rights: purchase users see the targets and the report; purchase
  managers define the targets. The sales drill-down needs the sale
  margin report rights.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Purchase › Products › Manufacturer Targets* and create a
   target: manufacturer, year, target type (amount or quantity).
2. Check **Vendors**: the bills of these partners count as achieved (by
   default the manufacturer).
3. On the **Targets** tab add one line per quarter: without a category
   for the overall target, with a category for the target of that
   category (its child categories are included).
4. Open *Purchase › Reporting › Achieved vs Target*: per line the target
   and the purchases of the quarter, the cumulated target and purchases
   since January, the achieved %, the remaining amount and the sales of
   the same products. The buttons open the bill lines and the sales
   behind them.

Notes:

- Achieved = posted vendor bills minus vendor credit notes, without VAT,
  by accounting date.
- A product in two eCommerce categories counts in each category target,
  but only once in the overall target of the manufacturer.
- Quantity targets add up the quantities in the unit of measure of each
  product.
