This module adds **purchase targets per manufacturer** and shows, live from the posted vendor
bills, how much of the target was achieved — overall and per eCommerce category, per quarter and
cumulated over the year.

From a business standpoint, it answers the question "where do we stand with the commercial
agreements of our manufacturers?": which manufacturer or category needs more purchases to reach
the volume bonus, and whether the sales on the same products keep up with the purchases.

Key features:

- **Manufacturer targets** (Purchase › Products): one target per manufacturer and year, as an amount
  (without VAT, company currency) or a quantity, split per quarter and per eCommerce category.
- **Only the manufacturer's bills count**: by default the vendor is the manufacturer itself; the
  bills of the resellers are left out. Other companies of the manufacturer can be added.
- **Achieved vs Target** (Purchase › Reporting): achieved in the quarter and cumulated, achieved %,
  remaining to target, and the sales of the same products, with drill-down to the bill lines and
  to the sale margin report.
- **Vendor Cost Comparison** (Purchase › Reporting): average purchase price of each product per
  vendor, from the vendor bills.
- **Manufacturer in the Purchase Analysis**: search and group the purchase orders by manufacturer.
