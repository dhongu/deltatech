1. Go to *Purchase › Products › Manufacturer Targets* and create a target: manufacturer, year,
   target type (amount or quantity).
2. Check **Vendors**: the bills of these partners count as achieved (by default the manufacturer).
3. On the **Targets** tab add one line per quarter: without a category for the overall target, with
   a category for the target of that category (its child categories are included).
4. Open *Purchase › Reporting › Achieved vs Target*: per line the target and the purchases of the
   quarter, the cumulated target and purchases since January, the achieved %, the remaining amount
   and the sales of the same products. The buttons open the bill lines and the sales behind them.

Notes:

- Achieved = posted vendor bills minus vendor credit notes, without VAT, by accounting date.
- A product in two eCommerce categories counts in each category target, but only once in the
  overall target of the manufacturer.
- Quantity targets add up the quantities in the unit of measure of each product; a bill line in
  another unit (box, dozen) is converted to the unit of the product.
- All the figures of a line are in the measure of its target: amounts, or quantities for a quantity
  target. The amount and the quantity of the quarter can be shown from the optional columns.
- *Purchase › Reporting › Vendor Cost Comparison* opens on the vendor bills of the current year,
  with the manufacturers on the rows (expand one to see its products) and the vendors on the
  columns; pick another period in the date filter.
