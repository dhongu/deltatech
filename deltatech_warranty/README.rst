Customers often ask for the warranty terms of the products they buy.
This module keeps the warranty period on each product and prints it on
the sales quotation and order, so the warranty list does not have to be
written by hand for every sale.

**Warranty period on products.** A **Warranty (months)** field is added
on the product form, in the *General Information* tab.

**Warranty page on quotations and orders.** When at least one line of a
sales quotation or order has a product with a warranty period, the
*Quotation / Order* PDF gets an extra page, on a new sheet, with a table
of those lines: the line label (product name and description) and the
warranty in months. Lines whose product has no warranty period, as well
as section and note lines, are left out; when no line has a warranty,
the PDF is unchanged.

Configuration
-------------

Set **Warranty (months)** on the products that have a warranty, in
*Sales > Products > Products*.
