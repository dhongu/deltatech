Overview

By default, Odoo lets a transfer be validated even when the stock is not there, and the quantity on hand quietly goes below zero. The error surfaces weeks later, at the next inventory count. This module stops the move at validation instead, and tells the user what is actually left in the location.

  • Blocks negative stock at validation
    A transfer that would take an internal location below zero cannot be validated.
  • Clear message
    The user sees the product, the location and the quantity that would remain, and is pointed to adjust the quantities or correct the stock.
  • Exceptions per location
    Allow negative stock on the locations where you need it, such as a production floor or a transit area.
  • Checked on the physical stock
    The quantity on hand in the location, per lot, package and owner, not the forecast.
  • Safe for multi-line transfers
    Several lines of the same product in one validation are added up, so they cannot overdraw the stock together.
  • Serial numbers, strict or by total
    Per location, check serial-tracked products on the serial number of the line or on the total of the location.
  • Point of Sale friendly
    Goods sold at the register have already left the shop, so Point of Sale receipts go through by default; each bypass is logged and the shortfall stays visible as negative stock to correct.
  • Only products with tracked stock
    Consumables without Track Inventory are not checked, since Odoo keeps no stock for them.
  • Company setting
    Turn the rule on or off separately for each company.
Configuration

  1. Go to Inventory > Configuration > Settings, section Traceability, and enable No negative stock. The setting applies to the current company; in a multi-company database, enable it on each company.

  2. Right below it, decide on Allow negative stock from Point of Sale (enabled by default). Point of Sale validates the delivery of a receipt in the background and does not show the cashier an error, so a refused delivery would only leave the stock undeducted:

    • enabled: a receipt's delivery is validated even when it takes the location below zero; the bypass is logged and the location shows negative stock to correct;
    • disabled: the rule applies to receipts as well, and their deliveries stay unvalidated until the stock is there.

The setting has no effect when Point of Sale is not installed.

1 No negative stock and Allow negative stock from Point of Sale in Settings

  1. Enable Storage Locations (section Warehouse) to see the location options. Open Inventory > Configuration > Locations and, on the internal locations where stock may go below zero (transit, work in progress), check Allow Negative Stock.

  2. For products tracked by serial number, Check Serial No. (enabled by default) checks the serial number of the line. Uncheck it on a location to check the total of all serial numbers there; reservation then also ignores the serial number.

2 Location form with Allow Negative Stock and Check Serial No.
  1. Before enabling the rule on an existing database, review the negative positions (Inventory > Reporting > Locations, filter Negative Stock): record the missing receipts or transfers first, and use an inventory adjustment only for the real differences. Negative positions left in place block every outgoing move from them.
Usage

Work with transfers as usual. The check runs when a transfer is validated, on every line that takes stock out of an internal location, and on all lines together.

  1. Prepare a transfer. In this example the location Raft A holds 1 unit of the product, and the transfer picks it twice, 1 unit per line, in Detailed Operations. Each line alone would fit; together they need 2 units.
1 Detailed Operations with two lines of 1 unit picked from Raft A, on a stock of 1 unit
  1. Validate. The lines are added up per location, lot, package and owner, after converting the units of measure. When the location would go below zero, validation stops with an Invalid Operation message naming the product, the location and the quantity that would remain (here -1). Nothing is moved: lower the quantities, pick from another location or lot, or validate the missing receipt first.
2 Invalid Operation dialog: you have chosen to avoid negative stock, with the product, the remaining quantity and the location
  1. Check the stock positions. Inventory > Reporting > Locations shows the positions the check compares against: one row per product, location, lot or serial number, package and owner. The check uses On Hand, not Available: a line on one lot, package or owner cannot use the stock of another.
3 Stock positions per location, lot, package and owner
  1. Locations allowed to go negative. On a location with Allow Negative Stock, the same transfer is validated and the position stays negative. Incoming moves are never checked, only the source location of a move.
4 Negative position on WH/Stock/Tranzit, the location that allows negative stock
  1. Serial numbers. With Check Serial No. unchecked on the location, the stock is checked on the total of all serial numbers there: two lines on two serial numbers pass when the location holds two units. Note that a line on a serial number that is not in the location also passes when the total is enough; that serial number then goes to -1 and has to be corrected by an inventory adjustment.
5 Done transfer from Raft Serii, Detailed Operations on SN-1001 and SN-1002
  1. Bring a negative position back to zero. Once the cause is found and the missing documents are recorded, open Inventory > Operations > Physical Inventory, filter Negative Stock, enter the counted quantity (0 when the shelf is empty) and click Apply. An increase is an incoming move and is not blocked; a count that would take the stock below zero is blocked like any other outgoing move.
6 Physical Inventory on a negative position: On Hand -5, Counted 0, Difference +5, before Apply

Not checked: products without Track Inventory, non-internal locations, and Point of Sale receipts while Allow negative stock from Point of Sale is enabled. Every Point of Sale bypass is written to the server log with the transfer, product, quantity and location.
