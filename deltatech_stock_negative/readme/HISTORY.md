## 19.0.2.1.0

- A Point of Sale receipt no longer leaves its goods on the stock. Point of Sale
  validates its own delivery in the background and swallows any `UserError` that
  validation raises, so the refusal issued here when a location would go below zero
  never reached the cashier: the receipt was closed, the customer walked out with the
  goods, and the transfer stayed unvalidated with the stock never decreased - silently,
  with nothing to act on but a stray open transfer nobody was looking for. Refusing the
  move does not keep the goods on the shelf; it only stops the system from recording
  what already happened at the register.
- New company setting *Allow negative stock from Point of Sale*
  (`no_negative_stock_pos_bypass`, on by default, shown only when *No negative stock*
  is enabled): a Point of Sale delivery is validated even when it takes the location
  below zero. Every bypass is logged as a warning naming the transfer, the quantity,
  the product and the location, and the deviation stays visible as negative stock to be
  corrected by an inventory adjustment. Turn the setting off to apply the rule to Point
  of Sale as well, accepting that such receipts will not decrease the stock.
- The check also honours a `skip_negative_stock_check` context key, for callers that
  have to force a validation knowingly.

## 19.0.2.0.11 (2026-10-01)

- **Serialized stock is no longer blocked when Check Serial No. is off.** On a
  location with the serial check disabled, the no-negative-stock check cleared
  the line's serial number and then searched explicitly for quants *without* a
  serial, so it excluded the very quants holding the stock and blocked a
  legitimate transfer, although reservation had gone through. The lot filter is
  now left out entirely in that case: the physical quantity is summed across all
  serial numbers of the location (product, package and owner filters kept), and
  every line of the product consumes from that same aggregate. With the serial
  check on (the default), the check stays per serial number. (STOCK-001)
- Romanian translations brought in line with the current strings: the blocking
  message was shown in English (its translation matched an older wording), the
  setting read "Fară stoc negativ" and Check Serial No. had no translation. A
  post-migration reloads them over the old ones on existing databases.
- The help of Check Serial No. described a reservation check the module does not
  do; it now says what the option changes.
- Consultant sheet (`readme/FISA_CONSULTANT.md`) with screenshots generated by
  `tests/test_screenshots.py`, covering every case of the check.

## 19.0.2.0.8 (2026-09-22)

- **Several lines of one validation no longer share the same stock.** The check
  runs over the whole recordset before `super()._action_done()` writes anything
  to the quants, so every line used to read the same untouched quantity. Taken
  one by one, two lines of 1 against a stock of 1 are each legal, and the
  transfer still landed at -1. It now carries what the earlier lines of the same
  batch already took from each quant, keyed by product, location, lot, package
  and owner -- so lines on different quants still do not add up.
  Reported twice from the shop floor: 2 pieces picked to shelve when only 1 was
  on hand.
