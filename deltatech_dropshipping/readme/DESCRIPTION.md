This module enhances the dropshipping workflow by providing better visibility and price control.

Features:

- Displays the delivery address in the stock picking.
- Adds a warning on the Purchase Order if the purchase price is higher than the sale price of the corresponding Sale Order line in drop shipping flows.
- Compares both prices on the same basis: after the line discount, excluding VAT, on the product's base unit of measure and in the company currency (at each order's date), so different currencies, units or discounts on the two documents do not give a wrong warning.
