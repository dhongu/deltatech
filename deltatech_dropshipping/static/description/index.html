Deltatech Drop Shipping

Delivery address in picking

Odoo 19.0 Warehouse OPL-1 Online • Odoo.sh • On-premise

Overview

This module enhances the dropshipping workflow by providing better visibility and price control.

Features: - Displays the delivery address in the stock picking. - Adds a warning on the Purchase Order if the purchase price is higher than the sale price of the corresponding Sale Order line in drop shipping flows. - Automatically calculates and compares prices excluding VAT for both documents to ensure accuracy.
