Features:

- Product category per company (field "Company" on the category form, visible in multi-company)
- Setting "Share product category to all companies" (Settings > Companies, debug mode): unchecked, it activates the multi-company record rule on product categories
- The standard categories ("All", "Goods", "Expenses", "Services") remain without a company

- Categorii de produse pentru fiecare companie
- Categoriile standard ("All", "Goods", "Expenses", "Services") trebuie să rămână fără companie
