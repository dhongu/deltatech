Overview

Features:

  • ✓
    Report with positions from stock location, with pricelist
  • ✓
    Choose stock location
  • ✓
    Choose partner or pricelist for price computation
  • ✓
    Two thresholds can be set for "text" quantities (i.e. Low stock, Limited stock, Stock) with their respective stock texts

Usage

  1. Open Sales > Reporting > Location Stock. The Reporting menu of Sales is shown to sales administrators; the Sales menu is available once the Sales application (sale_management) is installed.

  1. In the Location Stock window choose the Source Location and the Partner: the Reseller Price List is filled in from the partner's pricelist (you can also pick it directly; left empty, the reseller price equals the list price). Tick Show text instead of stock to replace the quantities with stock level labels: up to the 1st threshold the report shows the 1st threshold text, up to the 2nd threshold the 2nd threshold text, above it the Above 2nd threshold text. Keep Refresh Report ticked to recompute from the current stock, then press Apply.

  1. The Location Stock Quant Report lists every product with free quantity in the location (reserved quantities excluded), with its Quantity, the stock level text (Qty Text), the Reseller price and the List Price. Check the labels and prices, then select the lines and use Actions > Export; leave the Quantity column out when the partner should only see the stock level texts. The Description column shows the internal product description with its HTML tags, so leave it out of the export as well.

  1. Group the lines by Category of Product (or Product) from the search options, or switch to the pivot view. On the group rows, the price columns add up unit prices and are not meaningful.
