Customers often ask suppliers for a declaration that delivered goods
meet their technical standards. This module stores each product's
quality references and prints a numbered Declaration of Conformity for
lots, invoices and deliveries.

- **Quality references**: A *Quality Control* tab on the product holds
  *Standard of Company*, *Data Sheet*, *Technical Specification* and
  *Standards*, printed when filled in.
- **Declaration records**: A *Declaration of Conformity* list (Sales
  product catalog menu) shows *Number*, *Date of Declaration*, *Product*
  and *Lot*. Printing numbers them from the ``DC/`` sequence; by hand,
  you type the number.
- **Print and create**: The print action on a lot, a posted invoice or a
  delivery creates the missing declarations and prints them: one per
  lot, or per product and date without a lot. Services are skipped.
- **Production date**: The lot's *Production Date* dates its declaration
  (today when empty) and prints as the quality plan date for lots with
  an expiration date.
- **Second delivery form**: *Declaration of Conformity 2* on a delivery
  prints one declaration for all its lines, with product, quantity, lot,
  use date and storage conditions, and creates no records.

The module depends on Sales, Manufacturing, ``product_expiry`` and
``stock_account``. Internal users can edit declarations of their
companies. The wording is fixed in the templates; the second form reads
a partner *NRC* field that this module does not add.
