Customers often ask for a declaration that delivered goods meet their
technical standards. This module stores each product's quality
references and prints a numbered Declaration of Conformity for lots,
invoices and deliveries.

- **Quality references**: A *Quality Control* tab on the product holds
  *Standard of Company*, *Data Sheet*, *Technical Specification* and
  *Standards*, printed when filled in.
- **Declaration records**: A *Declaration of Conformity* list (Sales
  product catalog menu) shows *Number*, *Date of Declaration*, *Product*
  and *Lot*. Printing numbers them from the ``DC/`` sequence.
- **Print and create**: Printing from a lot, a posted invoice or a
  delivery creates the missing declarations, one per lot or per product
  and date without a lot. Services on invoices are skipped.
- **Production date**: The lot's *Production Date* dates its declaration
  and, for lots with an expiration date, prints as the quality plan
  date. Lot and invoice prints need it; delivery prints use today when
  it is empty.
- **Second delivery form**: *Declaration of Conformity 2* on a delivery
  prints one declaration for all its lines, with product, quantity, lot,
  use date and storage conditions, and creates no records.

The module depends on Sales, Manufacturing and ``product_expiry``.
Internal users can edit declarations of their companies. Both printed
forms use fixed wording and read a partner ``nrc`` field that this
module does not add.
