1. On a product form, open the **Quality Control** tab and fill in the standard of company, data sheet, technical specification and standards used by that product.
2. Go to **Sales > Products > Declaration of Conformity** to create a declaration: pick the **Product**, optionally a **Lot/Serial Number**, the declaration **Number** and **Date**. The quality fields from the product are shown for reference.
3. Print the declaration from the record, or let it be printed automatically together with the invoice/delivery report (Declaration of Conformity and warranty certificate reports).
