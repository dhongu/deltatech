Adds the product image to the transfers.

- an *Image* column on the lines of the transfer form (optional column,
  shown by default);
- the image at the start of the product cell on the delivery slip,
  before and after validation, with or without lots/serial numbers or
  packages;
- the image on the picking operations report, to help the warehouse
  team.

The option *Hide Product Images on Transfer PDFs* (Inventory settings,
per company) removes the images from the PDFs; on screen they stay.

Usage
=====

1. Open a transfer (**Inventory > Operations > Deliveries**, Receipts or
   Internal Transfers). In the **Operations** tab, the **Image** column
   shows the product image before the product. It is an optional column:
   hide it from the column menu if you do not need it.
2. Print the **Delivery Slip**, before or after validating. The image
   (at most 48 px) sits at the start of the product cell, also on lines
   with lots or serial numbers and on lines grouped by product.
3. Print **Picking Operations**: the image shows next to each product,
   to help the warehouse team.
4. To print without images, tick **Hide Product Images on Transfer
   PDFs** in **Inventory > Configuration > Settings** (Operations). The
   setting is per company; on screen the images stay.
