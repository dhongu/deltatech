1. Open a transfer (**Inventory > Operations > Deliveries**, Receipts or Internal Transfers). In the
   **Operations** tab, the **Image** column shows the product image before the product. It is an
   optional column: hide it from the column menu if you do not need it.
2. Print the **Delivery Slip**, before or after validating. The image (at most 48 px) sits at the
   start of the product cell, also on lines with lots or serial numbers and on lines grouped by
   product.
3. Print **Picking Operations**: the image shows next to each product, to help the warehouse team.
4. To print without images, tick **Hide Product Images on Transfer PDFs** in
   **Inventory > Configuration > Settings** (Operations). The setting is per company; on screen
   the images stay.
