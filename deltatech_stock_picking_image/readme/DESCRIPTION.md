Adds the product image to the transfers.

- an *Image* column on the lines of the transfer form (optional column, shown by default);
- the image at the start of the product cell on the delivery slip, before and after validation,
  with or without lots/serial numbers or packages;
- the image on the picking operations report, to help the warehouse team.

The option *Hide Product Images on Transfer PDFs* (Inventory settings, per company) removes the
images from the PDFs; on screen they stay.
