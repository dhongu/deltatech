Key Features
============

This module adds a generic "Card Payment" method to the accounting
configuration, allowing for better classification and tracking of
payments made by card.

Features:
---------

- Automatically creates a "Card Payment" entry in the Odoo payment
  methods.
- Provides a clear way to distinguish card payments from other inbound
  payment types.
- Simplifies bank reconciliation processes for businesses that process
  many card transactions.
- Facilitates the setting up of journals with specific payment handling
  for cards.

Usage:
------

1. After installation, the "Card Payment" method will be available under
   the accounting configuration for inbound payments.
2. Select this method when processing customer payments or when
   configuring accounting journals.
3. This is useful for retail or service-oriented businesses to organize
   their financial statements more effectively.
