1. On a **Contact**, the **Customer** field on a sale order or invoice only lists top-level (company) contacts — child/delivery/invoice sub-contacts are excluded from that main selector, keeping the "Invoice Address" and "Delivery Address" fields as the place to pick a specific sub-contact.
2. Open a company contact and go to one of its child contacts (an invoice or delivery address). Enable **Contact Default** on that child contact to make it the address Odoo proposes automatically for that type (invoice/delivery) when creating sale orders or invoices for the parent company. Only one child contact per type can be default — enabling it on one automatically clears it on the others.
3. On a partner's main form, the **Green Invoice** checkbox can be enabled to mark that the invoice should not be printed for this customer.
