Usage

Install only Change Unit of measure (deltatech_change_uom): its dependency UoM Domain by Reference (deltatech_uom_domain) is installed with it. Both folders of the download must be in your addons path; then use Apps → Update Apps List and search without the "Apps" filter.

Enable Units of Measure in Inventory → Configuration → Settings. Administrators can change units right away; give other users the Allow to change unit of measure group (user form, in developer mode).

The module relabels the unit, it does not convert quantities: a line of 4.50 Units becomes 4.50 m³, at the same price and amount. Use it when the figures were already right in the new unit and only the unit was chosen wrongly. When the product has not been invoiced yet, standard Odoo lets you change the unit directly on the product form.

  1. Open the action — open the product (Inventory → Products → Products), click the gear next to its name and choose Change Uom. The action is on the product form, not on a product variant.
1 Gear menu of the product form, with the Change Uom action
  1. Choose the units — in the Change Uom wizard, choose the new Unit of Measure (1), from any unit tree, and the Default Purchase Unit (2), among the units convertible to the new one; a purchase unit that no longer fits is cleared. Click Apply (3).
2 Change Uom wizard: Unit of Measure (1), Default Purchase Unit (2) and Apply (3)
  1. Check the product — the smart buttons show the same figures in the new unit (on hand 2.50 m³, sold 2.00 m³, purchased 4.50 m³). In the Purchase tab, the vendor line is in m³ at the same price, and Default Purchase Unit (1) is m³. Prices are not recalculated: check that the vendor price is the price per new unit.
3 Product after the change: vendor line in m³ and Default Purchase Unit m³ (1)
  1. Purchase orders — the received and billed purchase order line moved from Units to m³; quantity, received, billed, unit price and amount are unchanged. Lines in the old purchase unit move to the new purchase unit, so the order, the receipt and the bill stay in the same unit.
4 Received and billed purchase order, line in m³
  1. Posted invoices — the posted customer invoice line is in m³, at the same price and amount (2.00 m³, 2,900.00). This is the case standard Odoo refuses. The invoice is not reposted, and documents already sent outside Odoo (e-invoicing, tax reports) keep the old unit. The Unit column is optional on invoice lines: show it from the column menu.
5 Posted customer invoice, line in m³
  1. Stock moves — the receipt (4.50) and the delivery (2.00) are in m³, with the same quantities.
6 Moves history of the product, in m³

No journal entry is created and no amount or account changes; only the unit on the lines is replaced, also on posted entries and in locked periods.

Versions

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-11-03)

  • readme/USAGE.md for the Apps Store: installation, the wizard and the effect on orders, posted invoices and stock moves, with English screenshots in static/description/en/. The screenshot test captures the same shots in both languages (test_capture_fise RO, test_capture_usage EN).

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

  • Migration to 19.0. The purchase unit of measure (uom_po_id) is gone from the product in 19.0; the wizard now changes the Default Purchase Unit (purchase_uom_id) added by deltatech_uom_domain, a new dependency. Purchase order lines and vendor pricelist lines in the old purchase unit move to the new one; a purchase unit that cannot be converted to the new product unit is cleared on the form and refused on apply.
  • product_uom_id on sale and purchase order lines, SQL() builder for the updates, cache invalidated after them; the Allow to change unit of measure group no longer sets category_id.
  • Changing the unit of measure is restricted to the Allow to change unit of measure group: the Change Uom action is shown only to its members, the wizard is accessible only to them and the change is refused for anyone else. Settings administrators get the group by default.
  • One unit mapping on every document: lines in the old purchase unit move to the new purchase unit also on receipts and vendor bills, so a purchase order, its receipt and its bill stay in the same unit (19.0 no longer flags a conversion between incompatible units).
  • Own module icon (ruler with a change accent) instead of the generic gears, regenerated Apps banner, consultant sheet (readme/FISA_CONSULTANT.md) with screenshots generated by tests/test_screenshots.py; Romanian name of the group corrected.
