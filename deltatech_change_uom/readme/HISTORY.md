## 20.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-21)

- One unit mapping on every document: lines in the old purchase unit move to the
  new purchase unit also on receipts and vendor bills, so a purchase order, its
  receipt and its bill stay in the same unit (since 19.0 a conversion between
  incompatible units is no longer flagged).
- Own module icon (ruler with a change accent) instead of the generic gears,
  regenerated Apps banner, consultant sheet (`readme/FISA_CONSULTANT.md`) with
  screenshots generated by `tests/test_screenshots.py`; Romanian name of the
  group corrected.

## 20.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

- Migration to 20.0. The unit of measure is now `uom_id` on purchase order lines,
  stock moves, stock move lines and vendor pricelist lines (sale order lines and
  invoice lines keep `product_uom_id`); access rights moved to `ir.access`.

## 19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

- Migration to 19.0. The purchase unit of measure (`uom_po_id`) is gone from the
  product in 19.0; the wizard now changes the *Default Purchase Unit*
  (`purchase_uom_id`) added by `deltatech_uom_domain`, a new dependency. Purchase
  order lines and vendor pricelist lines in the old purchase unit move to the new
  one; a purchase unit that cannot be converted to the new product unit is
  cleared on the form and refused on apply.
- `product_uom_id` on sale and purchase order lines, `SQL()` builder for the
  updates, cache invalidated after them; the *Allow to change unit of measure*
  group no longer sets `category_id`.
- Changing the unit of measure is restricted to the *Allow to change unit of
  measure* group: the *Change Uom* action is shown only to its members, the
  wizard is accessible only to them and the change is refused for anyone else.
  Settings administrators get the group by default.
