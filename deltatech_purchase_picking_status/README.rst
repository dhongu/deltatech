Buyers who receive goods in several deliveries need to see at a glance
which purchase orders are still waiting for goods. This module adds a
*Delivery Status* to purchase orders, worked out from their receipts.

- **Delivery status**: The status is *In Progress* while any receipt of
  the order is neither done nor cancelled, and *Done* when every receipt
  is done or cancelled.
- **Orders without receipts**: A confirmed order with no receipts, such
  as an order for services, is *Done*; before confirmation it is *In
  Progress*.
- **Badge and column**: The status shows as a badge on the order form
  and as a column, visible by default, in the RFQ and purchase order
  lists.
- **Filters**: Two search filters, *Done* and *In progress*, select
  orders by delivery status.
- **Automatic update**: The status is not stored and follows the order
  as soon as it is confirmed or a receipt is validated or cancelled.

The module depends on ``purchase_stock`` and adds no groups, so anyone
who can see purchase orders sees the status. Cancelled orders follow the
same rule: a cancelled order without receipts shows *In Progress*.
