1. Create a purchase order in EUR for a supplier who bills in RON.
2. On the **Other Info** tab, set **Invoicing Journal** to the vendor bills journal without
   currency.
3. Confirm the order and click **Create Bill**: the bill is in RON, with the EUR prices
   converted at today's rate.
4. Set the **Bill Date** from the supplier invoice: the prices are converted again, at the rate
   of that date.

Leave **Invoicing Journal** empty for suppliers who bill in the order currency.
