Customers who order often keep their own reference on every order, but
the customer portal lists their quotations and orders only by Odoo's
order number. This module adds a reference column and a search box to
those two lists, so a customer can find a document by the number they
know.

- **Reference column**: The *Customer Reference* of the sales order is
  shown right after the order number, in both the quotation list and the
  order list.
- **Search box**: A search box above the lists lets the customer choose
  where to search: in the order name, in the customer reference, or in
  both (the default).
- **Partial matches**: The search matches part of the text and ignores
  upper and lower case, so a few digits of a reference are enough.
- **Extra sorting**: The lists can also be sorted by order name or by
  customer reference.

The module extends the customer portal of the Sales app and depends on
*eCommerce* (``website_sale``). It changes only the quotation and order
lists; invoices and the other portal documents stay as they are.
