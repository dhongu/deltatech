Overview

Adds rules to a pricelist for many products at once and applies the same price computation to many existing rules, instead of entering the rules one by one.

Add rules for many products. The Add Rules button on the pricelist form, and the Add to Pricelist action on the selected products (list or kanban of products or product variants), open a wizard where you:

  • ✓
    choose what the rules apply on: Products, Product Variants or Product Categories;
  • ✓
    pick the products, or fill the list with all the products of some categories and of their subcategories (Add Products of Categories);
  • ✓
    set once the Min. Quantity, the Validity Period and the price computation, with the same options as a standard rule: Fixed Price, Discount (optionally on another pricelist) or Formula (based on the sales price, the cost or another pricelist, with discount or markup, rounding, extra fee and margins);
  • ✓
    choose what happens when the pricelist already has a rule for the same product, minimum quantity and validity period: keep it, update it or add a new rule.

One rule is created for each product, variant or category. At the end, a message shows how many rules were added, updated or kept, and the resulting rules open in a list.

Apply the same formula to many rules. The Apply Formula button on the pricelist form (all the rules of the pricelist) and the Apply Formula action on the selected rules of a rules list change, on all the rules at once:

  • ✓
    the price computation; the values of the other price types are cleared, as on the standard rule form;
  • ✓
    and/or the minimum quantity;
  • ✓
    and/or the validity period.

Usage

Enable the pricelists first: Sales > Configuration > Settings, section Pricing, tick Pricelists and save.

Add rules for many products

  1. Go to Sales > Products > Pricelists and open a pricelist. The header has the Add Rules and Apply Formula buttons (Apply Formula only when the pricelist has rules).

  1. Click Add Rules. Choose Apply On (products, product variants or product categories) and add the products. To take all the products of a category and of its subcategories, select it in Add Products of Categories. Set the Min. Quantity, the Validity Period and the price computation, then choose what to do with a rule that already exists for the same product, minimum quantity and validity period: keep it, update it or add a new rule.

  1. Click Add Rules. A message shows how many rules were added, updated or kept, and the rules open in a list.

  1. You can also start from the products: in Sales > Products > Products, select the products and use Actions > Add to Pricelist. The wizard opens with the selected products; choose the pricelist.

Apply the same formula to many rules

  1. On the pricelist form, click Apply Formula to change all its rules, or select some rules in a list of pricelist rules and use Actions > Apply Formula. Remove from the list the rules you do not want to change.
  2. Tick what to change: Change the Price Computation, Change the Min. Quantity and/or Change the Validity Period, and set the new values. What is not ticked stays as it is on each rule.

  1. Click Apply. All the selected rules get the new computation; the values of the old price type (e.g. the fixed price) are cleared, as on the standard rule form.
