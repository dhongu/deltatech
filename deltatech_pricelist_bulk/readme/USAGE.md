**Add rules for many products**

1. Go to *Sales > Products > Pricelists* and open a pricelist.
2. Click **Add Rules**. Or select some products in *Sales > Products > Products* and use
   *Actions > Add to Pricelist*.
3. Choose **Apply On** and select the products, variants or categories. To take all the
   products of a category, select it in **Add Products of Categories**.
4. Set the **Min. Quantity**, the **Validity Period** and the price computation.
5. Choose what to do with the rules that already exist and click **Add Rules**.

**Apply the same formula to many rules**

1. On the pricelist form, click **Apply Formula** to change all its rules. Or select some
   rules in a list of pricelist rules and use *Actions > Apply Formula*.
2. Remove from the list the rules you do not want to change.
3. Tick what to change (price computation, min. quantity, validity period), set the new
   values and click **Apply**.
