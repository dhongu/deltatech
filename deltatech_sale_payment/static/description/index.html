Overview

Sales staff need to see from the order whether the customer has paid. This module adds the collected amount and a payment status to sales orders and lets a salesperson record a payment.

  • Payment on the order
    The order form shows Payment, the amount collected, with the Provider and Payment Status.
  • Seven statuses
    Orders get one of Without, Initiated, Pending, Authorized, Partial, Done or Cancelled, shown by default in the quotation and order lists, with filters and grouping.
  • Confirm Payment
    An action of the order opens a window starting from the latest draft or pending transaction, or from the amount left to collect, and asks for provider, method, amount and Payment Date.
  • Confirm or Add
    The Confirm button marks the transaction done and notes the entered date on the order; Odoo can then confirm the quotation. Add only records a pending transaction.
  • Safeguards
    The window needs write access to the order, not Invoicing rights, and refuses a negative amount, an authorized payment, another order's transaction, another company's provider, and a method the provider does not offer.

It depends on Sales, payment and account_payment; the action is for Sales users. A provider whose journal has no payment method line (such as wire transfer) gets no accounting payment; the accountant records it.

Versions

  • Apps Store page: a fuller description (what the module does, the problem it solves, its main functions, dependencies and limits). No change in behavior.

  • Development status raised from Beta to Production/Stable: the module has been in production use at customers for a long time.

20.0.1.3.3 (2026-10-09)

  • SALEPAY-008: the "Confirm Payment" wizard accepts again the payment provider of a parent company on an order of a branch, as Odoo does everywhere else for providers. Since 20.0.1.3.2 (SALEPAY-006) such an order, with a pending transaction on the parent company's provider, could no longer be confirmed ("The payment provider … belongs to another company than the order …"). A provider of an unrelated company is still refused.
  • Port of 19.0.1.3.4.

20.0.1.3.2 (2026-10-04)

  • SALEPAY-006 (security): through RPC, an internal user could create a "Confirm Payment" wizard with any transaction and call update_transaction on it, changing as superuser the amount, provider and payment method of another order's pending payment, or confirm it through an order they could access. The method is now private (_update_transaction), and every step of the wizard checks that the transaction belongs to the order, that the provider is of the order's company and that the payment method belongs to the provider. The normal use of the wizard from the order is unchanged; a custom code calling update_transaction() must call do_add_payment() instead.
  • Port of 19.0.1.3.2 (dhongu/deltatech#3114). In 20 every payment method (brands and the "unknown" one included) belongs to a single provider, so the payment method check is payment_method_id.provider_id == provider_id instead of the provider's method/brand list plus the global payment.payment_method_unknown; the tests create the other-company provider without state and the foreign method on a second provider; test_wizard_update_transaction gives the wizard a payment method of the new provider (the method of the transaction, taken by default, belongs to the old one and is now refused).

20.0.1.3.1 (2026-10-01)

  • Tests: the invoice payment tests sell a product without cost, so they no longer fail with "You can not sell below the purchase price." when deltatech_sale_commission is installed in the same database. Port of 19.0.1.3.1.

20.0.1.3.0 (2026-10-01)

"Confirm Payment" wizard (SALEPAY-002 to SALEPAY-005):

  • SALEPAY-002: the payment date is kept. It goes in the transaction's state message, in a note on the order and, when the provider creates an accounting payment, as the date of that payment. The transaction is post-processed when it is confirmed instead of waiting for the cron, so the payment exists with that date right away.
  • SALEPAY-003: the wizard takes over only a transaction still waiting for the money (draft or pending). A confirmed transaction is never cancelled or deleted any more: the wizard proposes the rest to pay and adds a new transaction for it. An order with an authorized payment is refused, the authorization is captured or voided at the provider.
  • SALEPAY-004: whoever may change the order may confirm its payment. The wizard checks the write access on the order and writes the transaction as superuser, so a salesman without Invoicing rights no longer gets an access error; salesmen can read the transactions, and the action is shown to sales users only.
  • SALEPAY-005: the post-processing of a transaction whose provider has no payment method line (wire transfer, "none": Odoo has no account.payment.method for them) creates no accounting payment instead of failing with "Please define a payment method line on your payment." every 10 minutes for 4 days. The quotation is confirmed (when it needs no online signature) and, with automatic invoicing, invoiced, as for the other providers; the accountant records the payment from the bank statement.
  • Port of 19.0.1.3.0. The transactions are written with payment_safe_write and post-processed with _post_process_with_lock, the method Odoo 20 uses to confirm a transaction manually; the fallback payment method is the provider's own "unknown" one. The SALEPAY-005 rule is in _should_create_payment. In 20 the wire transfer has a payment method line (account_payment_custom, auto-installed), so a wire transfer confirmed in the wizard does get a payment, dated with the payment date; the rule applies to the providers that still have none ("none", or a wire transfer without account_payment_custom).

20.0.1.2.3 (2026-10-01)

  • Consultant sheet (readme/FISA_CONSULTANT.md) with screenshots generated by tests/test_screenshots.py.
  • The "Payment Status" group-by of the order search showed in English: the view term was missing from the translations.

20.0.1.2.2 (2026-10-01)

Migration to Odoo 20, with the 19.0 history up to 19.0.1.2.2 (SALEPAY-001 included):

  • Payment link: amount_max is gone from payment.link.wizard; the wizard receives the amount already paid (amount_paid) next to what is left to pay.
  • Confirm Payment wizard: payment transactions are written with payment_safe_write (required in 20); the provider list no longer filters on state (a disabled provider is archived in 20); the default payment method is the first active one of the provider, other than "unknown". A confirmed transaction that is removed is no longer "cancelled" first (Odoo refused it with a warning).
  • Access rights in security/ir.access.csv.
  • The 19.0.1.2.0 and 19.0.1.2.2 migrations are kept: they run only for a database upgraded to 20 from a version below them (e.g. 19.0.1.1.x) and are skipped otherwise. Checked on 20 from 19.0.1.1.6.

19.0.1.2.2 (2026-10-01)

  • SALEPAY-001: the amount paid is computed in the order currency. The invoice side used the *_signed amounts (company currency) and compared them with the order total: a EUR 100 order invoiced at 5 RON/EUR and paid EUR 50 showed 250 paid, status done, and the payment link proposed 0. It now uses the invoice amounts in the invoice currency (negative for credit notes) and converts invoices and transactions in another currency at the document date.
  • Migration: the orders in a currency other than the company's are recomputed; the SQL of 19.0.1.2.0 uses the invoice currency too.

19.0.1.2.1 (2026-09-29)

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
