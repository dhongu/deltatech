1. Open an expense report in **Draft** or **Advance** and click **Preia cheltuieli HR**.
2. The wizard lists the employee's submitted or approved expenses that are not posted and not
   linked to another report; select them and confirm.
3. Each expense becomes a report line, with its amount, taxes, vendor, account and analytic
   distribution. The report is then validated and posted as usual.

From *Expenses*, you can also select several expenses of the same employee and use
*Actions > Adaugă în decont de cheltuieli*.
