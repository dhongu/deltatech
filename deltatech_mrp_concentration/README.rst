Recomputes the quantity of the main ingredient of a recipe from the
concentration you want in the finished product and the concentration of
the ingredient you have, then balances the batch with a diluent or a
by-product. Meant for chemical, food or pharmaceutical products made by
diluting or concentrating an active ingredient.

**Concentrations on the bill of materials.** The BoM form gets two
fields next to the product: *Concentration* (of the finished product)
and *Concentration Primary* (of the main ingredient). Both are plain
numbers, so use the same unit for both, for example percentages.

**Ingredient type on the components.** Each component line gets an
*Ingredient Type*: *Primary* (the active ingredient), *Secondary* (the
diluent) or *Normal* (default, not recalculated).

**Recalculation on the BoM.** When you change either concentration or
the BoM quantity, the *Primary* component quantity becomes BoM quantity
× *Concentration* / *Concentration Primary*. The rest of the batch is
balanced:

- dilution (the result is below the BoM quantity): the *Secondary*
  component gets the difference;
- concentration (the result is above the BoM quantity): the by-products
  get the excess and the *Secondary* component is set to zero.

**Recalculation on the manufacturing order.** The MO form shows the same
two fields, copied from the BoM when you pick it. Changing a
concentration or the quantity to produce recalculates the demand of the
primary and secondary components and of the by-products of that order
with the same rule, so the actual concentration of a received lot can be
used for one order without changing the BoM.

**Where to find it.** *Manufacturing > Products > Bills of Materials*
for the BoM, and *Manufacturing > Operations > Manufacturing Orders* for
the orders. The by-products are entered on the *By-products* tab of the
BoM, available when *By-Products* is enabled in the Manufacturing
settings.

Limitations
-----------

- The recalculation runs only while editing the BoM or the MO form.
  Orders created automatically (from a sales order or a replenishment
  rule) do not get the concentrations and keep the BoM quantities until
  the fields are changed on the form.
- When the BoM has several by-products, they all receive the same excess
  quantity.

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.5 (2026-11-03)
-----------------------

- Description rewritten after the code: the concentrations are on the
  BoM and the manufacturing order, not on the component lines; the lines
  get an *Ingredient Type* (Primary, Secondary, Normal).
- Documented the recalculation rule and the diluent / by-product
  balance; removed the traceability claims the code does not support.
- Added a Limitations section (form-only recalculation, same quantity on
  every by-product).
- Summary and Apps banner aligned with the description.

19.0.1.0.4 (2026-10-16)
-----------------------

- Development status raised from Beta to Production/Stable: the module
  has been in production use at customers for a long time.

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-08)
-----------------------

- Fixed CONCENTRATION-001: on a manufacturing order, a concentration
  higher than the base one raised an error, because the by-product
  quantity was written on a computed field of the stock move. The
  by-product demand is now set on the move quantity.
- Fixed CONCENTRATION-002: switching a BoM or a manufacturing order
  between dilution and concentration kept the quantity of the group no
  longer used (secondary ingredient or by-product). That group is now
  set to zero.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.
