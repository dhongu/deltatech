Odoo lets you restrict price changes in the Point of Sale to managers.
When every employee working at the register is a manager, or when the
business wants a fixed price at the till whoever sells, that restriction
is not enough: a manager can still type any price on the order.

This module adds one more step to the standard restriction:

- **Managers included**: a new checkbox under *Price Control*, per
  register. When it is ticked, nobody can change the product price on
  the order — cashiers and managers alike.
- **Every manual path is closed**: the *Price* key of the numpad is
  disabled, and the price check of *Employees* (``pos_hr``) refuses the
  change with its usual message.
- **Prices still adapt by themselves**: prices recomputed by Odoo —
  pricelists, fiscal positions, taxes included in the price — keep
  working. Only the manual change is blocked.
- **Corrections go through the product**: a wrong price is fixed on the
  product form and reaches the register from there.

The lock is checked in the register screen. It does not verify the price
of the orders on the server.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings* and select the
   register.
2. Under *Pricing*, tick *Price Control* (restrict price modification to
   managers).
3. Tick *Managers included*, right below it, and save.
4. Reload the register page if a session is already open: the setting is
   read when the register loads.

From now on the *Price* key of the numpad stays disabled for everybody
on this register. To change a price, edit the sales price of the
product; with *POS Price Sync* installed the new price reaches the open
registers without a reload.
