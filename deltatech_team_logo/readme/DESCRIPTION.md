One legal company, several brands: in standard Odoo every PDF report carries the same company
logo. With this module, each sales team can have its own logo, and invoices, quotations, sales
orders and delivery slips issued for that team show it in the header automatically.

- **Logo per sales team**: A **Logo** field on the sales team, set like the company logo.
- **Used automatically**: A document whose sales team has a logo prints with that logo; all
  other documents keep the company logo.
- **Invoices, quotations and deliveries**: Works for sales orders and quotations, invoices,
  and delivery slips that come from a sales order.
- **All report layouts**: Standard, Striped, Boxed, Bold, Folder, Wave and Bubble.
- **Nothing changes without a logo**: A team without a logo prints exactly as standard Odoo.
