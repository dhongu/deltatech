Overview

eCommerce Wishlist Extension

This module extends the standard Odoo eCommerce wishlist functionality by providing backend management and actionable insights into products that customers are interested in but have not yet purchased.

Key Features

  1. Backend Wishlist Visibility:

    • Adds a dedicated Wishlists menu under Website > eCommerce > Products.
    • Provides a structured list and form view of all wishlist items across the entire platform.
    • Allows administrators to see which partners have added which products to their wishlists.

  2. Stock Awareness:

    • Displays the Quantity On Hand of each product on a wishlist, computed when the list is shown, as the total stock of the warehouses of the companies selected in the company switcher (replenishment goes to the user's default warehouse).
    • Helps inventory managers identify demand for out-of-stock items.

  3. One-Click Replenishment:

    • Select wishlist lines in the list view and click Replenish.
    • Each selected line procures one unit of its product in the user's default warehouse, through the product route (a request for quotation with Buy, a manufacturing order with Manufacture).
Usage
  1. Go to Website > eCommerce > Products > Wishlists. The list shows every product your customers added to their wishlist, on all websites: the customer (Owner), the product, its Quantity On Hand and the price when it was added. Quantity On Hand is the stock of the warehouses of the companies selected in the company switcher. A product wished by several customers shows on one line per customer.
1 The Wishlists list under the eCommerce menu: columns Owner, Product, Quantity On Hand and Price; Lampă de birou LED is wished by three customers with 0.00 on hand, Scaun de birou ergonomic has 12.00 and Birou reglabil electric 2.00
  1. Click a line to open it. The form shows the same data. Quantity On Hand is computed each time it is displayed; it is not stored on the line.
2 The form of the wishlist line Ana Popescu - Lampă de birou LED: Owner Ana Popescu, Product Lampă de birou LED, Quantity On Hand 0.00 highlighted and Price 149.00
  1. Back in the list, select the lines of the products you want to bring in stock and click Replenish. For each selected line, the module procures one unit of the product in the stock location of the user's default warehouse, following the product route: a request for quotation with Buy (needs a vendor on the product) or a manufacturing order with Manufacture. If one selected product has no such route, Odoo shows an error and nothing is created for any selected line. A product with the Buy route but no vendor gets no request for quotation, without any message.
3 The Wishlists list with two lines selected, Lampă de birou LED for Ana Popescu and Birou reglabil electric for Mihai Ionescu, and the Replenish button highlighted next to 2 selected
  1. In Purchase > Orders > Requests for Quotation, with the vendor Group RFQ left on On Order (the default), each replenished line created its own request for quotation to the product vendor, with wishlist as source document. With Daily, Weekly or Always, the need is added to an open request of the vendor instead.
4 The Requests for Quotation list in Purchase: two new requests to Furnizor Mobilier SRL, in the RFQ status, with the order deadline Today and the totals 1,372.14 lei and 108.17 lei
  1. Open one of them: the line has a quantity of 1. Adjust the quantity if needed, then send or confirm it as usual. Clicking Replenish again creates new requests, it does not update the existing ones.
5 A request for quotation in the RFQ status: vendor Furnizor Mobilier SRL highlighted and the line Lampă de birou LED with Quantity 1.00 highlighted and the 21% VAT tax, total 108.17 lei
