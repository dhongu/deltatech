1. Go to **Settings > Users & Companies > Groups** and add the users who are allowed
   to change payment terms to the **Can modify payment terms** group (this group is
   hidden from the normal Users form, so it must be assigned from the Groups list).
2. Users who are *not* in this group will see the **Payment Term** field as read-only
   on:
   - the customer/vendor form (**Contacts > Sales & Purchase** tab, *Payment Terms*),
   - **Sales Orders** (Other Info tab),
   - **Invoices/Bills** (Other Info tab) — the **Due Date** field is likewise locked.
3. Users in the **Can modify payment terms** group keep full access and can pick or
   change the payment term (and the resulting due date) as before.
