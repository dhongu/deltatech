Overview

A technical module: a panel on the right of a kanban or a list that shows the form of the record the user selected, without leaving the screen.

  • One click selects
    — a click on a card or on a row shows the record in the panel and highlights it, instead of opening its full form. The panel has buttons to move to the previous or the next record, open the full form, refresh and close. A double click on a card or on a row opens the full form.
  • The form is yours
    — the panel embeds a form view, read-only, so a module defines what the panel shows in XML (header, fields, notebook, buttons) and the usual widgets work.
  • Kanban and list, one behaviour
    — the views side_panel_kanban and side_panel_list share the same panel and the same behaviour: a click selects the record in either view.
  • Resizable
    — drag the handle on the left edge of the panel to change its width; the width is remembered in the browser.
  • Small screens keep the usual views
    — below the extra-large breakpoint the click opens the form as before.
Usage

For a module developer:

  1. Give the view the js class: js_class="side_panel_kanban" on a <kanban>, or js_class="side_panel_list" on a <list>.
  2. Define the form the panel shows (a form view of the same model) and put its xml id in the context of the action: {'side_panel_view_ref': 'my_module.view_my_model_form_quick'}. Without it, the panel shows the default form view of the model.
  3. To switch the panel off for an action, add 'disable_side_panel': True to its context.

The demo data of this module has a working example on contacts: open the action Contacts with side panel (demo) (deltatech_web_side_panel.action_demo_partners).

For the user: click a card or a row to see the record on the right. Use the arrows in the header of the panel to move through the records, the arrow-out button, or a double click on the card or on the row, to open the full form and the cross to close the panel. To change the width of the panel, drag the handle on its left edge (or focus it and use the left and right arrow keys); a double click on the handle restores the default width. The chosen width is remembered in the browser.

Versions

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-11-13)

  • A double click on a card or on a row opens the full form of that record. A single click still selects it and shows it in the panel. Buttons, links and fields inside a card or a row keep their own behaviour, and on a small screen, where the panel is off, a click opens the form as before.

19.0.1.1.1 (2026-11-13)

  • The form of the panel follows the width of the panel on a wide window. On a window of 1500px or more the form sheet kept the width of the window (up to 1500px) and overflowed the panel: the right-hand fields were cut off and resizing the panel changed nothing in the form. The sheet and the levels above it may now shrink to the panel.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-11-13)

  • The side panel can be resized: drag the handle on its left edge (or use the arrow keys on it, Home and End for the limits). The width stays between 300 px and 70% of the content area, a double click on the handle restores the default width, and the chosen width is remembered in the browser for every view that uses the panel. On a wide window (extra-large and up) the form sheet now follows the width of the panel instead of keeping the width of its content.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

  • First release: a side panel with the form of the selected record, for kanban (side_panel_kanban) and list (side_panel_list) views. A click selects the record, the panel moves between records, opens the full form, refreshes and closes. The form comes from the action context key side_panel_view_ref.
