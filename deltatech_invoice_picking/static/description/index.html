Overview

  • Features:

    • an invoice can be created from one or more pickings
    • an invoice can be created from a batch
    • only the products in the picking(s) with their respective done quantities will be added to the invoice
    • only pickings that come from a sale order or purchase orders can be invoiced
    • a picking must be in "done" state to be invoiced
    • a "to invoice" field is added to pickings, for filtering purposes. When a picking that comes from a sale or purchase order is validated, the field is set to True. When an invoice is created, it is set to False. When the invoice is cancelled or deleted, it is set back to True and the link to the invoice is cleared
    • a field with link to the invoice is added to pickings. The field is computed to point to the invoice generated from the picking
    • on an invoice created from pickings, the quantity or product of storable product lines cannot be changed and those lines cannot be deleted, so the invoice stays aligned with the delivered quantities

Usage

Invoice a delivery

  1. Go to Inventory → Operations → Transfers → Deliveries and apply the To invoice filter. The list shows the done deliveries that are not invoiced yet, or only partly invoiced. To invoice several deliveries at once, select them and use Actions → Create Sale Invoices.

  1. Open a done delivery that comes from a sale order. The Invoice button is shown while the delivery is to invoice.

  1. Click Invoice. The standard Create invoice(s) wizard opens: keep Regular invoice and click Create Draft.

  1. The invoice contains only the products of the selected deliveries, with the delivered quantities (6 chairs out of the 10 ordered). Its product lines cannot be changed or deleted, so the invoice stays equal to the delivery.

  1. On the delivery, the Additional Info tab shows the invoice, and To invoice is cleared. If the invoice is cancelled or deleted, the delivery is to invoice again.

  1. A delivery that is only partly invoiced is linked to the invoice but stays To invoice, so the rest can be invoiced. The flag follows all the done moves of the transfer and the quantity invoiced on each order line (invoices minus refunds, cancelled ones excluded).

Bill a receipt

  1. In Inventory → Operations → Transfers → Receipts, open a done receipt that comes from a purchase order, fill in Supplier Invoice No and click Bill. Several receipts can be billed from the list with Actions → Create Purchase Invoices.

  1. A draft vendor bill is created with the received quantities. The supplier invoice number is copied to the purchase order as Vendor Reference and shown on the Purchases button of the bill; fill in Bill Reference and Bill Date before Confirm. A receipt that is already fully billed cannot be billed again.

Invoice a batch

Open a done batch in Inventory → Operations → Jobs → Batch Transfers and click Invoice. The transfers of the batch that are not invoiced, or only partly invoiced, go on the invoice; the fully invoiced ones are skipped. For a batch of receipts, fill in Supplier Invoice No on each receipt first. Batches need Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Batch, Wave & Cluster Transfers.

Refund a return

A validated return of an invoiced delivery is To invoice, with no invoice linked. Click Invoice on the return and Create Draft: Odoo creates the customer credit note with the returned quantity and links it to the return. A credit note made from the invoice with Credit Note does not update the return.

Invoice the ordered quantities

On the sale order, the Other Info tab has Force Invoice Order: the order lines are invoiced on the ordered quantities, whatever the invoicing policy of the product. When the invoice starts from a transfer, the quantities are still those of the transfer.
