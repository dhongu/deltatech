Overview

Shops often give a product a special price through a fixed-price pricelist rule and want customers to see the old price crossed out. This module makes the eCommerce shop treat fixed-price rules as rules whose discount is displayed.

  • Fixed-price rules
    A pricelist rule whose computation is a fixed price now always tells the shop to display its discount. Any other rule keeps the answer given by the standard code.
  • Where it applies
    According to the code comment, the change covers the shop, the product page and the product configurators, not the cart or the checkout.
  • Price comparison
    The module does not compare prices itself. Its summary says the cut-off price appears when the fixed price is lower than the list price.
  • No configuration
    The module adds no settings, fields, screens or menus. It applies to all pricelist rules as soon as it is installed.

The module depends on eCommerce (website_sale) and needs no access rights of its own.
