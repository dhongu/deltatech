Price Without Tax on Website
============================

A shop that sells to consumers and to companies usually shows prices
with tax, while a company buyer wants the net price. This module adds a
line with the price without tax to the product page of the website.

- **Net price line**: The price block of the product page gets a line
  *Price without tax* with the amount in the website currency. The line
  is hidden when the amount is zero.
- **Included taxes only**: The module takes the product taxes that are
  included in the price out of the ``list_price`` entry of the product
  price data. A tax that is not included leaves the amount unchanged,
  and the website display mode is not checked.
- **Display only**: The module adds a ``list_price_without_tax`` entry
  to the product price data and a template that shows it. Other prices,
  the cart, orders and invoices are not touched.
- **Optional view**: The line is an optional view called *Price without
  tax*, active after installation, which can be disabled for one
  website.

The module depends on Website and eCommerce (``website_sale``); every
visitor sees the line. The label is written in English in the template
and has a Romanian translation, *Preț fără TVA*. It reads
``list_price``, not ``price``, so test products that have a pricelist
discount.
