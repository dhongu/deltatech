Gives every eCommerce category its own **SVG icon** and shows it in the
shop.

- New field **Icon (SVG)** on the eCommerce category, with a live
  preview on the category form.
- The categories shown on top of the shop (filmstrip) display the icon
  instead of the category image.
- The left category list shows a small icon before the name of each
  category that has one (view **Category Icons in List**, can be
  archived).
- The icon is checked when saved, so it is safe to show on public pages:
  only flat shapes are accepted (``path``, ``rect``, ``circle``,
  ``ellipse``, ``line``, ``polyline``, ``polygon``, ``g``), with colors,
  numbers and ``transform``. Scripts, styles, links, external images and
  event handlers are refused with a message that says what to fix. The
  drawing needs a ``viewBox``; its size comes from the page.

The icons can be drawn by hand, generated in bulk by Terrabit in the
style of your shop, or written by any tool through the ``icon_svg``
field (import, XML-RPC).

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Website > eCommerce > Categories** and open a category.
2. Paste the SVG markup in **Icon (SVG)** and save. The preview shows
   the icon.
3. The left category list of the shop shows the icon before the category
   name.
4. To show the icons on top of the shop, open the shop in the website
   editor and turn on the categories filmstrip at the top of the page.
