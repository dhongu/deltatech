1.  Go to **Settings > Users & Companies > Credentials**.
2.  Click **New** and give the record a **Name** and optional **Code** to identify the external service.
3.  Choose the **Access Type**: *User and password*, *Client_id and client_secret*, or *Access token*, and fill in the corresponding fields (**Username**/**Password**, **Client Id**/**Client Secret**, or **Access Token**). Only the fields relevant to the selected type are shown.

This model is meant as a simple place to store service credentials used by other modules/integrations; it has no other automated behavior on its own.
