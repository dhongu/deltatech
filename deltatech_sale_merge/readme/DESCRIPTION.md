Merges several quotations of the same customer into one, from the quotations list.

Select two or more quotations, choose *Action > Merge orders* and pick the target: the oldest
selected quotation, one of the selected ones, or a new quotation. Identical lines (same product,
description, unit of measure, price, discount, taxes and analytic distribution) can be added up, or
all lines can be kept. The merged quotations are cancelled, not deleted, and linked to the target;
the chatter of each one records what happened.

The merge stops with a clear message, and nothing is changed, if the quotations differ in company,
customer or its addresses, currency, pricelist, fiscal position, payment terms, warehouse or
incoterm, or if one was already signed or has an online payment started.
