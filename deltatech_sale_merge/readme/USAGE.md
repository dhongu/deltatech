1. Go to *Sales > Orders > Quotations* and select two or more quotations of the same customer.
2. Choose *Action > Merge orders*.
3. Choose where to merge (*The oldest selected quotation*, *A selected quotation* or *A new quotation*)
   and how to treat the lines (*Add up identical lines* or *Keep all lines*). Tick *Add a Section per
   Quotation* to see which lines came from which quotation.
4. Click *Merge*. The target quotation opens; the others are cancelled and show *Merged Into*.

When identical lines are added up, the unit price may be recomputed by the pricelist for the new
quantity (quantity breaks).
