Salespeople and accountants often need to call a customer while they work on a quotation or an invoice. This module shows the customer's phone number on the sales order form and on the invoice form, so nobody has to open the contact first.

- **Phone on sales orders**: A *Phone* field appears right after the customer on the sales order form, displayed with the phone widget.
- **Phone on invoices**: The same *Phone* field appears in the left header group of the invoice form, after the delivery address field.
- **Read from the customer**: The value is the *phone* of the customer on the document and the field stays empty when the customer has none. It is read-only: to change the number, edit the customer.
- **Not stored**: The number is not saved on the document. It is read from the customer when the record is loaded, also on posted invoices.

The module depends on *Sales* and *Invoicing*. It adds no menus, settings or groups, and it changes neither list views nor printed documents.
