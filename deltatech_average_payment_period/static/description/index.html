Overview

Tracks how long customers and suppliers actually take to pay, giving finance teams a concrete measure of cash-collection and payment efficiency.

For each customer invoice or vendor bill line fully reconciled with its payment, the module records the exact payment date and computes the number of days elapsed since the accounting date of the invoice. An aggregated pivot/graph report — Average Payment Period — is added directly to the Accounting Reports menu so managers can slice the data by partner, journal, or period at a glance.

✨ Key features:

  • Payment Date
    — automatically derived from the reconciliation partner line and stored on each account.move.line.
  • Payment Days
    — difference (in days) between the accounting date of the invoice and the accounting date of the payment.
  • Plain Payment Days
    — simplified variant computed only on customer invoices (out_invoice) and vendor bills (in_invoice); reconciled credit notes count with 0 days in its average.
  • Average Payment Period report
    — a dedicated pivot and graph view (menu: Invoicing / Accounting > Reporting > Average Payment Period) grouped by partner by default; searchable by partner, reference and date, and groupable by partner or date (other fields through a custom group).
  • Weighted average calculation
    — when grouping rows, in the pivot and in the graph, Payment Days is averaged as the absolute value of sum(amount × days) / sum(amount) rather than a plain arithmetic mean, preventing distortion from small-amount outliers.
Usage

Before you start

The report only counts invoices that are fully reconciled with their payment. Make sure the payment methods of your bank journal have an account set (Invoicing, or Accounting on Enterprise, → Configuration → Journals → Bank, Incoming Payments and Outgoing Payments tabs): without it, a payment creates no journal entry, the invoice stays In Payment and does not reach the report.

1. Pay an invoice in full

Open a posted customer invoice or vendor bill and click Pay. When the payment covers the whole invoice, the receivable or payable line is reconciled with the payment and the module stores the payment date and the number of days automatically.

1 Customer invoice of Client Alfa SRL dated Aug 5 for 2,420.00, with 30 days payment terms, the Paid ribbon and the payment of 09/04/2026, 30 days after the invoice date
  • Payment Days = accounting date of the payment − accounting date of the invoice.
  • If the invoice is paid in several instalments, the date of the payment that closes it is kept.
  • If the reconciliation is removed (on the invoice, the ⓘ icon next to Paid on … → Unreconcile), the payment date and days are cleared and the invoice leaves the report; a new full reconciliation computes them again.

2. Read the report by partner

Go to Invoicing (or Accounting on Enterprise) → Reporting → Average Payment Period. The pivot opens grouped by Partner:

  • Balance — total of the paid invoices: positive for customers, negative for vendors (the grand total mixes both, filter customers or vendors to read it);
  • Payment Days — average days to payment, weighted by the invoice amount;
  • Plain payment days — simple average of the days, computed on customer invoices and vendor bills; a reconciled credit note counts with 0 days.
2 Average Payment Period pivot grouped by partner with the Balance, Payment Days and Plain payment days columns

Example: an invoice of 1,210.00 paid in 10 days and one of 2,420.00 paid in 30 days give Payment Days 23.33 and Plain payment days 20.00. Unpaid or partially paid invoices are not included. Use the Date filter of the search bar to select the period, and Download xlsx to export the table.

3. Break it down by month

In the same pivot, click the Total column header and choose Date → Month: each partner gets one column per month, with the average days of the invoices of that month.

3 Average Payment Period pivot with partners as rows and the invoice months as columns

4. Compare partners on a graph

Switch to the Graph view: it opens on the Payment Days measure, with one bar per partner for its weighted average days to payment, the same values as in the pivot.

4 Average Payment Period bar graph with one bar per partner for the Payment Days measure
