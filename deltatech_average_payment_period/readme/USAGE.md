1. No special configuration is required; the module extends the accounting views and adds a report.
2. Go to **Accounting > Reporting** (or the menu added by this module) to open the **Average Payment Period** report.
3. The report computes, for customer and supplier invoices:

   - **Payment Days**: the difference between invoice date and payment date, weighted by the paid amount.
   - **Payment Days Simple**: the difference between invoice date and payment date for invoices only (credit notes excluded).
4. Use this to track how quickly customers pay you and how quickly you pay suppliers, on average.
