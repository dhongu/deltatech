This module adds a button on Sales Orders and Invoices to quickly view
the products included in the lines.

Features:
~~~~~~~~~

- **Sale Order Integration**: Adds a button to display products from the
  order lines in a kanban view, showing available quantities (stock
  minus reserved).
- **Invoice Integration**: Adds a button to display products from the
  invoice lines in a kanban view, showing current stock levels.
- **Warehouse Specifics**: Information is filtered per warehouse for
  better inventory management.
- **Stock Visibility**: Enhances the user's ability to see real-time
  product availability directly from the sales or invoicing workflow.
