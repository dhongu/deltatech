Stock Inventory Product Display

  • Features:
    • Adds 2 widgets to view the product: - Invoice the button display the products from the invoice lines in kanban view with the quantities that are in stock - Sale the button will display the products from the order lines in kanban view with the free quantities (stock - reserved)
    • The info is separated per warehouse
