1. Open a **Sale Order** (in Sent, Sales Order or Done/Locked status) or a
   **Customer Invoice / Vendor Bill**.
2. Click the new **Products** button in the top-right status button box.
3. A kanban view opens showing the products from the order/invoice lines:
   - On the invoice, quantities shown are the current stock on hand.
   - On the sale order, quantities shown are the free-to-use quantity
     (stock minus reserved), so you can quickly see what is actually
     available to ship.
4. Stock is broken down per warehouse in the kanban cards.
