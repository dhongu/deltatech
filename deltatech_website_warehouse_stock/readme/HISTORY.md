## 19.0.0.0.7 (2026-10-22)

- After the price also with the boxed call to action: the block goes right under the box
  that holds the price, instead of falling back to the end of the product details.

## 19.0.0.0.6 (2026-10-22)

- Position of the block per website: in the product details (default, as before) or right
  after the price. After the price falls back to the details when the price is not above
  the Add to Cart button (boxed call to action, product that cannot be added to the cart).
- The design options are also in the website editor: click the block on the product page
  and change Position, Style, At zero stock, Quantity and Theme colors. They are the
  settings of the website, the same as in Website > Configuration > Settings.
- The block is its own template (Warehouse Stock Block), called from the product page.

## 19.0.0.0.5 (2026-10-21)

- Design options per website (Website > Configuration > Settings, Warehouse Stock Design):
  style (badges as before, list with icons, card, warehouse selector with a "Check stock"
  button), message at zero stock ("Out of stock" or "On order" with an optional note),
  quantity display (exact below the threshold or status only) and theme colors (badges
  tinted on the page colors, readable on dark themes). The defaults keep the previous look.
- The block is no longer shown when no warehouse is displayed on the website; the
  template is named "Warehouse Stock" in the Customize menu (was "Alternative code").
- The quantity below the threshold is shown without decimals when whole (4, not 4.0).
