- **Access rights.** The commission groups are not assigned to anyone on installation, not even the administrator. In **Settings → Users**, give **Commission Manager** to those who compute commissions and **Commission Viewer** to those who only consult them. Until then, the *Commission* menu is hidden; *Profit Analysis* (from `deltatech_sale_margin`) is shown to the cost group and the Sales administrators. The viewer group shows cost and margin, so grant it only to people allowed to see costs. To see the cost on invoices, also add the cost group from `deltatech_sale_margin`. Only **Commission Manager** can change the real commission and *Commission Paid*; the Sales administrators can change the cost.
- **Who earns the commission.** In **Settings → Sales → Invoicing**, set **Salesperson commission compute** to *Invoice* or *Sales Order*. Do not enable the standard *Commissions* option below it: it is a different mechanism.
- **Rates.** In **Sales → Configuration → Users Commission**, add one row per agent and sales journal, with the agent, manager and director rates as fractions of the profit (0.100 = 10%).
- **Payment on time (optional).** In **Settings → Technical → System Parameters** (developer mode), create `deltatech_sale_commission.days_for_commission` with the maximum number of days between due date and last payment, as an integer (0 = paid by the due date). Without it, commission is granted whether or not the invoice is paid.
