1. Go to **Inventory → Operations → Adjustments → Disassembly** and create a report.
2. Select the disassembled product, its lot or serial number, the quantity and the source location.
   The estimated cost is displayed.
3. Choose the allocation method and add the results:
   - *Product* lines enter the stock (destination location, lot or new lot name);
   - *Loss* lines (technological loss, non-recoverable waste) go to the loss location; tick
     **Above Norm** for the loss above the technological norm and give it a value (fixed or
     percentage);
   - tick **Fixed** and enter a value to keep it whatever the method (recoverable waste kept in
     stock, at its net realisable value).
4. Fill in the reason and the committee, then **Validate**. The product leaves the stock at its
   actual cost, the value is split over the lines (the last line takes the rounding difference),
   the results enter the stock and the journal entry is posted.
5. **Print** the disassembly report for signature. The stock moves and the journal entry are
   available from the buttons on the report.
