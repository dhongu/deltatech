Overview

Overview

A disassembly report takes one product out of stock and brings in the products obtained from it, without a bill of materials or a manufacturing order. The cost of the disassembled product is split over the resulting products, and the module posts one journal entry for the whole report.

Features

  • Numbered document
    , draft → done (PVD/<year>/<number>), with the committee, the reason and a printable PDF report with signatures.
  • Disassembled product
    taken from an internal location, with its lot or serial number, at its actual cost: average cost, FIFO layers or lot cost, as computed by the stock valuation. Only the own stock of the company can be disassembled, and never more than is available.
  • Resulting products
    entered freely, each with its quantity, unit, destination location and lot (an existing one or a new lot name, created at validation).
  • Cost allocation
    by quantity, by reference price × quantity, by percentages or by fixed values. Lines with a fixed value (for example recoverable waste at its net realisable value) are deducted first; rounding differences go to the last line. The resulting moves enter the stock exactly at their allocated value (average cost is updated, FIFO and lot layers are created).
  • Loss lines
    (technological loss, non-recoverable waste) sent to a loss location: within the technological norm they get no value (the cost stays in the resulting products); above the norm they must get a value and are booked as an expense, with a reminder in the chatter and on the PDF about the non-deductible expense and the input VAT adjustment.
  • One journal entry per report
    in the stock journal, at the date of the report, instead of one entry per stock move. The lines carry the product and the quantity, and every stock move of the report is linked to the entry.
  • Romanian chart of accounts
    (fiscal country Romania), by synthetic account:
    • stock to stock entry on the same synthetic account (371 → 371, 302 → 302) and where the chart provides the correspondence: own products used as materials or sold as goods (341 → 301/302, 345 → 301/302/303/371, 347 → 301/302/371) and materials sold as goods (30x → 371); the lines on the same account are not netted, for the audit trail;
    • resulting products on 34x (for example residual products, 346) enter through 711: the disassembled product leaves through its expense account (60x/607, or 711 for own products);
    • goods turned into materials (371 → 30x) and materials of another kind (301 ↔ 302 ↔ 303) only when the company allows it in the settings, as part of its accounting policy;
    • any other pair of accounts (for example residual products 346 into materials or goods) is refused before the stock is moved.
  • ✓
    The disassembled product and the resulting products must have the same valuation (automated or manual), so that the journal entry is complete.
  • ✓
    Works with the Romanian localization of stock accounting (accounts per location are used), and without it.

Configuration

  1. Go to Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Valuation.
  2. Romanian companies only: enable Disassembly: reclassify goods and materials if the accounting policy allows goods (371) to become materials (30x), or materials of one kind to become another (301 ↔ 302 ↔ 303), through a disassembly report.
  3. Check that the categories of the products have a stock valuation account and an expense account, and that the company has a stock journal.

Usage

  1. Go to Inventory → Operations → Adjustments → Disassembly and create a report.
  2. Select the disassembled product, its lot or serial number, the quantity and the source location. The estimated cost is displayed.
  3. Choose the allocation method and add the results:
    • Product lines enter the stock (destination location, lot or new lot name);
    • Loss lines (technological loss, non-recoverable waste) go to the loss location; tick Above Norm for the loss above the technological norm and give it a value (fixed or percentage);
    • tick Fixed and enter a value to keep it whatever the method (recoverable waste kept in stock, at its net realisable value).
  4. Fill in the reason and the committee, then Validate. The product leaves the stock at its actual cost, the value is split over the lines (the last line takes the rounding difference), the results enter the stock and the journal entry is posted.
  5. Print the disassembly report for signature. The stock moves and the journal entry are available from the buttons on the report.

Versions

20.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-12)

  • Migration to Odoo 20: the value of a stock move is signed (negative for the disassembled product), _get_value_from_production no longer takes at_date, the moves and move lines use uom_id, the quantities are compared with uom.compare (uom.uom.rounding is gone), a product without tracking has tracking empty (no more 'none') and the access rights are in ir.access.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-12)

  • New module: disassembly report without a bill of materials. The disassembled product leaves the stock at its actual cost, which is split over the resulting products (by quantity, reference price, percentages or fixed values, rounding on the last line); losses within or above the norm; one journal entry per report, with the Romanian account correspondences by synthetic account (34x through 711, 371 → 30x and 30x → 30y only by company setting, other pairs refused); PDF report with the signatures of the committee.
