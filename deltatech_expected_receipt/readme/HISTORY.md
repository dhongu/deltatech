## 19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-12)

- Tests: the test suite now runs on the generic chart of accounts, so it also runs
  where the Romanian localization is not installed; the Romanian down payment
  entry (419 / 4427) is checked in a separate test class. New tests for the card
  payment dialog (defaults, warnings, refusals), the settlement search and its
  refusals, the register (cancellation, synchronization with accounting, late
  receipts) and the card terminals. No change in behaviour.

## 19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-11)

- Docs: emoji symbols added to the bold headings of the module description, for a uniform look on the Odoo Apps page. No code change.

## 19.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-06)

- Consultant sheet (FISA_CONSULTANT.md) with screenshots generated by
  tests/test_screenshots.py.
- The messages and the configuration guide now point to the right place of the
  Downpayment Account: Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, Default Accounts
  (not the Sales settings).

## 19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-06)

- First version. Card payments registered when received (5125 = 4111) from sales
  orders, invoices and customers, with a down payment invoice on unbilled
  orders; Expected Receipts register; settlement of the grouped bank transfer
  by exact combination; card terminals; cashier group without accounting
  rights. Down payment invoices require the Down Payment Account (419) to be
  set. Based on the cash and bank module written by Alexandru Grecu for MD
  Trade Concept SRL.
