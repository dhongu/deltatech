1. Go to **Inventory > Reporting > Picking Analysis**.
2. The report lists every stock move line with its picking, date, source/destination location, category, operation type, partner and amount.
3. Use the search filters to narrow by Incoming, Internal or Outgoing operation type, or group by picking type, partner, category, product, location or date.
4. Switch between the pivot and list views to analyze quantities and amounts, e.g. total quantity moved per partner per month.
