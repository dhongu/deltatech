1. Go to **Website > eCommerce > Pricelists** (or **Sales > Products > Pricelists**) and open a pricelist.
2. From the action (gear) menu, select **Open Pricelist Wizard**.
3. Choose the **Public Category** to assign, and decide whether to also apply it to **Children Categories**.
4. Click **Confirm**: the wizard scans the pricelist's rule lines and adds the chosen public category to every product they apply to — matching products, product templates, or entire product categories (including sub-categories when that option is checked). Rules that apply globally (all products) are skipped.
