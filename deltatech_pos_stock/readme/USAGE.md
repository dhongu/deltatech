1. Go to **Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings**, open a POS configuration.
2. In the **Point of Sale Interface** section, enable **Show stock** to display each storable product's real-time available quantity as a badge on its product card, and/or enable **Show price** to display the price on the same badge.
3. Open the POS session: product cards now show the enabled badge(s) directly on the product image. Only storable products show a stock badge.
