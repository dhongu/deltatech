Overview

Shoppers like to start with what others buy, visit and rate highly, or with what is in stock. This module adds five such criteria to the product sort options of the website and keeps their figures up to date with a daily scheduled action.

  • New sort options
    Best sellers, Most Visited, Reviews, Best Review and Availability join the website's product sort list, each with the highest value first.
  • Sales and visits
    The daily action reads the quantity sold in the last 365 days from the sales report (done states) and the visits recorded for each product page. Best sellers and Most Visited sort on these figures.
  • Ratings
    Reviews orders products by the number of ratings submitted for them, and Best Review by their average.
  • Stock status
    Availability puts first the products whose on-hand quantity is above zero.
  • Daily refresh
    The scheduled action Products: Update products statistics recomputes the figures once a day for every product and variant, in batches of 2,000 (queued jobs when queue_job is installed).

It depends on eCommerce (website_sale) and Inventory. The figures are stored on products and variants, so new sales, visits and ratings count in the sort only after the next run; archived products are skipped. The action runs as the system user and the module has no security groups.
