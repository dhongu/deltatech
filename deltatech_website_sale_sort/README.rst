Shoppers like to start with what others buy, visit and rate highly, or
with what is in stock. This module adds five such criteria to the
product sort options of the website and keeps the figures behind them up
to date with a daily scheduled action.

- **New sort options**: *Best sellers*, *Most Visited*, *Reviews*, *Best
  Review* and *Availability* join the website's product sort list, each
  with the highest value first.
- **Sales and visits**: *Best sellers* uses the quantity sold in the
  last 365 days, read from the sales report for its done states. *Most
  Visited* uses the visits recorded for the product page.
- **Ratings**: *Reviews* orders products by the number of ratings
  submitted for them, and *Best Review* by their average.
- **Stock status**: *Availability* puts first the products whose on-hand
  quantity is above zero.
- **Daily refresh**: The scheduled action *Products: Update products
  statistics* recomputes the figures once a day for every active
  product, in batches of 2,000 products, as queued jobs when
  ``queue_job`` is installed. Each batch first refreshes all variants of
  its products, archived ones included, so the product totals never use
  stale variant figures.

It depends on *eCommerce* (``website_sale``) and *Inventory*. The
figures are stored on products and variants, so new sales, visits and
ratings count in the sort only after the next run; archived products are
skipped, while the archived variants of an active product still count in
its totals. The action runs as the system user and the module has no
security groups.

Usage
=====

The figures behind the new sort options are computed once a day by a
scheduled action. Until its first run they are all zero, and the new
options do not change the order of the products.

1. Open the shop (the *Shop* menu of the website, or ``/shop``) and
   click *Sort By*. Below the standard Odoo options, the module adds
   *Best sellers*, *Most Visited*, *Reviews*, *Best Review* and
   *Availability*. Every visitor of the website sees the same list.

   |The Shop page with the Sort By list open: below Featured, Newest
   Arrivals, Name (A-Z) and the two price sorts, the options Best
   sellers, Most Visited, Reviews, Best Review and Availability are
   highlighted|

2. Pick *Best sellers*. The *Sort By* button shows the chosen option,
   and the first products are the ones with the highest quantity on
   confirmed sales orders in the last 365 days. The other options work
   the same way: *Most Visited* uses the visits on the product page,
   *Reviews* and *Best Review* the number and the average of the
   ratings, *Availability* the on-hand stock.

   |The Shop page sorted by Best sellers: the Sort By button shows the
   chosen option and the first three products are Scaun de birou
   ergonomic, Birou reglabil electric and Lampă de birou LED, in the
   order of the quantities sold|

3. To make one of them the default sort of the shop, click *Editor* (top
   left) on the *Shop* page, then *Edit*, and click the product grid. In
   the right panel, on the *Style* tab, choose it in *Default Sort* and
   click *Save*. Visitors who do not pick a sort themselves now see the
   products in that order.

   |The website editor on the Shop page: on the Style tab of the right
   panel, the Default Sort field under Toolbar is highlighted, with the
   value Featured|

4. With developer mode on, go to *Settings > Technical > Automation >
   Scheduled Actions* and open *Products: Update products statistics*.
   It runs once a day and recomputes, for every active product, the
   sales and visits of its variants (archived ones included, refreshed
   in the same batch before they are summed), the ratings and the stock.
   *Run Manually* computes the figures at once, which is useful right
   after installing the module. With ``queue_job`` installed the work is
   split into queued jobs, and the new order shows once they are done.

   |The form of the scheduled action Products: Update products
   statistics: model Product, Execute Every 1 Days highlighted and the
   Run Manually button highlighted|
