One dashboard for the ten technical pillars of World Class Manufacturing
(WCM): Safety, Cost Deployment, Focused Improvement, Autonomous
Maintenance, Planned Maintenance, Quality Control, Logistics and
Customer Service, Early Equipment Management, People Development,
Environment and Energy.

Each pillar groups its indicators. Each indicator has a target, a
warning threshold and a traffic light: green, yellow, red, or "no data".
The pillar shows the worst state of its indicators.

Indicators read from Odoo:

- OEE availability, from the work centers (``mrp``);
- MTBF and MTTR, from the maintenance equipment (``maintenance``);
- quality control failure rate (``quality``) and scrap rate (``stock``,
  ``mrp``);
- on-time deliveries, from the outgoing transfers (``stock``).

The other indicators, including the Safety and Focused Improvement
(Kaizen) placeholders, are entered manually until a dedicated module
provides them.

The seed targets are placeholders: confirm them with the plant before
use.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.1.0
----------

- Unit production cost: standard (BoM) vs actual (MO Overview) per
  product, cost of each loss in lei per unit (scrap, stoppages,
  changeovers, energy, waste), manual energy and waste costs per work
  center, and the indicator "Unit cost deviation" on the Cost Deployment
  pillar.
- New dependencies: ``mrp_account``, ``mrp_workorder``.

20.0.1.0.0
----------

- First version: ten WCM pillars, configurable indicators with target,
  warning threshold and traffic light, live sources from ``mrp``,
  ``maintenance``, ``quality`` and ``stock``, manual placeholders for
  Safety and Kaizen.
