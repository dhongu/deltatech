Technical module: one place for the rules that decide whether two
partner records are the same, so the modules that look for an existing
partner (marketplace import, duplicate finder, trial balance import)
recognize the same partner the same way.

It adds no fields, data, menus, scheduled actions or record rules, and
changes no behavior by itself: it only provides functions that other
modules call.

**Normalization** (``deltatech_partner_match.tools.partner_match``,
plain Python):

- **Name**: lowercase, without diacritics, punctuation and legal forms
  (one list for Romania and the EU: SRL, SA, PFA, II, GmbH, Ltd, SARL,
  KFT...), words sorted. "S.C. Popescu Ion S.R.L." and "ion popescu srl"
  give the same key.
- **VAT**: alphanumerics only, without the country prefix and the
  leading zeros ("RO 012.345" = "12345"); the check digit of a Romanian
  CUI or CNP, or of an EU VAT number, with ``python-stdnum``.
- **Phone**: E.164 (``+40722123456``) with the same rule Odoo uses for
  ``phone_sanitized`` (a possible and valid number, with
  ``phonenumbers``); without the library, the Romanian formats and the
  numbers written with an international prefix. For bulk comparison, the
  last nine digits.
- **Email**: Odoo's own ``email_normalize``.

**Matching**:

- ``name_similarity`` (0-1) pairs every word with the closest word of
  the other name, so a shared surname alone does not make two people
  alike: use it to decide whether two records are the same partner.
- ``token_sort_ratio`` and ``similar_pairs`` (0-100) compare whole
  names, in bulk; ``rapidfuzz`` is used when installed, ``difflib``
  otherwise, on limited blocks. Use them to propose pairs for review.
- ``res.partner._find_similar_partners(values, domain, keys, limit)``
  selects the partners that share the VAT number, the email or the phone
  of ``values`` (never a name alone), and returns them with a score and
  the keys they share. Whether one is reused is decided by the calling
  module.

``phonenumbers``, ``rapidfuzz`` and ``unidecode`` are optional: the
results stay the same without them on the exact keys, and slightly
coarser on the phones of other countries.

Usage
=====

For developers. Add ``deltatech_partner_match`` to ``depends``, then:

.. code:: python

   from odoo.addons.deltatech_partner_match.tools import partner_match as pm

   pm.normalize_name("S.C. Popescu Ion S.R.L.")  # "ion popescu"
   pm.normalize_vat("RO 012.345")  # "12345"
   pm.check_vat("RO18547290")  # True
   pm.normalize_phone("0722 123 456")  # "+40722123456"
   pm.phone_key("+40 722 123 456")  # "722123456"
   pm.name_similarity("Ana Popescu", "Ion Popescu")  # 0.67

   candidates = self.env["res.partner"]._find_similar_partners(
       {"name": "Ion Popescu", "phone": "0722 123 456", "email": "ion@example.com", "city": "Cluj"},
       domain=[("is_company", "=", False)],
   )
   # [{"partner": res.partner(42,), "score": 0.94, "name_score": 1.0, "reasons": ["phone"]}, ...]

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- The legal forms "SAS", "AG", "BV", "NV" and "RA" are no longer dropped
  from the names: on a database of 551,000 partners they removed the
  surname Sas ("Sas Vasile" became "vasile") and initials ("INSTALCO
  A.G. SRL", "BO & RA TRANS SRL"). KFT, SARL, s.r.o., SPRL and CMI stay.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- Add: shared normalization and fuzzy matching of partners (name, VAT,
  phone, email), and ``res.partner._find_similar_partners``, which
  selects candidates on the VAT number, the email or the phone and
  scores them, without deciding anything. One list of legal forms
  (Romania and EU), the phone rule of Odoo's ``phone_validation``
  (possible and valid number), the CUI / CNP / EU VAT check with
  ``python-stdnum``. ``phonenumbers``, ``rapidfuzz`` and ``unidecode``
  are optional.
- Installed with ``deltatech_partner_merge`` and
  ``deltatech_marketplace`` once they use it: an accepted exception to
  the rule of no new dependency on modules installed at customers, since
  this module adds no fields, data, scheduled actions or record rules
  and changes no behavior by itself.
