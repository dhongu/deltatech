Overview

Technical module: one place for the rules that decide whether two partner records are the same, so the modules that look for an existing partner (marketplace import, duplicate finder, trial balance import) recognize the same partner the same way.

It adds no fields, data, menus, scheduled actions or record rules, and changes no behavior by itself: it only provides functions that other modules call.

Normalization (deltatech_partner_match.tools.partner_match, plain Python):

  • Name
    lowercase, without diacritics, punctuation and legal forms (one list for Romania and the EU: SRL, SA, PFA, II, GmbH, Ltd, SARL, KFT...), words sorted. "S.C. Popescu Ion S.R.L." and "ion popescu srl" give the same key.
  • VAT
    alphanumerics only, without the country prefix and the leading zeros ("RO 012.345" = "12345"); the check digit of a Romanian CUI or CNP, or of an EU VAT number, with python-stdnum.
  • Phone
    E.164 (+40722123456) with the same rule Odoo uses for phone_sanitized (a possible and valid number, with phonenumbers); without the library, the Romanian formats and the numbers written with an international prefix. For bulk comparison, the last nine digits.
  • Email
    Odoo's own email_normalize.

Matching:

  • ✓
    name_similarity (0-1) pairs every word with the closest word of the other name, so a shared surname alone does not make two people alike: use it to decide whether two records are the same partner.
  • ✓
    token_sort_ratio and similar_pairs (0-100) compare whole names, in bulk; rapidfuzz is used when installed, difflib otherwise, on limited blocks. Use them to propose pairs for review.
  • ✓
    res.partner._find_similar_partners(values, domain, keys, limit) selects the partners that share the VAT number, the email or the phone of values (never a name alone), and returns them with a score and the keys they share. When both sides name a different county, the street and the city do not count: the same village name exists in several counties. Whether one is reused is decided by the calling module.

phonenumbers, rapidfuzz and unidecode are optional: the results stay the same without them on the exact keys, and slightly coarser on the phones of other countries.

Usage

For developers. Add deltatech_partner_match to depends, then:

from odoo.addons.deltatech_partner_match.tools import partner_match as pm

pm.normalize_name("S.C. Popescu Ion S.R.L.")  # "ion popescu"
pm.normalize_vat("RO 012.345")  # "12345"
pm.check_vat("RO18547290")  # True
pm.normalize_phone("0722 123 456")  # "+40722123456"
pm.phone_key("+40 722 123 456")  # "722123456"
pm.name_similarity("Ana Popescu", "Ion Popescu")  # 0.67

candidates = self.env["res.partner"]._find_similar_partners(
    {"name": "Ion Popescu", "phone": "0722 123 456", "email": "ion@example.com", "city": "Cluj"},
    domain=[("is_company", "=", False)],
)
# [{"partner": res.partner(42,), "score": 0.94, "name_score": 1.0, "reasons": ["phone"]}, ...]

Versions

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-10-24)

  • Add name_words: the name without diacritics, punctuation and legal forms, with the words in their order, for an exact name key where the order matters ("Alfa Beta" and "Beta Alfa" can be two companies). normalize_name is the same with the words sorted. Used by the trial balance import.

19.0.1.1.1 (2026-10-23)

  • Development status Production/Stable: deltatech_marketplace (Production/Stable) depends on this module, and a stable module cannot depend on a Beta one (manifestoo check-dev-status in CI). The rules are those the marketplace import and the duplicate finder already use in production.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-10-23)

  • The county counts in the score of _find_similar_partners: when both sides name a county (state_id, or a code or name in county / state_code, as the marketplace connectors send it) and it is not the same, the street and the city score 0. A village name is shared by up to 23 counties ("Satu Nou", "Poiana", "Dumbrava": 1,411 of the 10,565 Romanian locality names), so the same street and village in another county is another address. The score can only go down: a county that matches no state, or a missing one, leaves it as before.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-23)

  • normalize_text and jaccard_similarity (street and city in _find_similar_partners) split the words on dots again, as the marketplace import did: "Str.Memorandumului 10" and "Str. Memorandumului 10" scored 0.25 on the street instead of 1. The name, street and city keys of the duplicate finder are unchanged.
  • Apps Store banner (banner.json), instead of the generic screenshot.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-23)

  • The legal forms "SAS", "AG", "BV", "NV" and "RA" are no longer dropped from the names: on a database of 551,000 partners they removed the surname Sas ("Sas Vasile" became "vasile") and initials ("INSTALCO A.G. SRL", "BO & RA TRANS SRL"). KFT, SARL, s.r.o., SPRL and CMI stay.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

  • Add: shared normalization and fuzzy matching of partners (name, VAT, phone, email), and res.partner._find_similar_partners, which selects candidates on the VAT number, the email or the phone and scores them, without deciding anything. One list of legal forms (Romania and EU), the phone rule of Odoo's phone_validation (possible and valid number), the CUI / CNP / EU VAT check with python-stdnum. phonenumbers, rapidfuzz and unidecode are optional.
  • Installed with deltatech_partner_merge and deltatech_marketplace once they use it: an accepted exception to the rule of no new dependency on modules installed at customers, since this module adds no fields, data, scheduled actions or record rules and changes no behavior by itself.
