Overview

Deltatech Delivery Status

This module extends the functionality of the delivery system in Odoo to provide enhanced tracking and status management for shipments.

Features

  • Adds detailed delivery status tracking to stock pickings
  • Lets you postpone and release the deliveries of a sales order
  • Lets sales teams postpone the deliveries of their orders paid by wire transfer
  • Extends payment providers with a Postponed Delivery option
  • Keeps the carrier status of the parcel on the delivery, updated by the shipping integrations
  • Enables monitoring of delivery status throughout the logistics process
  • Improves communication between sales, warehouse, and customers regarding shipment status
  • Centralizes delivery information for better visibility across the organization

Delivery Blocking Features

  • Manual Delivery Blocking
    Allows users to manually postpone deliveries by marking pickings as postponed
  • Payment Provider Based Blocking
    Automatically postpones deliveries based on payment provider configuration
    • Each payment provider can be configured with the "Postponed Delivery" option
    • When enabled, deliveries are automatically blocked when the order is confirmed before the payment is done
    • Once payment is confirmed, the system automatically releases the blocked deliveries
  • Sales Team Configuration
    Sales teams can be configured to postpone the deliveries of the orders paid by wire transfer
    • Special handling for wire transfers, allowing businesses to verify payment before shipping; these deliveries are released manually, from the order
  • Order-level Management
    Provides functions to postpone or release deliveries at the sales order level
  • Status Visibility
    Shows the postponed status on pickings (ribbon and field) and, on orders, through the Release button and the Postponed delivery filter

Delivery State Tracking

The module introduces a comprehensive delivery_state field on stock pickings that tracks the package's journey through the entire logistics process:

  • Draft
    Initial state when the delivery is just created
  • Ready in warehouse
    The package is prepared and waiting in the company's warehouse
  • Pre advice
    The delivery note/AWB has been generated but the carrier hasn't picked up the package yet
  • In Transit
    The package has been picked up by the carrier and is on its way
  • In Carrier Warehouse
    The package has arrived at the carrier's sorting facility/warehouse
  • In delivery
    The package is out for final delivery to the customer
  • Delivered
    The package has been successfully delivered to the customer
  • Refused
    The customer refused the package

This granular state tracking provides several benefits:

  • Allows customer service to give precise information about delivery status
  • Helps identify bottlenecks in the logistics process
  • Enables better coordination between warehouse operations and delivery carriers
  • Provides data for analytics on delivery performance and efficiency
  • Improves customer experience with more accurate delivery status information

The module also adds an available_state field that visually indicates the availability status of products in pickings:

  • Available
    All products are available and ready for delivery
  • Partially available
    Some products are available, but not all
  • Unavailable
    The products are not reserved (for transfers delivered all at once, also when they are only partially reserved)

This module is essential for businesses that need to closely monitor the status of their deliveries and want to provide accurate tracking information to their customers. It enhances the standard Odoo delivery system by adding more granular status tracking, payment-dependent delivery control, and better integration between the sales, warehouse, and payment processes.

Usage

Configure the postponed delivery

  1. On a payment provider (Accounting → Configuration → Online Payments), tick Postponed Delivery. When an order paid through this provider is confirmed before the payment is done, its deliveries are postponed; they are released automatically when the payment is confirmed.
1 Payment provider form with the Postponed Delivery option ticked
  1. On a sales team (Sales → Configuration → Teams), tick Postpone delivery for transfer payment to postpone the deliveries of the team's orders paid by wire transfer. These deliveries are released manually, once the payment is received.
2 Sales team form with the Postpone delivery for transfer payment option ticked
  1. Optionally, Postponed on an operation type makes the transfers created manually from that operation type start postponed. The system parameter backorders.postponed = True does the same for the backorders.
3 Delivery Orders operation type with the Postponed option

Postpone and release a delivery

  1. On a confirmed sale order, click Postpone: all the deliveries of the order that are not done or cancelled become postponed.
4 Confirmed sale order S00201 with the Postpone button
  1. A postponed delivery shows the Postponed ribbon and waits, even if the products are reserved.
5 Postponed delivery of order S00202, with the Postponed ribbon and the Postponed field ticked
  1. A postponed delivery cannot be validated: Validate stops with an error.
6 Error dialog: the transfer is postponed
  1. The Postponed delivery filter of the sale orders lists the orders waiting for release; on the transfers, the same role has the Postponed filter.
7 Sales Orders list with the Postponed delivery filter: order S00202
  1. Once the payment is received, click Release on the order. The Postpone Delivery and Release Delivery actions do the same on several orders at once.
8 Sale order S00202 with the Release button

Follow the availability and the parcel

  1. On the transfers in progress, Available state shows whether the products are reserved: available, partially available or unavailable.
9 Deliveries list with the availability column
  1. On a done delivery, Delivery State shows where the parcel is at the carrier, from Ready in warehouse to Delivered or Refused. The shipping integrations update it; it can also be set by hand.
10 Done delivery DC/OUT/00003 with the Delivery State In Transit

  • Apps Store banner (banner.json).

20.0.2.3.3 (2026-10-08)

  • Fixed DELIVERYSTATUS-002: when the payment was completed before the order was confirmed (the usual online payment flow, where the payment confirms the quotation), the transfers created at confirmation were postponed and stayed blocked, because the payment had already been processed. The delivery is now postponed at confirmation only while the last payment is not done, for the provider option and for the team wire-transfer option.

20.0.2.3.2 (2026-09-29)

  • Romanian translations completed and corrected: Refused was not translated (the state showed in English on the delivery), Ready in warehouse lacked its diacritics, and the postponed-delivery labels were missing. On upgrade the corrected ones replace the old.

19.0.2.3.1

20.0.2.3.1

  • Own module icon, instead of the generic gears it had.

19.0.2.3.0

  • Orders with a postponed delivery can now be found: a "Postponed delivery" filter in the sale order search and "Postponed" in the transfer search. Without them, a flow that requires a manual release was practically impossible to operate — the "Release" button only appears on the open order, and the waiting orders were not visible anywhere.
  • sale.order.postponed_delivery becomes searchable (a search method that is translated into picking_ids.postponed, a stored field). Being computed and not stored, it could not be used in any domain — neither in predefined filters, nor in custom filters created by the user from the interface.
  • The "Postpone Delivery" / "Release Delivery" server actions now have binding_model_id, so they appear in the Actions menu and allow mass operation from the order list, not only one by one.
  • New test for the search on postponed_delivery (including the negation).

19.0.2.2.0 (2026-08-04)

  • Validating a transfer no longer marks it as delivered when no carrier is found. That test read an unfinished transfer as a finished delivery: with a shipping integration at rate level the operator validates first and sends to the shipper afterwards, so carrier_id is empty on both the picking and the order for as long as the shipping wizard takes. Parcels reached delivered within the same second as the validation, which pushed the order to its delivered phase — and, with a carrier already on the order, mailed the customer — while the label was still being printed. Verified on Sanodor production, where three transfers went draft -> delivered -> pre_advice in about a minute, and the orders stayed on the delivered phase (set_phase never demotes). A wrong delivered is also excluded from the delivery status cron, so nothing brought those back on its own.
  • The two cases — a transfer that will never have a carrier and one that does not have it yet — are indistinguishable at validation time, on the same operation type and with the same field values; what separates them is whether an AWB shows up afterwards. The carrier-less transfers are therefore marked delivered by the delivery status cron in deltatech_delivery (18.0.5.7.0) once a grace period has passed without one, instead of at validation time. Installations without deltatech_delivery keep the transfers on draft. Ported from 18.0.

19.0.2.1.4 (2026-06-11)

  • Fix: releasing a postponed delivery when the payment is confirmed never worked — _set_done accessed the non-existent field payment.transaction.sale_order_id (the correct field is sale_order_ids), raising AttributeError for providers with Postponed Delivery enabled. All linked postponed orders are now released, and a failure to release no longer blocks the payment processing (logged instead).
  • Added @api.depends("picking_ids.postponed") on _compute_postponed_delivery so the value is recomputed within the same transaction after postpone/release.
  • Added tests for the postpone/release flow and the payment-driven release.
