1. Go to **Accounting > Reporting > Invoices** (the Invoice Analysis report). Use the new **Product** and **Region** group-by filters (available in the Filters/Group By menu) to break down invoiced amounts by product or by the customer's/vendor's county (state).
2. On a product form (Sales/Purchase tab), open the **History** page to see a yearly breakdown of quantities invoiced in (purchases) and out (sales) for that product.
3. Click **Refresh** on the History page to recompute the history for that product from posted invoices/bills.
4. A daily scheduled action, **Update Product Invoice History by Year** (Settings > Technical > Automation > Scheduled Actions), keeps this history up to date for all products automatically; adjust its interval or run it manually if needed.
