Every night the module computes, for each customer and company, where
the customer stands in the sales portfolio:

- **buying rhythm**: the average number of days between purchases,
  measured on the customer's own history, and how many days he has been
  silent;
- **rhythm segment**: new, active, at risk, dormant or lost, judged
  against the customer's own rhythm and not against a company average. A
  weekly buyer silent for three weeks is at risk; a quarterly buyer
  silent for two months is still active;
- **sales**: lifetime, last 12 months, current period against the
  previous one, average purchase, growth;
- **receivables**: open balance, overdue amount, number of overdue
  invoices and the age of the oldest one, read only from customer
  invoices;
- **portfolio classification**: top customer, growing, high potential,
  small orders (upsell), stable, declining, inactive (three levels) or
  payment risk, plus a risk score and a potential score.

The data comes from posted customer invoices (credit notes deducted) or
from confirmed sales orders, untaxed and in company currency. Sales of
the contacts roll up to their company. No field is added on the partner:
the results live in their own table, one row per customer and company,
and are opened from the partner form (*Action > Customer Segment*) or
from *Sales > Customer Portfolio > Customer Segments*.

Salespeople with the *Own customers* role see only the customers they
are the salesperson of; the *All customers* role sees every customer and
edits the thresholds.

The engine is the base of the customer analysis dashboard and of the
weekly sales missions.

Configuration
=============

1. Grant the role in *Settings > Users > Customer Portfolio*: *Own
   customers* for the salespeople, *All customers* for the sales
   director.
2. Open *Sales > Customer Portfolio > Settings* and choose:

   - the source: posted invoices (default) or confirmed orders;
   - whether individuals are skipped (B2B only);
   - the comparison period (months) used for growth and decline;
   - the thresholds, in company currency. The defaults come from a
     Romanian distributor and are meant to be adjusted to your own order
     sizes.

3. Press *Recompute now*, or wait for the nightly scheduled action
   *Customer Segment: nightly recompute*.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Sales > Customer Portfolio > Customer Segments*.
2. Use the filters *Declining*, *Inactive*, *Payment risk*, *At risk
   (own rhythm)* or *Bought only once* to build the call list of the
   day, and *My customers* to keep only your own portfolio.
3. Switch to the pivot view to see the sales of the last 12 months by
   classification and rhythm segment.
4. From a customer form, *Action > Customer Segment* opens the row of
   that customer.
