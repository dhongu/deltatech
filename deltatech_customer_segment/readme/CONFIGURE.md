1. Grant the role in *Settings > Users > Customer Portfolio*: *Own customers*
   for the salespeople, *All customers* for the sales director.
2. Open *Sales > Customer Portfolio > Settings* and choose:
   - the source: posted invoices (default) or confirmed orders;
   - whether individuals are skipped (B2B only);
   - the comparison period (months) used for growth and decline;
   - the thresholds, in company currency. The defaults come from a Romanian
     distributor and are meant to be adjusted to your own order sizes.
3. Press *Recompute now*, or wait for the nightly scheduled action
   *Customer Segment: nightly recompute*.
