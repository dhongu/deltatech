1. Go to *Sales > Customer Portfolio > Customer Segments*.
2. Use the filters *Declining*, *Inactive*, *Payment risk*, *At risk (own
   rhythm)* or *Bought only once* to build the call list of the day, and
   *My customers* to keep only your own portfolio.
3. Switch to the pivot view to see the sales of the last 12 months by
   classification and rhythm segment.
4. From a customer form, *Action > Customer Segment* opens the row of that
   customer.
