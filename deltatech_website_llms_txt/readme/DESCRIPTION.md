Publishes `/llms.txt` on your Odoo website: a short Markdown summary of the site, in the
[llms.txt](https://llmstxt.org) format, that ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other AI assistants
read to understand who you are and which pages matter, instead of guessing from the HTML.

- **Generated from the website**: The site name, a summary, the contact details of the company,
  the published and indexed pages (with their meta description), the shop and its categories when
  eCommerce is installed, and the sitemap.
- **Your words first**: A summary and a free Markdown text (delivery areas, payment, returns, what
  an assistant should know before recommending you) are set per website in the settings.
- **Full control when needed**: A custom file, when filled in, is served as is instead of the
  generated one; the file can also be switched off per website.
- **One file per website**: On a multi-website database each domain serves its own file, with its
  own pages and categories.

**Data published:** only what is already public on the website (published pages, shop
categories, the company email, phone and city) and the texts you type in the settings.
