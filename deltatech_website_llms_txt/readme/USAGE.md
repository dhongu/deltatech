1. Go to *Website > Configuration > Settings*, section *Tracking & SEO*. Keep the *llms.txt*
   setting checked and click *Edit llms.txt*.

   ![Website settings, Tracking and SEO section: the llms.txt setting checked, with the Edit llms.txt and View llms.txt links highlighted](static/description/en/01_setare_llms_txt.png)

2. In the *llms.txt* tab of the website, write the *llms.txt Summary* (who you are and what you
   sell) and, optionally, *llms.txt Details* in Markdown (delivery, payment, returns). Save, then
   click *View llms.txt*.

   ![The llms.txt tab of the Grădina Demo website: Publish llms.txt checked, the llms.txt Summary of the shop, llms.txt Details with a Delivery section in Markdown, an empty Custom llms.txt and the View llms.txt link](static/description/en/02_tab_llms_txt.png)

3. The file is served at `https://<your-domain>/llms.txt`: the website name and the summary,
   the company contact, your details, the published and indexed pages with their meta
   description, the shop categories (with eCommerce) and the sitemap. Pages without a
   description in their SEO settings use the one set in their template, as theme pages do.

   ![The /llms.txt file of the Grădina Demo website at www.gradina-demo.ro: title, summary after the > sign, the Contact line with email, phone, city and country, the Delivery section from the details, then Pages with Home, Contact Us and Delivery and returns, each with its description, and Optional with the Sitemap](static/description/en/03_fisier_llms_txt.png)
