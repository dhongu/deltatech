Overview

Lets a company keep the Sales Price of its products in a currency other than its accounting currency, for example prices in EUR in a company that keeps its books in RON. Sales orders and pricelists convert the price to their own currency at the rate of the day.

Price List Currency on the company. Adds the Price List Currency field on the company form, under Currency. The currency of the product Sales Price becomes this currency for all the products of the company. Products shared between companies use the price list currency of the main company.

Conversion in pricelists and sales. When a pricelist or a sales order is in another currency, the sales price is converted from the price list currency with the standard Odoo currency rates. The Cost of products stays in the company currency.

Configuration

Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Companies, open the company and set Price List Currency. To keep the sales prices in the company currency, set it to the same currency as Currency.

Limitations

  • The setting applies to the whole company: all products use the same price list currency; it cannot be chosen per product.
  • On installation every company gets EUR as Price List Currency. In a company whose currency is not EUR, the existing sales prices are then read as EUR amounts until the field is changed.

Usage

  1. Open the company (Settings > Users & Companies > Companies). Under Currency (the accounting currency), choose the Price List Currency: the currency in which the sales prices of all the products of the company are kept. Save.

  1. Check that the price list currency has a rate (Invoicing > Configuration > Accounting > Currencies, open EUR, tab Rates). Odoo uses the last rate up to the date of the document.

  1. Open the product (Sales > Products > Products) and enter the Sales Price in the price list currency: the currency symbol (€) is shown next to it. The Cost stays in the company currency.

  1. Create a quotation (Sales > Orders > Quotations) with a pricelist in the company currency, or with no pricelist, and add the product: the Unit Price is the sales price converted at the rate of the quotation/order date, here 120.00 € × 5 = 600.00 lei.

A pricelist in the same currency as the sales price (EUR) does not convert it. On a pricelist in another currency, Discount and Formula rules start from the converted price; Fixed Price amounts are already in the pricelist currency. A quotation keeps the price computed when the line was added, at the rate of its quotation/order date: it does not follow a later rate by itself. Customer invoices created without a sales order convert the sales price in the same way.
