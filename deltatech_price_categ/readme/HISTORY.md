## 19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-01)

- Category prices add the price-included taxes through the Odoo tax engine: fixed and grouped taxes are no longer treated as percentages, and taxes not included in the price are no longer added (PRICE-001).
- Category prices are recomputed when an existing tax changes its amount, type or price inclusion (PRICE-002).
